**Style lock:** Cloud-ridge Twilight I Ching manuscript cards. Identical cloud-thunder border in verdigris teal #55806D and antique gold #BDA476, parchment cream field #F0E4D0, forest-ink edge #0D1411. Each card MUST show three layers: (1) HEXAGRAM FIGURE — six stacked horizontal lines (solid yang / broken yin) in upper third; (2) SCENE — flat emblematic illustration in center; (3) NAMEPLATE — bottom band with the Chinese hexagram name character(s) rendered from the Unicode code points given in each file description, plus small English subtitle in gold. Flat mineral paint, thick contours, readable at small size. No photorealism, no 3D, no anime, no neon.
