# Music source credits

Indian Ocean and Latin retain six regional loops each. East Asia, West Asia
and Steppe use the same neutral opening theme in gameplay; their individual
loops and older stems were removed. Source files, SHA-256 hashes, and download
pages for the retained recordings and archived experiments are recorded in
`tools/audio/samples/songbook/SOURCE.json`,
`tools/audio/samples/china/SOURCE.json`, the sitar and guqin `SOURCE.json`
files, and the performance `PERFORMANCES.json` files.

The opening theme uses recorded recorder, plucked psaltery, and frame drum
samples from [VCSL](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), published under CC0.

| Culture | Recorded sources and attribution | License |
|---|---|---|
| East Asia, West Asia, Steppe | VCSL recorder, plucked psaltery and frame drum in the shared opening theme | CC0. Previous guzheng, oud and tovshuur recordings are archived sources only; none are in current gameplay music. |
| Indian Ocean | [Recorded sitar sample set](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/Discord-SFZ-GM-Bank/tree/master/Discord%20GM/Melodic/105-Sitar) contributed by Dr. Narayan Bhagawan Raikar; [tabla strokes](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4327350) by Subodh Deolekar through [tabla_app](https://github.com/SimardeepSG/tabla_app/blob/main/ATTRIBUTIONS.md) | Sitar CC0; tabla CC BY 4.0. Both current source sets are lossless. |
| Latin | [VCSL](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) Baroque alto recorder, plucked psaltery and frame drum | CC0. These are original game melodies, not the previous Mainerio excerpts. |

FateQuest extracts instrument attacks, high-pass filters, balances, and fades
the retained regional sources. No source artist endorses FateQuest. The old
[pipa source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pipa_-_sound.ogg) is
archived under CC BY-SA 4.0 and absent from the current outputs.

Earlier guqin, jaw-harp and MP3 tanpura samples remain in the source archive
but are absent from the current regional runtime tracks.
