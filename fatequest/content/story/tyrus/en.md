## city.tyrus.name

Tyre

## ev.tyrus.entry.choice.pass

Thank Salma and continue along the coast

## ev.tyrus.entry.choice.rest

Stay by Salma's shelter for a day

## ev.tyrus.entry.body

The road reaches the sea at Tyre. Beside some old stonework, Salma has spread a cloth over a low shelter. She is mending her younger brother's sandal with a thick needle. He holds the other sandal against his foot and announces that this pair will last until he is grown.

“You said that about the last pair,” Salma tells him. “It lasted six weeks.” He says the stones near the shore are unusually sharp. She gives him the needle to hold while she tests the new stitching.

Salma offers you water and asks where you came from. Her brother supplies three guesses before you can answer. She knows which stretch of the coastal road stays clear after rain, and draws it with her finger in the dust. When you take out your map, she asks you to mark the shelter too. “Travellers bring their sandals to me. My brother asks each one where they came from, then draws their roads in the dust.”

## ev.tyrus.entry.title

Arriving at Tyre

## ev.ev_tyrus_entry.choice_1_result

You drink, thank Salma, and continue along the coast. Her brother runs after you to point out the turn past the old stones. You mark Tyre and the shore road on your map.

## ev.ev_tyrus_entry.choice_2_result

You stay at the shelter for the day. Salma lets you rest under the cloth while she finishes the sandal. By evening her brother has walked the whole shore in it and returned to show that the seam has held. Salma shares warm food with you, and you sleep there before leaving the next morning. Your strength returns, and you mark Tyre on your map.

## ev.gap.tyrus.scene.title

The Shell in the Cloth Worker's Basket

## ev.gap.tyrus.scene.body

At a work space near the shore, Rana rinses a piece of dyed wool. Her daughter sets smooth pebbles on its corners so the wind will not lift it. A small sea shell lies in the basket of pebbles. The daughter says her grandfather picked it out of an old wall and told her that people here once made purple dye from such creatures.

“He told me the cloth was worth a house,” she says. Rana laughs. “He told you a good story. This piece is for your aunt, and she would rather have it before the cold weather.” The girl touches the wet edge, then withdraws her hand when Rana gives her a look.

Rana has been walking the shore to find people willing to carry finished cloth inland. She asks you to help her remember where you met the last caravan. She can also describe the coast road toward Acre; she has relatives who have travelled that way.

## ev.gap.tyrus.scene.choice.talk

Tell Rana where you met the caravan

## ev.gap.tyrus.scene.choice.route

Ask Rana about the road toward Acre

## ev.gap.tyrus.scene.result.talk

You name the place and show Rana the mark in your notebook. She calls her daughter over so both of them can remember it. The girl adds a shell beside your mark; Rana promises to take the finished cloth to the next caravan herself. You add this part of Tyre to your map, including the place where travellers gather.

## ev.gap.tyrus.scene.result.route

Rana traces the coast southward in the damp sand. She has family who have travelled toward Acre and tells you where they stopped for water. Her daughter adds a shell to the starting point so you cannot mistake Tyre for the next bend. You record Tyre, Acre, and the route between them on your map.

## ev.tyrus.fishrack.title

The Fish Rack by the Shore

## ev.tyrus.fishrack.body

Farida is tying a new crosspiece to a drying rack. Her daughter Lina holds it up from below. A strip of salted fish hangs from the old side of the frame, just out of the reach of a cat that has been following the work.

Lina says her uncle taught her the knot before he went inland to work as a carpenter. She has tied it twice, and it still slips. “He used to pull the rope with his teeth,” she says. Farida makes her take it out of her mouth. “He also lost a tooth. Use both hands.”

Farida sells small packets of fish to people on the road. The rack needs another crosspiece before she can hang today's catch. She asks whether you would like food for the journey or have time to stay and work with them. The cat sits beneath the unfinished side, waiting for an answer of its own.

## ev.tyrus.fishrack.choice.buy

Buy Farida’s salted fish for 0.8 silver

## ev.tyrus.fishrack.choice.stay

Spend the day helping them finish the rack

## ev.tyrus.fishrack.result.buy

You pay Farida for a packet. Lina wraps it in a clean scrap of cloth and ties the knot her uncle taught her; this time it holds. On the road, you eat the fish with bread and feel stronger. Farida counts the coins into the small pot she keeps for new rope. The cat receives one tiny piece that fell from the rack.

## ev.tyrus.fishrack.result.stay

You hold the frame while Farida fastens the crosspiece. Lina tries the knot again with both hands. It stays tight, and she hangs the first fish on the new side. By evening the rack is full. Farida gives you supper and a place beneath the shelter for the night; you leave the next morning. She tells the nearby families who helped build it, and they greet you when you pass.
