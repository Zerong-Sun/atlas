## city.tyrus.name

Tyrus

## ev.tyrus.entry.body

Sūr — Tyre — lies thirty miles down the coast from Acre. The city is
proverbial for impregnability: the sea encompasses it on three sides; a mole
closes the fourth. It has but two gates — landward, through three or four
posterns in outer walls; seaward, flanked by towers, with a great chain that
when raised bars the harbour. Ships enter below the walls and anchor within.
Its streets are cleaner than Acre's; Muslim strangers find easier lodging here.
You must know that the Franks hold this place as a refuge in extremity, and that
its harbour is counted among the wonders of the maritime cities.
