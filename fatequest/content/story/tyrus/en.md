## ev.tyrus.entry.body

Tyre — the ancient Phoenician island-city, now joined to the mainland only by a sandy
causeway that Alexander built when he besieged it. The glassblowers of Tyre work the
white sands of the beach into vessels of astonishing clarity. The Venetian consul keeps
a counting-house by the water; silk and sugar from the coastal plain are the principal
exports to Europe.
