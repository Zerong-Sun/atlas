## city.caiju.name

Caiju

## ev.caiju.entry.body

Caiju is a small city towards the south-east. The people are subject to the Great Kaan
and have paper-money. It stands upon the river before mentioned. At this place are
collected great quantities of corn and rice to be transported to the great city of
Cambaluc for the use of the Kaan’s Court; for the grain for the Court all comes from
this part of the country.

## ev.caiju.a.title

Caiju: The Granary Canal

## ev.caiju.a.body

At the small city where the great canal is dug between stream and stream, the granary clerks of Caiju keep the corn and rice that go up to the Kaan's court at Cambaluc. A clerk who stamps the grain boats says the whole water-communication from this city to the great city passes through his books — and that a traveller who rides on without hearing the canal's seasons pays in days what another pays in coin.

## ev.caiju.a.choice.1

Buy rhubarb at the granary road price

## ev.caiju.a.choice.2

Ask the clerk the water road toward Nanghin

## ev.caiju.a.choice.3

Count a day of grain boats with the clerks

## ev.ev_caiju_a_followup.title

Caiju: The Clerk's Measure of the Canal

## ev.ev_caiju_a_followup.body

The clerk draws the canal on the ground with his seal: this reach deep, that reach shoal, the locks where the boats wait, the days between Caiju and the great city. He says the grain of this part of the country feeds the Kaan's court, and that a traveller who knows the water-communication may ride the canal as another rides a road.

## ev.ev_caiju_a_followup.choice_1

Buy tea sealed for the canal passage

## ev.ev_caiju_a_followup.choice_1_result

He sells you tea at a fair price. The leaves are sealed for the passage north or south.

## ev.ev_caiju_a_followup.choice_2

Ask him to name the reaches toward Sinju

## ev.ev_caiju_a_followup.choice_2_result

Sinju and Tiju are marked on your map with the reaches between. The canal is no longer a single line.

## ev.ev_caiju_a_followup.choice_3

Wait a day and watch the grain fleet make up

## ev.ev_caiju_a_followup.choice_3_result

You wait a day while the grain fleet makes up. The convoy's measure is learned, and in Caiju your name now carries a little more weight.
