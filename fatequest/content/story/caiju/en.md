## city.caiju.name

Caiju

## ev.caiju.entry.body

Caiju is a small city towards the south-east. The people are subject to the Great Kaan
and have paper-money. It stands upon the river before mentioned. At this place are
collected great quantities of corn and rice to be transported to the great city of
Cambaluc for the use of the Kaan’s Court; for the grain for the Court all comes from
this part of the country.

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.title

Bamboo Baskets by the Bridge

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.body

A vegetable seller sets down two bamboo baskets. Leaves go in one and roots in the other; stacked together, the leaves would wilt. If you are going to Chinghianfu, she says, separate them for the jolting road. She hands you thin cord and tells you to tie leafy greens loosely so air can pass through.

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.choice.route

Ask the way to Chinghianfu

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.choice.route_result

“Go south from here along the official road to Chinghianfu.”

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.choice.scene

Help sort the vegetables

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.choice.scene_result

You separate the wet leaves from the dry roots. “Leaves on top,” she says. “At market this saves much of what would otherwise be thrown away.”
