## city.caiju.name

Caiju

## ev.caiju.entry.body

At Caiju’s landing, a clerk has counted one grain sack twice. The boat owner catches it when his daughter calls the marks from the deck. ‘I put that bag by the wrong post,’ she says. Her father asks her to bring the sack back where everyone can see its mark. The clerk crosses out the extra line. ‘Read the last three marks once more,’ he tells her. She does, and asks, ‘Is that the count you have?’ He nods and writes the new total. A carter beside you wants to know when the embankment road will be clear; porters have spread sacks across one end of it. The clerk says they will finish the count before the next cart arrives. You ask how to go overland toward Chinghianfu. ‘Follow the raised road past the last storehouse,’ the owner says. ‘The carters know the next stopping place.’ His daughter has brought a spare sheet to copy the corrected list. ‘Let me do this one myself,’ she tells the clerk. He gives her the board and promises to check it after she has finished. If you stay tomorrow, she can show you the warehouses and the high road along the water.

## ev.caiju.entry.choice.look

Spend a day learning the landing and stores

## ev.caiju.entry.choice.pass

Continue through the town today

## ev.caiju.entry.title

The Grain Boat’s Second Count

## ev.ev_caiju_entry.choice_1_result

The daughter reads the corrected count back to the clerk. The porters lift the last sacks off the embankment, giving the waiting cart room to pass. The boat owner points out the street beyond the landing, and you continue through Caiju.

## ev.ev_caiju_entry.choice_2_result

You stay another day. The daughter walks you past the warehouses and along the high road above the water, then introduces you to a boatman who knows the onward channel. She keeps the corrected cargo sheet dry beneath her arm.

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.title

The Vegetable Seller’s Two Baskets

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.body

A vegetable seller by the bridge has put tender leaves and hard roots into separate baskets. Her younger brother has tied the leaf basket too tightly, and the greens at the bottom are already bending. ‘They will wilt before the buyer sees them,’ she says. He loosens the cord, but now the basket tips when he lifts it. You help him set the bundle upright while she moves the heavy roots to the other side of the carrying pole. A traveller asks if there are vegetables that will last on his cart journey toward Chinghianfu. The seller offers him the dry roots first; the leaves will go to a family cooking nearby. The boy asks why they cannot send everything on the cart. ‘Because their supper is today,’ she says, pointing to the family’s fire. The traveller knows the road onward. ‘After the bridge, where does the cart turn?’ you ask. ‘Past the grain store,’ he says, waiting while the boy reties the lighter basket. The seller asks you to listen to his directions, or help her finish sorting the produce before he goes.

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.choice.route

Ask the traveller about Chinghianfu

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.choice.route_result

The traveller marks the route to Chinghianfu on your board, including the grain store where the cart road turns. The seller hands him a small packet of roots for the trip. Her brother can now carry both baskets without crushing the leaves.

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.choice.scene

Help sort the two baskets

## ev.ev_gap_caiju.choice.scene_result

You help the seller shift the heavy roots and tie the leaf basket loosely. Her brother walks a short stretch of the bridge and turns back without spilling anything. The family cooking nearby takes the greens while they are still fresh.

## ev.caiju.umbrella.title

A Rib for Grandfather’s Umbrella

## ev.caiju.umbrella.body

At a stall above the canal, an umbrella maker is replacing a broken bamboo rib. Her grandfather has already come to collect the umbrella, though the glue is still wet. ‘I can carry it closed,’ he says. She asks him how he will open it if the rain begins before he reaches home. He says he can wait under the bridge. You notice a dry seat beneath the stall roof and offer it to him. Her apprentice is cutting a fresh rib but has left it too long. The grandfather shows him where the old rib used to meet the joint. ‘Measure against the sound one,’ he says, and the apprentice tries again. A shower passes over the canal, making it easy to see why the repair matters. The maker asks you to hold the frame while she binds it, or stay until tomorrow when the glue has set and the family can share a meal before you leave. The grandfather does not mind the delay; he wants to see his old umbrella open smoothly once more.

## ev.caiju.umbrella.choice.sew

Hold the frame while they repair it

## ev.caiju.umbrella.result.sew

You hold the umbrella open as the maker binds the new rib. The apprentice checks each joint against the sound side. The grandfather tests the handle beneath the roof and thanks all three of you before taking the covered umbrella home.

## ev.caiju.umbrella.choice.stay

Wait a day for the repair and a meal

## ev.caiju.umbrella.result.stay

You stay another day while the glue sets. The maker opens the umbrella for her grandfather in the morning; he walks a short stretch in the light rain to test it. The family invites you to eat before you leave, and you rest through the afternoon.
