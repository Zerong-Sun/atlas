## div.xiangmian.result.00

A clear brow: an open road and an open heart; proceed.

## div.xiangmian.result.01

A furrowed brow: worry clouds the way; resolve the doubt first.

## div.xiangmian.result.02

Bright eyes: a sharp glance finds the best goods at the fair.

## div.xiangmian.result.03

Clouded eyes: a deception waits; read the seal twice.

## div.xiangmian.result.04

A strong nose: fortune rises with the nose of a prince; negotiate well.

## div.xiangmian.result.05

A wide mouth: generous speech earns a discount at the gate.

## div.xiangmian.result.06

A thin lip: a tight bargain; give nothing, ask double.

## div.xiangmian.result.07

A long chin: endurance; the road is long but you will finish.

## div.xiangmian.result.08

A short chin: turn back soon; the plan shortens.

## div.xiangmian.result.09

Cheeks that catch the light: the town will remember your face kindly.

## div.xiangmian.result.10

Cheeks in shadow: keep your face from the guard's lamp.

## div.xiangmian.result.11

A scar across the brow: an old wound speaks; heed its lesson.

## div.xiangmian.result.12

A mole at the corner of the eye: a small treasure hides near the road.

## div.xiangmian.result.13

A mole on the cheek: a tear and a smile; a mixed fortune.

## div.xiangmian.result.14

A mole on the chin: a late reward for a long patience.

## div.xiangmian.result.15

A mole on the nose: gossip follows you; ignore it.

## div.xiangmian.result.16

Lines at the eyes: laughter lines; seek the tavern's cheer.

## div.xiangmian.result.17

Lines at the mouth: years of silence; speak now.

## div.xiangmian.result.18

A high forehead: learning; a scholar at the next city will help.

## div.xiangmian.result.19

A low forehead: simplicity; an honest deal, but small.

## div.xiangmian.result.20

Brows that meet: a tangled road; cut the knot early.

## div.xiangmian.result.21

Brows apart: a wide choice; many paths open.

## div.xiangmian.result.22

The left eye twitches: a woman brings news.

## div.xiangmian.result.23

The right eye twitches: a man brings a quarrel.

## div.xiangmian.result.24

A reddened nose: wine and warmth; beware the open purse.

## div.xiangmian.result.25

A pale face: illness; rest a day before the pass.

## div.xiangmian.result.26

A flushed face: anger; cool it before the bargaining.

## div.xiangmian.result.27

A smiling face: the road smiles back; go with cheer.

## div.xiangmian.result.28

A downcast face: a heavy heart; unburden it to a friend.

## div.xiangmian.result.29

A serene face: peace; the road holds no harm for you.
