## city.cabul.name

Cabul

## ev.cabul.entry.body

At a Kabul lodging yard, a cobbler is measuring a mule driver’s worn boots. His daughter holds the new felt against the old sole and says they need another strip of leather. The driver wants the boots before his caravan leaves, but the cobbler says the stitching will pull free if he rushes it. ‘Your feet will be the ones inside,’ he reminds him.

You ask where the caravan is going. The driver names the road toward Taican and the northern mountain approaches. His daughter points out that travellers also reach Kabul from the south and east; she has repaired boots from more than one direction. Her father sets the felt by the warm brazier and asks her to fetch the stronger thread.

The driver tries to stand on the old sole and feels its edge lift. ‘Fine, finish it properly,’ he says. The cobbler shows you a place to sleep beside the yard. His daughter returns with thread. Her father finishes the last seam, and the driver tries the boots on before offering to share his supper.

## ev.cabul.site_gap.title

The Felt Boot at the Pass

## ev.cabul.site_gap.body

At the cobbler’s stall, the mule driver has come back wearing the repaired boots. He says one foot feels tighter than the other. The cobbler’s daughter asks him to loosen the upper strap and walk across the yard again. He does, and the left sole no longer lifts. ‘That was the part I worried about,’ she says.

You ask how they make a felt boot warm enough for the mountain road. Her father shows you washed wool, dampens a small layer, and rolls it between his palms. He points to the leather beneath the sole, where stones would wear through the felt. The driver wants to know whether this pair will last until Taican. The cobbler says it depends on the path and asks which pass the caravan plans to use.

The driver draws a line in the dust, then sees the cobbler’s daughter shake her head. She knows a better branch where the mule has firm footing. Her father hands her the stick to redraw it for you both.

## ev.cabul.site_gap.choice.route

## ev.ev_cabul_site_gap.choice_1_result

She draws the branch she trusts and names its water stop. You mark the route toward Taican on your map while the driver checks the straps on his boots again.

## ev.cabul.site_gap.choice.interact

## ev.ev_cabul_site_gap.choice_2_result

You watch him roll damp wool and stitch it above the leather sole. His daughter tests the driver’s fit once more; you record the craft and understand why each layer has its place.


## ev.cabul.bellstrap.title

The Mule’s New Bell Strap

## ev.cabul.bellstrap.body

In a caravan yard, a leather worker is fitting a new strap to a mule’s bell. Her younger brother thinks the old strap can last one more journey. She holds it against the light and shows him a thin split near the buckle. ‘If it breaks on the pass, we will be searching for this bell in the snow,’ she says.

The mule driver from the cobbler’s yard arrives with his repaired boots and asks whether his animal is ready. The leather worker tells him to walk the mule while she listens. The bell rings once, then falls silent because the new strap twists against its mouth. You ask whether a shorter loop would help. Her brother fetches a knife and waits for her to say where to cut.

She can pay you 0.8 silver to stay the day and help test the caravan’s bells, or show you the quick check she uses before each mountain stage. The mule nudges the driver’s sleeve while everyone listens for the next ring.

## ev.cabul.bellstrap.choice.test

Stay a day and test the caravan bells

## ev.cabul.bellstrap.result.test

You help test each bell and the worker pays you 0.8 silver. The brother trims the new strap where she marked it; at last the mule’s bell rings clearly across the yard.
