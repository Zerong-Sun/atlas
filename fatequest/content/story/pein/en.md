## ev.pein.entry.body

Pein is a province five days in length, lying between east and north-east. The people
are worshippers of Mahommet, and subjects of the Great Kaan. There are a good number of
towns and villages, but the most noble is PEIN, the capital of the kingdom. There are
rivers in this country, in which quantities of Jasper and Chalcedony are found. The
people have plenty of all products, including cotton. They live by manufactures and
trade. But they have a custom that I must relate.
