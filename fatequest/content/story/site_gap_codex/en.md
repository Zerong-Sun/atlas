## codex.cx-axuma.name

Water at Axuma's Highland Stele

## codex.cx-axuma.body

A water carrier near the stele fills southbound travellers' skins from a mountain spring. He warns that water is scarce on the road towards Dongola. He also keeps dust out of his jars with clean cloth beneath their stoppers.

## codex.cx-cabul.name

Felt Boots at Cabul Pass

## codex.cx-cabul.body

A shoemaker at the pass layers wet wool, then rolls and rubs it into dense felt. Leather beneath the soles protects his boots on stony paths. He advises setting out before noon rather than crossing the windy ridge after dark.

## codex.cx-dongola.name

The Dongola Ferry Rope

## codex.cx-dongola.body

At the ferry, the boatman uses reed rope that yields as the river rises. He inspects it for wear before crossing and asks passengers to put their cargo in the centre of the boat. He checks the water marks on his pole before setting off.

## codex.cx-mecha.name

Water Jars by the Mecha Spring

## codex.cx-mecha.body

A potter shows you clay jars at the spring. He compares a thick jar, which bears knocks but weighs more, with a lighter, thinner jar that needs care on a cart. He keeps both in shade and warns against putting them directly on a fire.

## codex.cx-medina.name

The Shared Water Jar at Medina

## codex.cx-medina.body

An innkeeper keeps a covered water jar by the door for travellers. He asks each person to rinse the ladle and turn it over after use. Before setting out towards Petra, he says, check that your own waterskins are full.

## codex.cx-merva.name

Paper Beneath the Mulberry Tree at Merva

## codex.cx-merva.body

A copyist dries thin sheets in the shade, holding their corners down with wooden strips. Folding a damp sheet sticks its fibres together, and stacking it before the ink sets transfers writing to the next page. He wraps books against damp on the road.

## codex.cx-sachiu.name

Horse Feed at the Sachiu Station

## codex.cx-sachiu.body

A station hand mixes dry grass with beans for horses making a long journey. He picks out thorns that could hurt their mouths, lets wet feed dry before bagging it, and asks travellers to check the next stop's water and fodder before departure.

## codex.cx-samara.name

Monsoon Markers at Samara

## codex.cx-samara.body

A shipwright hangs coloured cloth on a rope to mark today's wind. He still watches cloud and wave before sailing. He dries wet hemp rope and sails between voyages, because saltwater left in them weakens their fibres and seams.
