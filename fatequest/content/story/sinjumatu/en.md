## city.sinjumatu.name

Sinjumatu

## ev.sinjumatu.entry.body

Beside the river at Sinjumatu, lamp seller Han is testing a clay oil lamp. Her grandson watches the flame lean in the wind and says the boatmen will never keep it lit. She puts a small hood around it and asks him to try again. A boatman passing with rope says the hood helps, but the lamp must stand flat when the boat rocks. Han sets it in a shallow dish and lets him shake the table.

You ask how to enter the town from the river road. Han points to the street behind her stall and says carts take that way while crews use the landing. Her grandson wants to know why the same town has two ways in. Han says people carry different things. The boatman lifts his rope, and the child says he sees the point.

Han invites you to spend a day among the lamps and boats if you want to know more. The boatman brings the tested lamp back, pleased that it stayed upright in the dish.

The Lamp Seller by the River

## ev.ev_sinjumatu_entry.choice_1_result

## ev.ev_gap_sinjumatu.title

The Groom’s Bridle Check

## ev.ev_gap_sinjumatu.body

At the posting yard, groom Qiu is checking the leather bridle of a horse that came in with a cart. His daughter runs a finger over a pale seam and asks whether it will rub the horse’s mouth. ‘It might,’ he says. He loosens the buckle and shows her how the worn stitch has lifted. The driver asks if he can leave before midday. Qiu says the horse needs water and a short rest while the seam is sewn.

You ask how the road goes toward Coigangiu. The driver knows the land route south and says he can draw it after the horse has drunk. Qiu’s daughter fetches a clean cup for you, then asks the driver which post has the best water for animals. He names one and tells her the trough has been repaired.

Qiu threads the needle. His daughter holds the bridle steady; the horse lowers its head toward the water, waiting for them to finish.

‘Did I leave enough room at the buckle?’ the daughter asks. Qiu slides one finger under it and lets her feel the space herself. The driver watches the horse relax.

## ev.ev_gap_sinjumatu.choice.route

Ask the driver about the land road to Coigangiu

## ev.ev_gap_sinjumatu.choice.route_result

After the horse drinks, the driver marks the land road toward Coigangiu and the repaired trough along it. Qiu’s daughter checks the first turn against his cart book. You record the route.

## ev.ev_gap_sinjumatu.choice.scene

Help Qiu’s family mend the bridle seam

## ev.ev_gap_sinjumatu.choice.scene_result

You hold the leather while Qiu sews and his daughter tests the buckle. The horse takes a short walk without pulling away from the bit. The driver thanks you before loading the cart again.

## ev.sinjumatu.lampdish.title

The Boatman’s Lamp Shelf

## ev.sinjumatu.lampdish.body

The boatman from Han’s stall has returned with a wooden shelf from his boat. It slopes slightly to one side, so even a hooded lamp would slide when the river turns rough. Han’s grandson says they could wedge a piece of cloth under the dish. The boatman asks what happens when the cloth gets wet. The boy looks at the shelf again and suggests a wooden strip instead.

You ask who made the shelf. The boatman says his older sister built it when they first worked together on the river. She is away carrying cargo today and wants the lamp to be safe before she returns at dusk. Han has a narrow offcut from an old stall board, and the boy measures it against the sloping edge. He wants to trim it himself, but Han tells him to show the boatman his mark first. The boatman checks it and says there is enough wood to try twice if the first cut goes wrong.

‘May I mark the cut?’ the boy asks. Han nods but first makes him check the sloping shelf one more time.

## ev.sinjumatu.lampdish.choice.fit

Help the grandson fit a wooden strip

## ev.sinjumatu.lampdish.choice.day

Spend a day learning to make stable lamp shelves

## ev.sinjumatu.lampdish.result.fit

You steady the shelf while the grandson trims the strip. The boatman sets the dish and lamp on it, then tilts the board gently. Nothing slides. His sister returns to find her old shelf repaired.

## ev.sinjumatu.lampdish.result.day

You spend a day making a second lamp shelf with Han’s grandson. The boatman tests both aboard before his sister returns. At dusk she points out a bend in the river where a lamp needs an even deeper dish.
