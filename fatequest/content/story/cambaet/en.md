## city.cambaet.name

Cambaet

## ev.cambaet.entry.body

A dyer in Cambaet is hanging lengths of indigo cloth above the lane. Her apprentice holds one end too low, and a passing merchant nearly walks into it. “Lift it above his turban,” she says. The merchant ducks and asks whether his order has dried. “The dark bolts have. Your sister asked for the lighter shade, remember?” she replies. He admits that he has forgotten which of his family placed the order and pulls a creased note from his belt. She smooths it against a board and reads the mark she made beside the sister’s name. You ask why she uses two blue threads to mark the bundle. “One tells me how often it went into the vat. The other tells the porter whose doorstep to find.” The merchant laughs and helps the apprentice raise the line. Across the lane, cotton is being bundled for the next caravan. The dyer points you toward the through road, then offers to show you the stalls if you can spare a day.

## ev.cambaet.entry.title

The Blue Cloth Lane

## ev.ev_cambaet_entry.choice_1_result

The apprentice steps aside so you can pass beneath the cloth. At the far end of the lane he points out the road beyond the market. “Come back when the cloth is dry,” he says, returning to his line. You continue with the town's turns clear in mind.

## ev.ev_cambaet_entry.choice_2_result

You spend a day walking the stalls with the dyer. She introduces the cotton seller who buys her offcuts and the porter who carries finished cloth to the water. When the merchant returns, he recognizes his sister's two blue threads on the bundle. You leave with the neighbourhood's trades and paths in mind.

## ev.cambaet.site_gap.title

A Colour Returned to the Vat

## ev.cambaet.site_gap.body

At the dye yard, an apprentice has laid two lengths of cloth side by side. One is a deep blue; the other has pale streaks near its folded edge. “The customer says they were meant to match,” he tells his aunt. She asks whether he unfolded the second length before dyeing it. He looks down at the fold and answers, “I thought the colour would reach through.” She does not scold him. Instead she fills a basin and asks him to rinse both edges so they can see how much dye remains. You ask how long the cloth must stay in the vat. “That depends on the cloth,” she says, lifting the wet end for you to see. “Watch the edge when it dries. If the colour sits only on the surface, another dip won't help until we wash it.” The apprentice fetches clean water. His aunt puts a marker by the road leading toward Semenat so the customer can find them when he returns.

## ev.cambaet.site_gap.choice.route

Ask her about the Semenat road

## ev.ev_cambaet_site_gap.choice_1_result

The dyer takes you to the corner where carts leave for Semenat and points out the road marker she uses for deliveries. “Ask at the next water stop before taking a side track,” she says. You trace the turn on your map while the apprentice carries his basin back to the yard.

## ev.cambaet.site_gap.choice.interact

Help check the two cloth edges

## ev.ev_cambaet_site_gap.choice_2_result

You help rinse and spread the cloth. The streaks remain near the old fold, so the apprentice opens the whole length before his aunt returns it to the vat. She shows you how to judge the dried edge and gives him the task of checking the next batch himself.

## ev.cambaet.tidemark.title

The Missing Harbour Mark

## ev.cambaet.tidemark.body

A boatman's daughter is standing beside a row of stakes at the muddy landing. One has come loose and rolled against a cargo basket. “Father will blame the tide,” she says, “but I tied this one yesterday.” Her father comes up carrying a coil of rope and studies the knot. “You used the short end. Look how it slipped.” She asks whether they can put it back before the next boat arrives. He says yes, then notices that the stake's painted mark has worn away. Without it, the carriers cannot tell where the shallower boats should stop. The girl offers to fetch a pot of pigment from her mother's stall. “Tell her I need the red one,” he says. She gives him a look: “She has only one pot for the landing. You used the rest on the door.” He grins and hands her his rope. You can hold the stake while they reset it, or listen as he explains which waterside road the carriers take toward Tana.

## ev.cambaet.tidemark.choice.help

Help reset and mark the stake

## ev.cambaet.tidemark.result.help

You hold the stake upright while the boatman drives it into firmer ground. His daughter paints a fresh mark and makes him test her knot. It holds when the next shallow boat touches the landing. The carriers thank you for giving them a place to unload without sinking into the mud.

## ev.cambaet.tidemark.choice.route

Ask where the porters take the goods

## ev.cambaet.tidemark.result.route

The boatman points along the waterside road used by carriers travelling toward Tana. His daughter corrects him at one fork: the old bank washed out last season, and people now use the higher path. You follow her correction on the ground before moving on, better prepared for the next leg.
