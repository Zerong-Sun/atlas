## city.cambaet.name

Cambaet

## ev.cambaet.entry.body

Cambaet is a great kingdom lying further west. The people are Idolaters, and have a
language of their own, and a king of their own, and are tributary to nobody. The North
Star is here still more clearly visible; and henceforward the further you go west the
higher you see it. There is a great deal of trade in this country. It produces indigo in
great abundance; and they also make much fine buckram. There is also a quantity of
cotton which is exported hence to many quarters; and there is a great trade in hides,
which are very well dressed; with many other kinds of merchandize too tedious to
mention.

## ev.cambaet.a.title

Cambaet: The Indigo Vats of the Free Kingdom

## ev.cambaet.a.body

In the great kingdom that lies further west, tributary to nobody, the dyers of Cambaet work the indigo that grows in great abundance, and the North Star stands clear above their vats. A dyer who has served both the local trade and the foreign ships says the kingdom makes fine buckram and exports cotton to many quarters, and that hides are dressed here better than anywhere on the coast — and that merchants come with gold, silver, and copper to carry all of it away.

## ev.cambaet.a.choice.1

## ev.cambaet.a.choice.2

## ev.cambaet.a.choice.3

## ev.ev_cambaet_a_followup.title

Cambaet: The Dyer's Second Word on the Star

## ev.ev_cambaet_a_followup.body

The dyer points to the North Star and speaks of it as the kingdom's second king. He says the star is more clearly visible here than anywhere east, and that every ship that comes to this coast sets its course by it — the kingdom needs no tribute because it needs no master, only the star and the trade. He asks whether you sail by the star or by the cargo.

## ev.ev_cambaet_a_followup.choice_1

Buy cotton cloth at the dyer's export price

## ev.ev_cambaet_a_followup.choice_1_result

He sells you cotton at a fair price. The cloth is fine and ready for the sea road.

## ev.ev_cambaet_a_followup.choice_2

Ask him to mark the road toward Aden

## ev.ev_cambaet_a_followup.choice_2_result

Aden is marked on your map with the days between. The gulf road is open.

## ev.ev_cambaet_a_followup.choice_3

## ev.ev_cambaet_a_followup.choice_3_result

You wait a day at the coast. The ships' courses are learned, and in Cambaet your name now carries a little more weight.
