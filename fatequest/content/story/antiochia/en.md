## ev.antiochia.entry.body

Antioch — once the third city of Rome, now a frontier town of the Mamluk sultanate. Its
walls still stand, scarred by a century of crusades and counter-sieges. Silk from Aleppo
and wool from Anatolia change hands in its covered bazaar.
