## city.samara.name

Samara

## ev.samara.entry.body

Samara — a kingdom on the northern coast of Sumatra, with its own king and its own
language. The people eat rice and fish; the forests are full of elephants and
rhinoceros. Ships from China and India call here for fresh water and fruit. The king
claims descent from Alexander the Great; his palace is built of teak and decorated with
Chinese porcelain.

## ev.samara.site_gap.title

The Shipwright’s Monsoon Markers

## ev.samara.site_gap.body

A shipwright hangs hemp rope beside a mast, with three strips of different colours tied to it. They show sailors changes in wind direction, though clouds and waves must also be checked before sailing. Small boats furl their sails offshore. He hangs the wet rope in moving air: saltwater left in its fibres makes it brittle.

## ev.samara.site_gap.choice.route

Ask about the sea route to Mien

## ev.ev_samara_site_gap.choice_1_result

“Take this sea route east to Mien,” he says. “Ask when a ship is due to leave. If the monsoon is unfavourable, a captain may wait several days.”

## ev.samara.site_gap.choice.interact

Help furl an old sail

## ev.ev_samara_site_gap.choice_2_result

As you roll the cloth, he points to worn stitching. A repair should follow the old needle holes. Let the sail dry before folding it; saltwater left in the cloth weakens the thread.
