## ev.alexandria.welcome.entry

Ask where a traveller might find a welcome

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.title

A Stranger at the Gate

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.body

Groups pass through the gate together. You wait beside the wall, with no familiar face to greet you. A porter notices that you have let several groups go by. “Are you looking for someone?” he asks. You can tell him who you are, admit that your purse is running low, or enter the city alone.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.faith

Offer a Muslim greeting and introduce yourself as a traveller

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.tears

Sit by the wall and let the tears come — your purse is nearly empty

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.leave

Thank him and find your own way into the city

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.faith_result

He returns your greeting, then listens to your story. He knows a household that sometimes welcomes travellers and offers to introduce you. The invitation is theirs to make and yours to accept.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.tears_result

The porter waits until you can speak. You explain how little money remains. He brings water and offers to take you to a household he knows. You can accept their help without promising to stay.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.leave_result

You step into the street. The market is open, and the road list will still be there whenever you wish to leave.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.title

A Place at the Table

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.body

Your host makes room at the table. Bread and a dish of food are set before you, and conversation turns to the road. A trader mentions glass lamps, imported pearls and inexpensive beads in the market. These are things to inspect, not promises of profit.

The host can help a traveller short of money with a small purse, or simply share this meal. You may also ask about the road towards Cairo.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.aid

Accept 12 silver for the road and ask about Cairo

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.food

Accept the meal, decline the money and ask about Cairo

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.decline

Thank the household and return to the street

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.aid_result

The host presses 12 silver into your hand and names the road to Cairo. You have spent a day here. Back in the city you can browse the market, inspect your bag, or choose Set Out. This household’s purse is a single gift.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.food_result

The host accepts your refusal without pressing. You spend the day talking and learn which road leads to Cairo. Afterwards you return to the city, free to explore or leave.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.decline_result

You thank the household for making room. There is no payment and no obligation. Outside, the market and the roads remain yours to choose.
