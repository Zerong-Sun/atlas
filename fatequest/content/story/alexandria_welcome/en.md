## ev.alexandria.welcome.entry

Ask where a traveller might find a welcome

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.title

Samir Shows You a Door

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.body

By the city gate, a porter sets a crate against the wall and shakes out his aching hands. You recognise him from the quay. He says his name is Samir and recognises you too. "We meet again. Did you find where you're going?"

You ask where to find the road to Cairo. Samir thinks for a moment. "I know a house where travellers often get a meal. The host has been to Cairo. Ask him; he'll know more than I do. I'll take you after I deliver this crate."

Someone is breaking bread inside the house. Its warm smell comes through the door. Samir slows down and knocks twice. "Who is it?" the host calls. Samir steps aside so that you can answer for yourself.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.faith

"Could you tell me the way to Cairo? I'd welcome a place at the table too."

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.tears

"My purse is running low. May I ask you about the road first?"

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.leave

"Thank you both. I'd like to look around the market first."

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.faith_result

The host opens the door and asks for another bowl. "Come in. Samir, wash your hands and eat with us." Samir says he has waited all the way here to hear that, then runs to the courtyard for water.

The bread is still hot when you break it. The host asks how you plan to reach Cairo. You have not decided, so he takes an old sheet of paper and draws the gate and the waterway. "Ask about boats heading inland tomorrow morning. If none leaves soon, grain caravans also go by land. Find out which gate they use and how much water they carry."

A trader holds the paper down with his cup. On his first journey to Cairo, he says, he asked when the boat would leave but forgot to ask how many stops it made. He spent two extra days aboard. He points out the quays where his boat stopped. Samir returns from washing his hands and sits down. "Save me some bread. I have a thing or two to say about the road as well." You stay at the house that night and leave with the paper in the morning.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.tears_result

The host opens the door. "Come in and eat. We can talk about the cost of the road afterwards." Samir leaves your bag against the wall and goes to wash his hands.

After the meal, the host draws the port and the water seller's shelter outside the land gate. "You can ask about boats heading inland. If the sailing doesn't suit you, look for a grain caravan at the shelter." He sees your hand around your purse and waits until the trader goes for more water. "I have twelve silver for this part of your journey. When you get to Cairo, find yourself a meal first."

Samir returns, spots the shelter on the page, and laughs. "That water seller talks a lot. He'll tell you a dozen stories, but his directions are good." He folds the page and hands it to you. You stay at the house that night and leave with the paper and the fare in the morning.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.gate.leave_result

Samir points down the lane with his chin. "Go left, then turn right at the red awning. Those stalls stay open late." The host calls for the crate. Samir waves, lifts it, and carries it into the house.

Back on the main street, the scales at the quay have been put away and the market lamps are coming on.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.title

The Host's Table

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.body

Samir sets an empty bowl beside the table and the host brings out bread. When you sit down, a trader moves his cup to make room for your bag.

The host has travelled to Cairo. He draws the port and the water seller's shelter beyond the land gate. "Ask about boats tomorrow morning. If you travel by land, look for the grain caravans at the shelter." The trader rubs a peppercorn between his fingers. "Ask the boatman how many times he'll unload on the way. If the boat waits, you wait with it."

Seeing your purse, the host offers twelve silver for the next part of your journey. He turns the paper towards you and waits for your answer.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.aid

"I'm short of money. I'll accept your help with the fare."

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.food

"The meal is plenty. Please keep the money and tell me more about the road."

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.decline

"Thank you for the meal. I'd like to see the market and the boats."

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.aid_result

The host places twelve silver at the corner of the paper and rests a finger on the road out of the city. "Ask about boats first. If the sailing is late, find the caravan. Keep your money close; the quay is crowded." Samir folds the paper into quarters. The trader moves his cup aside so you can put it away.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.food_result

The host puts the money away and marks two places on the paper. "Ask about boats at the quay and caravans at the water seller's shelter. See what's leaving tomorrow, then choose." The trader leans over and draws a crooked little boat beside the port. Samir laughs.

## ev.alexandria.welcome.meal.decline_result

The host walks you to the door with a drying cloth in his hand. Samir is returning to the quay and goes with you as far as the end of the lane. He points right. "See the red awning? The market is just beyond it."

## ev.alexandria.water_seller.title

The Leaking Waterskin

## ev.alexandria.water_seller.body

Several camels crowd the water seller's shelter beyond the gate, stretching their noses towards the trough. Ali holds up a leather waterskin. Water drips from its mouth, and his apprentice catches it in a bowl, growing flustered.

"Did you tie this?" Ali asks. The boy nods, holding the wet end of the rope. "It was tight last night. It came loose when we filled it."

Ali turns the skin over and shows you two strips of leather stitched around the mouth. "The rope goes through these. Wrap it round the outside and a jolt of the cart will empty it." A camel driver calls that he is ready to leave. Ali asks him to wait and reaches for dry rope.

## ev.alexandria.water_seller.choice.help

"I'll hold the mouth. Show him how to tie it again."

## ev.alexandria.water_seller.choice.cairo

"I can see you're busy. Which caravans go to Cairo?"

## ev.alexandria.water_seller.choice.jerusalem

"Do the caravans for Jerusalem leave from here too?"

## ev.alexandria.water_seller.result.help

You hold the mouth of the skin while Ali threads the rope through the leather. Then he lets the boy pull it tight himself. Once the skin is full, the three of you watch it hang for a while. No new dark patch appears on the ground.

The camel driver shoulders the skin and asks whether Ali will have water tomorrow. "Yes. Come a little later, so my apprentice gets some sleep." The boy laughs and empties his bowl back into the trough. Ali wipes his hands, tells you which gate the Cairo caravan uses, and calls a passing driver over to show you the wagons you can travel with.

## ev.alexandria.water_seller.result.cairo

Ali looks for rope and points beyond the wall at the same time. "The Cairo caravans line up over there. The grain carts take longer. Ask the leader whether he's taking passengers today."

The boy finds a dry length of rope, threads it through the leather, and lifts the skin for Ali to test. This time the water stays in it. The camel driver pats the boy's shoulder and carries the skin out of the gate.

## ev.alexandria.water_seller.result.jerusalem

"Look for the people taking the coast road to Jerusalem." Ali points from the shelter with a wet hand. "They were choosing waterskins this morning. You can still catch them to ask."

The apprentice threads the new rope and holds up the skin, waiting for Ali's verdict. Ali makes him wait a moment, then nods. "That one can go on the cart." The boy hurries towards the caravan with it. You walk to the other line of travellers Ali has shown you.
