## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.title

The Ferry Rope by the Bridge

## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.body

At a small ferry landing in Sindafu, boatwoman Lan is testing a mooring rope while carts queue for the bridge upstream. Her brother says the knot is fine; she asks him to put his weight on it. It slips. ‘It held yesterday,’ he says. Lan shows him the worn outer fibres and asks when he last turned the rope around. He remembers that he did not.

You ask whether the ferry is still running. Lan says it carries people across this part of the river when they do not need the cart bridge. A mother with two tired children has been waiting for a dry place to sit. Lan’s brother spreads a mat under the awning while she prepares the boat. She offers 1.5 silver for a day helping replace and check the mooring line. A quiet crossing costs 1.9 silver; the mother asks whether the children can board after the new knot is tested.

Lan hands her brother the sound end of the rope. He looks at you and asks which post should take the first turn.

## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.choice_a

Spend a day helping Lan replace the ferry rope

## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.choice_a_result

You spend the day checking the posts and tying the new line with Lan’s family. She pays you 1.5 silver for the work. Her brother tests every knot before the mother and children board, and the family remembers your care.

## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.choice_b

Pay 1.9 silver for a quiet ferry crossing

## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.choice_b_result

You pay 1.9 silver and take the ferry after Lan has tested the mooring line. The children sit under the awning and point out fish in the water. You cross without the bridge queue and feel rested for the next part of your journey.
