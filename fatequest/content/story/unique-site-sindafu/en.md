## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.title

Between the River Bridges

## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.body

The river divides Sindafu into its old parts, and bridge traffic is heavy. A boatman asks for help securing his mooring ropes. Another offers a dry boat crossing for a small fee.

## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.choice_a

Help secure the moorings

## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.choice_a_result

You spend a day at the river and earn 150 silver. The boatmen remember your care with their ropes.

## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.choice_b

Pay for a dry crossing

## ev.ev_unique_sindafu.choice_b_result

You pay 190 silver. The boatman spares you the muddy bridge queue; you recover strength during the quiet crossing.
