## city.alexandria.name

Alexandria

## ev.alexandria.entry.body

A peppercorn rolls from a sack and lodges in the stonework by your foot. The
sack still hangs from a scale. Its escort holds the mouth shut while a clerk
holds the ledger open. Both are waiting for a merchant named Hasan.

"Where is his wooden tally?" the clerk asks. The escort searches his belt
and comes up with dust. Someone farther down the quay calls for the clerk,
but he stays by the scale. "Bring Hasan here. I can't enter the cargo without
his tally."

The wind smells of wet rope. A porter edges past you with
a crate on his shoulder. Seeing you linger, he nods towards the city gate:
"That way in. This pepper will be hanging here a while."

## ev.alexandria.entry.choice.look

Stay at the quay and find out why the sack is held up

## ev.alexandria.entry.choice.pass

Ask the porter how to leave by land today

## ev.alexandria.entry.title

Arriving at Alexandria

## ev.ev_alexandria_entry.choice_1_result

You ask the porter how to leave the city. He shifts the crate to his other
shoulder. "Follow the wall to the water seller's shelter. He watches the
caravans pass every day; ask him there." He steadies the swaying crate and
heads back towards the quay.

## ev.ev_alexandria_entry.choice_2_result

Hasan arrives at last. He scolds his escort for leaving the tally behind,
then apologises to the clerk. The clerk checks tally against ledger and
finally lowers the sack onto the scale.

The porter points towards the merchants waiting under the arcade. "If you
carry someone else's goods, bring the tally. Otherwise the pepper may hang
there until dark." You spend a day at the quay and talk with merchants about
the roads out of town. One is leaving for Bethlehem in the morning. He draws
the caravan's gate on a sheet of paper and tells you where to find them.
