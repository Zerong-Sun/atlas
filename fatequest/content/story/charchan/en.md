## city.charchan.name

Charchan

## ev.charchan.entry.body

By a stream outside Charchan, a boy is washing sand from a handful of river stones. His aunt, who buys stones for a trader, asks him to put the cracked pieces back. He holds one up to the light. ‘This one looks sound.’ She turns it in her fingers and shows him a thin line along the edge.

You ask whether every stream here has good drinking water. ‘No,’ the aunt says, and points to two water carriers walking in from different directions. One fills at a sweet well; the other brings water for washing from a bitter source. The boy asks how you can tell the difference. ‘You ask the person who filled the skin,’ she replies. The carrier agrees, then tells him to stop setting the clean stones in the wet sand.

The boy spreads his sleeve and starts again. His aunt says merchants take sound jasper and chalcedony east, though finding a piece worth keeping takes patience. You can mark Charchan and continue, or spend a day asking which wells travellers use and learning how she sorts the stones.

A River Stone at Charchan

## ev.ev_charchan_entry.choice_1_result

The boy waves a clean stone as you leave. His aunt reminds you to ask before filling your water skin at the next well. You mark Charchan on your map.

## ev.ev_charchan_entry.choice_2_result

## ev.charchan.site_gap.title

The Rope at the Oasis Well

## ev.charchan.site_gap.body

At the oasis well, a water carrier named Aysa is changing a worn rope. Her younger brother has tied the replacement to the bucket, but the knot catches on the stone rim each time he lowers it. Aysa asks him to pull it up and try again. He worries the people waiting with their jars will complain.

‘They have seen us fix worse,’ she says. A woman in the queue sets down her jar and asks whether Aysa can still fill it before the cooking fire goes out. Aysa tells her yes and asks you to hold the loose end of the rope. You do, while her brother unwinds the knot and tries a flatter one.

You ask which road reaches Pein. Aysa says she can show you after the bucket comes up smoothly; she knows the drinking wells on that road. Her brother gives the rope a cautious pull. This time the knot clears the rim, and the waiting woman leans forward to see whether the bucket has stayed upright. You can ask Aysa to trace the road, or stay at the well and learn the knot yourself.

## ev.charchan.site_gap.choice.route

Ask Aysa to trace the road to Pein

## ev.ev_charchan_site_gap.choice_1_result

Aysa names the road to Pein and the wells she trusts along it. You mark both the route and Pein on your map while her brother fills the waiting woman’s jar.

## ev.charchan.site_gap.choice.interact

Learn to tie the new rope

## ev.ev_charchan_site_gap.choice_2_result

You try the knot while Aysa holds the bucket. Her brother checks it against the rim; it passes without catching. You record what you learned about the well and its ropes.


## ev.charchan.stonebag.title

The Missing Stone Pouch

## ev.charchan.stonebag.body

A girl at the river crossing is searching the sand for a small cloth pouch. She had gathered three stones for her grandfather, who teaches her to polish them, but one strap broke while she helped a traveller lift his basket. Her grandfather sits nearby with his polishing wheel and calls her back for a drink. She shakes her head: she remembers leaving the pouch above the wet bank.

You ask what the stones look like. ‘One dark, two pale,’ she says. ‘The dark one has a white line.’ A boatman points out that the water has already fallen since morning; anything dropped near the bank may still be between the reeds. Her grandfather calls again, asking whether she wants him to come. She finally tells him what happened. ‘We can look together,’ he says, leaving the wheel covered against the dust.

You can spend the day searching the reeds with them, or help the boatman finish sorting his cargo and earn a little money before the next crossing. The girl says she will keep looking either way, and her grandfather brings her water first.

## ev.charchan.stonebag.choice.search

Search the reeds for a day

## ev.charchan.stonebag.result.search

You find the torn strap caught beneath a reed root. The three stones are still in the pouch. The girl shows her grandfather the one with the white line; he says she may polish it tomorrow. You spend the day there, and the family thanks you.

## ev.charchan.stonebag.choice.cargo

Sort the boatman’s cargo

## ev.charchan.stonebag.result.cargo

You sort the sacks by crossing and the boatman pays you 0.8 silver. As you finish, the grandfather and girl find the pouch at the edge of the reeds. She holds it up for you to see before they go home.
