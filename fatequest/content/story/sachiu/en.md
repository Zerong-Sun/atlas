## ev.sachiu.entry.body

After you have travelled thirty days through the Great Desert, you come to a city called
SACHIU, lying between north-east and east. It belongs to the Great Kaan and lies in a
province called Tangut. The people are for the most part Idolaters, though there are
some Nestorian Christians and some Saracens. Those who are Idolaters have a peculiar
language and are no traders, but live by the fruits of the earth. They have many abbeys
and minsters, all full of idols of divers fashions, to which they pay great honour and
show reverence. The city lies on the edge of the Desert, where caravans restock after
the terrible crossing; the farmers grow excellent melons and wheat in the irrigated
fields.
