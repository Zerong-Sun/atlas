## div.western.result.00

The Sun in the first house: the journey begins in your favor.

## div.western.result.01

The Moon in the second house: keep your purse close; spending runs high.

## div.western.result.02

Mercury in the third house: letters and talk speed your way.

## div.western.result.03

Venus in the fourth house: home calls, but the road holds a meeting.

## div.western.result.04

Mars in the fifth house: bold play wins at the gaming house; leave before midnight.

## div.western.result.05

Jupiter in the sixth house: a servant or hireling proves honest.

## div.western.result.06

Saturn in the seventh house: a partnership is tested; sign after the inspection.

## div.western.result.07

The Sun in the eighth house: another's goods pass through your hands; guard them.

## div.western.result.08

The Moon in the ninth house: a long voyage is blessed by the stars.

## div.western.result.09

Mercury in the tenth house: your trade is spoken of; your name opens the gate.

## div.western.result.10

Venus in the eleventh house: a friend brings news of a fair.

## div.western.result.11

Mars in the twelfth house: an enemy works in shadow; keep counsel.

## div.western.result.12

The ascendant in the Bull: move slowly but steadily; the load is heavy.

## div.western.result.13

The ascendant in the Twins: two roads; take the one the merchants take.

## div.western.result.14

The ascendant in the Crab: the road by the water favors you.

## div.western.result.15

The ascendant in the Lion: act like a prince; the town will follow.

## div.western.result.16

The ascendant in the Maiden: check your accounts before you sleep.

## div.western.result.17

The ascendant in the Archer: aim far; the arrow lands well.

## div.western.result.20

The ascendant in the Fishes: follow the current; it leads to the market.

## div.western.result.24

Jupiter ascends: luck grows with each league; share a little.

## div.western.result.26
