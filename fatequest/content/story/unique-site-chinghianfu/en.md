## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.title

The Loom’s Gold Thread

## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.body

A weaver has set gold thread beside an unfinished cloth. His apprentice has wound one bobbin too tightly, and the thread pulls against the pattern. ‘I wanted every turn to match,’ she says. He asks her to feel how the thread moves when he loosens it a little. You hold the bobbin while she tries again. A buyer arrives with a sample of the cloth he ordered. He wants to know why a narrow strip costs more than a wider plain one. The weaver lays the sample beside the loom and shows the small turns where the gold thread must be guided by hand. His apprentice points out how often they have to stop and adjust it. The buyer says he will wait until they finish measuring the next section. The weaver could use your help for a day winding and checking thread; he also offers a paid lesson on how he counts materials and labour before naming a price.

## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.choice_a

Spend a day winding the thread

## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.choice_a_result

You stay another day at the loom, handing the weaver evenly wound thread while his apprentice watches the pattern. He shows you how to feel a snag before the cloth is damaged. The family thanks you for helping finish a difficult section, and their buyer learns your name.

## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.choice_b

Pay for a lesson on cloth prices

## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.choice_b_result

You pay the weaver for his time. He lays out the plain and gold-thread samples, counts the material used in each, and shows where the extra handwork appears. His apprentice checks your figures, leaving you better able to judge the next cloth offered for sale.
