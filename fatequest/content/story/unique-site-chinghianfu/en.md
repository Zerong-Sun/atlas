## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.title

The Gold Thread Measure

## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.body

At Chinghianfu, a silk weaver lays gold thread beside a half-finished bolt. The loom crew needs another hand to wind it evenly. The master will instead teach you how labour and thread set the price.

## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.choice_a

Help wind the gold thread

## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.choice_a_result

You spend a day at the loom. The weavers remember your careful work, and you learn to work with them.

## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.choice_b

Pay the silk master for a pricing lesson

## ev.ev_unique_chinghianfu.choice_b_result

You pay 180 silver. The master shows how thread and labour change the price of a bolt, improving your judgement in trade.
