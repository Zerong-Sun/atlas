## city.paukin.name

Paukin

## ev.paukin.entry.body

When you leave Coiganju you ride south-east for a day along a causeway laid with fine
stone, which you find at this entrance to Manzi. On either hand there is a great expanse
of water, so that you cannot enter the province except along this causeway. At the end
of the day’s journey you reach the fine city of PAUKIN. The people are Idolaters, burn
their dead, are subject to the Great Kaan, and use paper-money. They live by trade and
manufactures and have great abundance of silk, whereof they weave a great variety of
fine stuffs of silk and gold. Of all the necessaries of life there is great store.
