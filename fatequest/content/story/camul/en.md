## ev.camul.entry.body

Hami — the first oasis east of the Great Desert, where the traveler who has survived the
sand begins to breathe again. The people are Idolaters who once practiced a strange
custom: they offered their wives to passing strangers as a mark of hospitality. The
Great Khan forbade it, but the old people still speak of it. The melons of this district
are the sweetest on the whole road.
