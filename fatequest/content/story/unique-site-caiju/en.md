## ev.ev_unique_caiju.title

The Grain Tally

## ev.ev_unique_caiju.body

At Caiju, rice and corn for the Kaan’s court pass from river boats into marked storehouses. A tally keeper needs someone to check each mark; a boat master will copy his departure list for a small fee.

## ev.ev_unique_caiju.choice_a

Check the grain tally yourself

## ev.ev_unique_caiju.choice_a_result

You spend a day matching sacks to marks. The keeper entrusts you with the final count, and you learn the scale of the grain trade.

## ev.ev_unique_caiju.choice_b

Buy the departure list

## ev.ev_unique_caiju.choice_b_result

You pay 150 silver. It names the next waterway toward Chinghianfu and shows how the grain fleet is timed.
