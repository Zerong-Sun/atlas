## challenge.liuyao.culture

Moon blocks on the mat

## challenge.jiaobei.culture

Coastal China · Moon blocks

## challenge.jiaobei.note

Read the flat and curved faces. Local terms vary; this exercise labels two flat faces laughing, two curved faces yin, and mixed faces sheng.

Align three twelve-sector rings. Coupled gears are a game device for learning circular positions, not a complete astronomical calculation.

Study carved marks, then uncover matching symbols. Inspired by writing and carving, without claiming to reconstruct a disputed ancient divination system.

Study the bright points, cover the reference, then restore their positions. Observation is self-paced, with no forced countdown.

## challenge.tarot.title

Choose three of seven two-symbol practice cards to meet Cups, Swords, Batons and Coins exactly. These composite cards are a game adaptation of Italian suits.

## challenge.lenormand.title

## challenge.lenormand.culture

Europe · Nineteenth-century small-card tradition

## challenge.lenormand.note

Build the requested boat, key, letter and tree combination from a limited hand. Composite practice cards use these motifs without posing as the original thirty-six-card deck.

Use three practice cards to compose moon, lamp, gate and seed motifs. Oracle decks have no single ancient system.

## challenge.bazi.title

Linked five phases

## challenge.bazi.culture

China · Foundations for Bazi

## challenge.bazi.note

Follow Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water in the generating cycle. Each move changes its phase and the next through three levels: a relationship puzzle, not a strength calculation.

## challenge.bazi-relationship.title

Interwoven five phases

## challenge.bazi-relationship.culture

Chinese five-phase ideas · Game adaptation

## challenge.bazi-relationship.note

Change a phase and the one two places ahead, following Wood–Earth–Water–Fire–Metal–Wood to restore the pattern.

## challenge.numerology.title

Balancing number stones

## challenge.numerology.culture

Cross-cultural · Number symbolism

## challenge.numerology.note

Use exactly three stones to reach the target, each at most once. Practice arithmetic without conflating different cultural number systems.

## challenge.meihua.title

## challenge.meihua.culture

China · Meihua Yishu

## challenge.meihua.note

Choose three stones to meet the total, then inspect residues modulo eight and six, using eight or six for zero. This is a number exercise, not the full casting procedure.

## challenge.ui.parity_rule

Add each row: odd → one point, even → two. Click an answer cell to toggle.

## challenge.ui.rings_rule

Turning one ring also turns its neighbour. Align all three pointers. Twelve sectors run clockwise from the top.

## challenge.ui.route_rule

Move to adjacent cells without revisiting. Pass both ◆ marks, then reach ◎. × blocks passage. Undo is available.

## challenge.ui.memory_rule

Study eight marks, then cover them. Flip two at a time; match the shapes. Mismatches spend a mistake.

## challenge.ui.trace_rule

Study the marks on the left. Cover them, then restore all six on the right; leave blank cells clear.

## challenge.ui.draft_rule

Choose exactly three cards. All four symbol counts must match the target. Click again to return a card.

## challenge.ui.cycle_rule

Click a phase to add one to it and its linked phase; 2 wraps to 0. Restore all five targets. You can undo.

## challenge.ui.numbers_rule

Choose exactly three stones from distinct positions to reach the target sum. Click again to return a stone.

## challenge.ui.timing_rule

Press Steady when the pointer enters the gold band. Each round narrows the band; a mentor hint removes timing.
