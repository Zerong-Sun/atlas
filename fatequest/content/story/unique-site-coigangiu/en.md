## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.title

A Hand for the Grain Boat

## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.body

A grain boat at the Coigangiu landing needs an extra rower for a difficult bend. Its owner has asked for help until the cargo reaches the next mooring. His daughter is checking the paddle straps and notices one has begun to fray. ‘If it breaks midstream, you will lose the whole stroke,’ she says. Her father finds a sound spare and lets her fit it. You ask whether the work will last all day. ‘Through the bend and back to the mooring, yes,’ he answers, offering wages for the effort. A local guide beside the shed overhears and says he can instead show you the dry road toward Paukin for a small fee. The daughter asks him whether the first bridge is still passable after rain. He has crossed it this morning and draws the firm approach in the sand. The boat owner tests the replacement strap with both hands, then calls for whoever will take the spare paddle.

## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.choice_a

Row with the grain crew for a day

## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.choice_a_result

You row through the bend and return to the mooring after a hard day. The boat owner pays your agreed wage and tells the crews who helped him. Your arms ache from the work, but the daughter is pleased that her repaired strap held fast.

## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.choice_b

Pay the guide for the Paukin road

## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.choice_b_result

You pay the local guide for his directions. He walks you to the first bridge and points out the dry approach toward Paukin, then marks the later turns on your board. The daughter calls from the grain boat as it leaves the quay.
