## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.title

One More Boat for the Fleet

## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.body

The grain fleet has filled the canal at Coigangiu. A boatman needs an oarsman for a hard day against the current; a pilot offers a place on the leading boat for a fee.

## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.choice_a

Row against the current for pay

## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.choice_a_result

You spend a tiring day at the oars and earn 200 silver. Your arms ache, but the boat families remember you.

## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.choice_b

Pay to ride with the pilot

## ev.ev_unique_coigangiu.choice_b_result

You pay 180 silver. From the leading boat you see the waterway toward Paukin and learn its turns.
