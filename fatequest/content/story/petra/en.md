## ev.petra.entry.body

Petra — the rose-red city of the Nabateans, carved into the living rock of the desert
cliffs. Its temples and tombs, pillared and pedimented, still stand in the narrow gorge
called the Siq. Now a way-station on the Hajj road, its caves shelter caravans bound for
Mecca and Damascus. The Bedouin guard the passes for a toll; no one passes without their
leave.
