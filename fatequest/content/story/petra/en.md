## city.petra.name

Petra

## ev.petra.entry.body

You follow a narrow gorge into Petra. Old water channels are still visible along the rock. Near the opening, Rashida has stretched a cloth shelter and moved the waterskins into its shade. Her grandson Sami counts with pebbles. Each time someone has a bowl of water, he moves one pebble to the other side.

“Why haven't we touched that skin?” Sami asks.

“It's for your father,” Rashida says. “He went to look for the goats beyond the slope. Last time he came back late, everyone had drunk the water. He ate dry bread first and waited.”

Sami says his father had forgotten to take a waterskin with him. Rashida laughs. “This time I'm remembering for him.” She sees you standing nearby and sets out an empty bowl. Sami quickly moves a pebble back. He says a guest should drink before he counts it.

## ev.petra.entry.choice.pass

Thank Rashida and continue through the gorge

## ev.petra.entry.choice.rest

## ev.petra.entry.title

Petra

## ev.ev_petra_entry.choice_1_result

You drink and thank Rashida. Sami points out the well-trodden path beyond the gorge, then carefully moves his pebble to the other side. You walk between the rock walls and mark Petra on your map.

## ev.ev_petra_entry.choice_2_result

You spend the day in the shelter and help Sami hang the empty waterskins to dry. At dusk his father comes back with the goats. He gives Rashida a small bag of flour, then drinks from the skin she saved for him. “I was thinking about this water all the way back,” he says. Sami moves a pebble across to count his father's share.

They make hot soup and ask you to eat with them. You sleep in the shade of the rock and leave the next morning, your legs rested. You mark Petra on your map.

## ev.gap.petra.scene.title

The Waterskin's Seam

## ev.gap.petra.scene.body

Rashida's son Hassan sits beside the shelter mending a waterskin. He pulls out an old leather cord and hands it to his daughter Nadia. “This can still serve. Try tightening the mouth of the bag.”

Nadia does, but a small drop seeps from the side. She admits she patched that spot only yesterday. Hassan holds the skin up to the light. “Your new stitches are firm. It's the old seam beside them that's leaking. I used to check only the part I'd just sewn too.”

Nadia threads her needle and asks whether you will hold the bag for her. Hassan has drawn several routes towards Egypt in the sand and is showing another traveller where to carry extra water.

You ask Nadia whether she has repaired a water skin alone before. ‘Only the small one my grandmother carries,’ she says. Hasan shows her how to keep a little slack beside the seam so it does not tear when the skin is filled. Rashida comes over with a cup and asks whether they have tested the patch. Nadia fills the skin partway, holds it over the sand, and waits. No drop falls this time. Her father asks her to mark the repaired side with a bit of thread so they can check it again before the next journey.

## ev.gap.petra.scene.choice.talk

Hold the waterskin while Nadia repairs the old seam

## ev.gap.petra.scene.choice.route

Listen to Hassan describe the Cairo road, then check on Nadia

## ev.gap.petra.scene.result.talk

You hold the leather while Nadia sews along the loose stitches. Hassan pours in half a bowl of water and has her hang up the skin to test it. After a while, the seam is still dry. Nadia puts the needle back into the ball of thread. “I'll check my own bag tonight.” You remember where to find the shelter if you need a waterskin repaired on another journey through Petra.

## ev.gap.petra.scene.result.route

Hassan draws the road to Cairo in the sand and shows you where travellers may miss a water source. You copy his directions. Beside you, Nadia finishes her stitching, tests the skin with water, and hangs it in the shade again. Hassan sees that she has checked the old seam as well. You add Cairo and this road to your map.

## ev.petra.goatbell.title

A Bell in the Gorge

## ev.petra.goatbell.body

Beside a rock face with a carved doorway, Inan is looking for a grey goat. His daughter Maya hears a bell and takes a few steps into the gorge. Then the sound seems to come from the other side, and she stops.

“Which way did it go?” she asks.

Inan looks at the hoofprints. “It went around that big rock and into the shade. The sound moves between the walls. We'll follow the prints.” Maya says this was the first goat she had been trusted to watch alone. She hurried away to help carry water that morning and did not tie its rope tightly enough.

Inan lets her take a moment. He lays the grass he brought beside the stone steps. “It might come out when it smells this. Will you follow the prints with her, or wait here with us?”

You ask Maya what she calls the goat. ‘Cloud,’ she says, because its grey back looks pale in the morning. Inan asks whether Cloud knows the sound of her grain pouch. Maya shakes it gently; the goat bleats from behind the stone. ‘There you are,’ she says, but her father asks her to walk slowly so it will not bolt farther into the shade. She looks back at the loose rope in her hand and asks him to show her the knot once more when they reach it.

## ev.petra.goatbell.choice.tracks

Follow the hoofprints with Maya

## ev.petra.goatbell.choice.wait

Wait by the stone steps and rest overnight

## ev.petra.goatbell.result.tracks

You and Maya walk around the large rock and find the grey goat beside a narrow crevice. It is eating a low patch of grass, the bell still around its neck. Maya holds out the cut grass and waits until it approaches before taking the rope. This time she ties the knot twice and asks her father to pull it tight.

Inan pats the goat and thanks you. Back at the resting place he tells the nearby families what happened. When you pass them again, they call you over for water.

## ev.petra.goatbell.result.wait

You wait beside the steps with Inan and Maya until dark. Maya starts towards the gorge several times, but Inan asks her to listen. The bell is drawing closer. The grey goat looks out from behind the rock wall. Maya lures it over with grass and reties the loosened rope.

Inan's family asks you to stay and shares warm bread and soup. You sleep against the rock that night and leave the next morning with your strength restored.
