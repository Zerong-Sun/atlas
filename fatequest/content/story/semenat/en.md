## city.semenat.name

Semenat

## ev.semenat.entry.body

At Semenat’s market, a flower seller is helping her father move baskets away from the path used by the crews bringing goods from the landing. He points to the smallest basket. ‘That one belongs at the shrine. Your aunt will wonder where it has gone.’ She replies, ‘Auntie borrowed it yesterday to carry supper home.’ The father asks whether you have seen a woman with a basket of yellow flowers. You have not, but a basket maker beside them has. He says she passed toward the temple lane and promised to bring it back when she had emptied it. The daughter laughs: ‘Then the flowers are probably gone and the supper stayed in the basket.’ A sailor comes to ask which boat next sails toward Tana. The basket maker knows a captain at the landing who has room for passengers. The father asks his daughter to keep the stall open while he checks on his sister, and she offers to show you the market if you can spend the day here. She ties the stall cloth back so the crews can pass without stepping over the baskets.

## ev.semenat.entry.title

The Borrowed Flower Basket

## ev.ev_semenat_entry.choice_1_result

The basket maker points you through Semenat’s market toward the landing, where captains announce the boats bound along the coast. The flower seller is still calling after her father about the missing basket. You know how to find the quay before you continue.

## ev.ev_semenat_entry.choice_2_result

You spend another day at the market. The aunt returns the basket after lunch, telling her niece what she carried in it. The flower seller introduces nearby stalls and walks you to the landing where a boat for Tana takes passengers. You know the people and the quay better when you leave.

## ev.semenat.site_gap.title

A Basket Made from Leaves

## ev.semenat.site_gap.body

Under a market awning, a basket maker is showing his daughter how to fold a fresh palm leaf back through the rim. She has made the base sound but keeps pulling one side tighter than the other. ‘It looks like a boat,’ she says. ‘A boat that drops the fruit when you lift it,’ he answers, smiling. You ask how long the leaves must dry before the basket can carry a load. He lifts an older one from a beam and lets you feel its firm edge. The girl tries again, turning the basket as she works rather than tugging at one place. A deckhand waiting for fruit baskets asks whether they will finish before his boat sails toward Tana. ‘Tell her where you stop along the coast,’ the father says. The deckhand names a sheltered landing where they take on water. The girl insists he repeat it after she has tightened the last leaf. He sets down his rope coil to watch her test the basket with both hands.

## ev.semenat.site_gap.choice.route

Ask the carrier about the Tana road

## ev.ev_semenat_site_gap.choice_1_result

The deckhand describes the boat bound toward Tana and the coastal landing where it takes on water. The girl repeats the names while lifting her finished basket, pleased that neither her route note nor the rim has gone crooked.

## ev.semenat.site_gap.choice.interact

Help the girl finish the basket rim

## ev.ev_semenat_site_gap.choice_2_result

You hold the basket while she folds each leaf stem through the rim. Her father asks her to lift it by both handles and tests it with a few pieces of fruit. It stays level. He shows you how the folded stems share the weight instead of tearing one side.

## ev.semenat.lamps.title

The Shrine Keeper’s Oil Lamp

## ev.semenat.lamps.body

A keeper at a small shrine is checking the lamps before visitors arrive. His niece has cleaned them and set them in a row, but one has a wick much shorter than the others. “You took a bit from it to mend the kitchen lamp,” he says. She nods and shows the strip she saved. He asks her to put the spare strip in the shrine lamp, then notices that the shelf beneath it tilts toward the doorway. A carpenter from the neighbouring stall offers to adjust the shelf after he finishes a customer's box. The niece wants to do it sooner: visitors will come before dusk. “Then ask him what tools you may borrow,” her uncle says. She walks over, returns with a wooden wedge and asks you to steady the shelf while she places it. The keeper watches the lamps from the doorway, making sure people can pass without brushing the flames. The niece tests the wedge with the empty shelf first. Her uncle hands her a lamp only after the wood has stopped shifting.

## ev.semenat.lamps.choice.shelf

Help steady the lamp shelf

## ev.semenat.lamps.result.shelf

You hold the shelf while the niece taps the wedge beneath it. The carpenter comes over to check her work, and the keeper lights each wick. Visitors can pass without touching the lamps. He thanks you in front of them for helping make the entrance safe.

## ev.semenat.lamps.choice.stay

Stay for the evening gathering

## ev.semenat.lamps.result.stay

You stay through the day. The niece learns to set the wicks at equal height, and her uncle asks her to light the first lamp at dusk. After visitors leave, they invite you to eat with them. You rest before returning to the market the next morning.
