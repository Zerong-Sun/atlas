## city.semenat.name

Semenat

## ev.semenat.entry.body

Semenat is a great kingdom towards the west. The people are Idolaters, and have a king
and a language of their own, and pay tribute to nobody. They are not corsairs, but live
by trade and industry as honest people ought. It is a place of very great trade. They
are forsooth cruel Idolaters.

## ev.semenat.entry.choice.look

## ev.semenat.entry.choice.pass

## ev.semenat.entry.title

Concerning the Kingdom of Semenat

## ev.ev_semenat_entry.choice_1_result

You pass through without stopping. You set the way to Semenat on your map.

## ev.ev_semenat_entry.choice_2_result

You rest and ask what men say of this place. The road to Semenat opens on your map. The account is written down before the hour turns. It costs you 1 day on the road.

## ev.ev_semenat_a.choice_1_result

He sells you cotton at a fair weight, weighed openly before your eyes. The account of Semenat goes with it.

## ev.ev_semenat_a.choice_2_result

Cambaet and Tana are marked on your map. The western trade roads are open.

## ev.ev_semenat_a.choice_3_result

You spend a day at the scales. The market's law is learned, and a small turn of fortune favours you.

## ev.semenat.a.title

Semenat: The Honest Trade of the Free Kingdom

## ev.semenat.a.body

In the great kingdom towards the west that pays tribute to nobody, the merchants of Semenat are not corsairs but live by trade and industry as honest people ought, and their city is a place of very great trade. A merchant who has weighed cargoes for thirty years says the kingdom has its own king and its own language, and that a traveller who deals straight with this market is dealt with straight in return — the one law of the place. He asks your word on it.

## ev.semenat.a.choice.1

Buy cotton cloth at the merchant's straight price

## ev.semenat.a.choice.2

Ask the merchant the road toward Cambaet

## ev.semenat.a.choice.3

Watch the weighing a day and learn the market's law

## ev.ev_semenat_a_followup.title

Semenat: The Merchant's Second Word on the Scale

## ev.ev_semenat_a_followup.body

The merchant sets a balance on the bale between you and lays a stone of known weight beside it. He says the market of Semenat runs on this: the stone is the same for everyone, and the man who changes the stone for his own gain is not a merchant but a thief, and is dealt with as such. He asks whether you will trade by the same stone.

## ev.ev_semenat_a_followup.choice_1

Buy indigo at the market's known-weight price

## ev.ev_semenat_a_followup.choice_1_result

He sells you indigo at a fair weight, weighed on the same stone. The dye is ready for the road.

## ev.ev_semenat_a_followup.choice_2

Ask him to mark the road toward Delli

## ev.ev_semenat_a_followup.choice_2_result

Delli is marked on your map with the days between. The inland road is open.

## ev.ev_semenat_a_followup.choice_3

Wait a day at the scales and learn the weights' keeping

## ev.ev_semenat_a_followup.choice_3_result

You wait a day at the scales. The weights' keeping is learned, and in Semenat your name now carries a little more weight.
