## city.semenat.name

Semenat

## ev.semenat.entry.body

Semenat is a great kingdom towards the west. The people are Idolaters, and have a king
and a language of their own, and pay tribute to nobody. They are not corsairs, but live
by trade and industry as honest people ought. It is a place of very great trade. They
are forsooth cruel Idolaters.

Concerning the Kingdom of Semenat

## ev.ev_semenat_entry.choice_1_result

## ev.ev_semenat_entry.choice_2_result

You rest and ask what men say of this place. The road to Semenat opens on your map. The account is written down before the hour turns. It costs you 1 day on the road.

## ev.semenat.site_gap.title

Weaving Coconut Leaves at the Road Pavilion

## ev.semenat.site_gap.body

Coconut-leaf baskets hang beneath a pavilion roof. A basket maker flattens the leaf ribs and weaves them across one another. Every few rows he folds a rib back to spread the load, keeping the mouth from stretching wide. He lets you hold an empty basket. Newly made ones must dry under the eaves before storage, or green leaves may grow mould.

## ev.semenat.site_gap.choice.route

Ask about the road to Tana

## ev.ev_semenat_site_gap.choice_1_result

“Follow this road south to Tana, past villages where coconuts grow. If you want the market, leave in the morning; carts crowd the road later.”

## ev.semenat.site_gap.choice.interact

Try weaving a row around the rim

## ev.ev_semenat_site_gap.choice_2_result

He steadies the basket as you tuck each leaf rib into a gap. “Work all the way around before drawing it tight, or the basket will lean to one side.” Your palms smell faintly of the leaves.
