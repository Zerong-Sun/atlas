## city.semenat.name

Semenat

## ev.semenat.entry.body

Semenat is a great kingdom towards the west. The people are Idolaters, and have a king
and a language of their own, and pay tribute to nobody. They are not corsairs, but live
by trade and industry as honest people ought. It is a place of very great trade. They
are forsooth cruel Idolaters.
