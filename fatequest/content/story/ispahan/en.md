## ev.ispahan.entry.body

Isfahan — the garden city of the Persian plateau, where the Friday Mosque with its twin
domes of turquoise tile has stood since the days of the Seljuk sultans. The Zayandeh
River waters a hundred gardens within the walls. Its workshops produce the finest
miniature paintings, the deepest blue tiles, and the softest carpets in the Ilkhan's
lands. The covered bridge of the Jews crosses the river; merchants from India, Khorasan,
and Anatolia crowd its arcades.
