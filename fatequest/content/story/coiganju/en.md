## city.coiganju.name

Coiganju

## ev.coiganju.entry.body

Coiganju is, as I have told you already, a very large city standing at the entrance to
Manzi. The people are Idolaters and burn their dead, and are subject to the Great Kaan.
They have a vast amount of shipping, as I mentioned before in speaking of the River
Caramoran. And an immense quantity of merchandize comes hither, for the city is the seat
of government for this part of the country. Owing to its being on the river, many cities
send their produce thither to be again thence distributed in every direction.
