## ev.zayton.bowl.title

The bowl her uncle brought home

## ev.zayton.bowl.body

At the soup shop by the bridge, A-He has set a wooden bowl in the sunlight. Her uncle brought it back from his last voyage. A crack runs through its bottom, and her mother says soup will escape as soon as it is filled. A-He has borrowed a thin strip of wood from the boatyard and is trying to fit it into the crack.

"It held our rice through three storms," her uncle says. "You might let it rest." A-He asks why he carried it all the way home if he meant to throw it away. He smiles, then holds the bowl while she measures the gap. Her mother comes out with hot water and asks them to test it outside first. The shop's other bowls are stacked by the stove; she can still serve lunch while they work.

The uncle tells you the bowl was bought from a boatman bound for Chamba. A-He asks him to show her where the ship stopped for water, since she wants to mark his travels on the inside of the lid. He draws a coastline in spilled flour. You can help fit the patch and stay for the water test, or ask him to draw the sea road clearly for your own map.

## ev.zayton.bowl.choice.help

Stay a day to mend the bowl with A-He

## ev.zayton.bowl.choice.route

Ask her uncle to show the sea road to Chamba

## ev.zayton.bowl.result.help

You hold the bowl while A-He fits the patch. By evening it holds a little water. Her mother serves you soup from another bowl and asks A-He to let the repair dry overnight. The family remembers your help.

## ev.zayton.bowl.result.route

Her uncle marks the ports where the ship took on water. A-He copies their names onto a wooden slip and reads them back to him. You add Chamba and the sea road from Zayton to your map.
