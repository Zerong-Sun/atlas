## div.liuyao.result.00

The first line moves: the foundation is sound; begin as planned.

## div.liuyao.result.01

The second line holds: a neighbor's help comes at the river.

## div.liuyao.result.02

The third line trembles: do not go forward half-prepared.

## div.liuyao.result.03

The fourth line yields: a door opens that you thought closed.

## div.liuyao.result.04

The fifth line stands firm: the prince of the road favors you.

## div.liuyao.result.05

The sixth line turns back: return; the way ahead is not yet ready.

## div.liuyao.result.06

A moving line in the money seat: silver will change hands twice before dusk.

## div.liuyao.result.07

The officer line is strong: one who holds authority will aid you.

## div.liuyao.result.08

The self line is weak: rest before you answer the call.

## div.liuyao.result.09

A strong father line: a letter or a guide comes first.

## div.liuyao.result.10

The enemy line faces you: do not provoke the argument at the inn.

## div.liuyao.result.11

A line of response rises: your question is answered in three days.

## div.liuyao.result.12

The god of joy sits on the road: an auspicious journey; laugh as you go.

## div.liuyao.result.13

A hidden line in the palace: a secret of the house will surface.

## div.liuyao.result.14

The star of calamity appears: a small loss saves a greater one.

## div.liuyao.result.15

The line of the tomb seals the plan: delay until the next full moon.

## div.liuyao.result.16

A line of the void: what you seek is not where you look; ask again.

## div.liuyao.result.17

The changing line opens the road: a gate that was shut now stands ajar.

## div.liuyao.result.18

A line of the crown prince: a younger person speaks truth; listen.

## div.liuyao.result.19

The line of the servant is weak: do not rely on another's errand.

## div.liuyao.result.20

A line of the spouse crosses the travel: a marriage or a bond changes the route.

## div.liuyao.result.21

The line of the thunder: act, but expect a sudden turn.

## div.liuyao.result.22

A line of the lake: pleasure delays the schedule; keep the watch.

## div.liuyao.result.23

The line of the mountain: a barrier holds; wait for the season to turn.

## div.liuyao.result.24

A line of wind: news travels fast; your plans are already known.

## div.liuyao.result.25

The line of fire: anger flares; keep your temper at the market.

## div.liuyao.result.26

A line of water: an obstacle dissolves; the way clears by evening.

## div.liuyao.result.27

The line of earth: stability; stay with what you know.

## div.liuyao.result.28

A line of the gate: an official checks your papers; have them ready.

## div.liuyao.result.29

The six lines settle: the cast is complete; go in peace with its counsel.
