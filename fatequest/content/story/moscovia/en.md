## ev.moscovia.entry.body

Moscow — a wooden fortress on a northern river, deep in the forests of Rus. The Grand
Prince pays homage to the Khan at Sarai each year, bearing tribute of sable furs and
forest honey. The Kremlin walls are of oak; the churches are of white stone brought from
distant quarries. Caravans of merchants from Novgorod and the Baltic call here before
the winter snows close the roads.
