## city.moscovia.name

Moscovia

## ev.moscovia.entry.title

A Letter beside the River

## ev.moscovia.entry.body

At the Moscow landing, a woman named Darya is wrapping a packet of cloth for a northern traveller. Her little son presses a birchbark note into the knot. ‘For Grandmother,’ he says. Darya unties the packet, puts the note inside where it will stay dry, and makes him tie it again.

Houses and workshops spread from the river toward the town’s churches and walls. Timber, wax, and furs pass through the landing. Darya tells you where the caravans stop, then calls her son back before he can ask the traveller for a second promise.

The traveller asks the boy, ‘What did you write?’ The boy grips the packet and says it is a surprise. Darya replies, ‘Your grandmother can barely see small letters. Did you make them large enough?’ He takes the note back for a moment, frowns, and promises to check. You ask when the northern party leaves. Darya says it is waiting for a wagon axle to be repaired; there is time to find a bed by the landing before they set out.

## ev.moscovia.entry.choice.pass

Continue through the landing

## ev.moscovia.entry.choice.rest

Rest for a day by the river

## ev.ev_moscovia_entry.choice_1_result

Darya shows you the way into town. You mark Moscow on your map and continue.

## ev.ev_moscovia_entry.choice_2_result

You spend the day in a room above the landing and regain your strength. Darya’s son draws the river bend for you, and you add Moscow to your map.

## ev.gap.moscovia.scene.title

The Fur Lining

## ev.gap.moscovia.scene.body

A fur seller named Pavel turns a pelt over in his hands. His mother wants a warm lining for her coat, and he has kept this one for her. His apprentice nearly hangs it beside the fire to dry.

‘In the shade,’ Pavel reminds him. ‘She complained last time when the edge curled.’ The apprentice carries it away, smiling at the thought of Pavel’s mother inspecting his work. Pavel knows the road south toward Kyiv if you need it.

You ask Pavel if his mother knows about the lining. ‘She does,’ he says. ‘She keeps asking when I’ll stop measuring it and start sewing.’ His apprentice calls from the shaded rack that the pelt is dry enough now. Pavel makes him bring it over and checks the back for stiff patches. ‘This part goes under the collar,’ he tells him. ‘She’ll notice if it scratches.’ He offers to show you where the Kyiv caravans gather after the fitting is cut.

## ev.gap.moscovia.scene.choice.talk

Help choose the lining

## ev.gap.moscovia.scene.choice.route

Ask about the road to Kyiv

## ev.gap.moscovia.scene.result.talk

You feel the pelt along its grain while Pavel checks the leather side. He folds it for his mother and points out his stall on your map of Moscow.

## ev.gap.moscovia.scene.result.route

Pavel tells you where the southbound caravans meet and which road reaches Kyiv. You mark the road and city while his apprentice folds the lining.

## ev.moscovia.birchletter.title

A Note for Grandmother

## ev.moscovia.birchletter.body

Before the northern traveller departs, Darya’s son returns with the note he took out of the packet. He has copied it twice in larger letters because he worries the first will be hard for his grandmother to read. Both say that he has learned to mend a net and wants his grandmother to visit when the river opens. The traveller is mending the last strap on his wagon outside.

Darya suggests giving him the clearer copy and keeping the other. Her son asks you to read it aloud first. You can sit with him over the words, or take the note to the traveller yourself and earn a small fee for the errand.

The boy points to a crooked letter and asks whether his grandmother will think it says ‘boat’ instead of ‘net.’ Darya leans over the bark: ‘Read the whole sentence to me.’ He does, slowly. The traveller knocks at the doorway and says his wagon is nearly ready. ‘One more moment,’ Darya calls. You can help the boy choose the clearer copy, or carry it down to the wagon while Darya finishes tying the cloth.

## ev.moscovia.birchletter.choice.read

Read the note with the boy

## ev.moscovia.birchletter.choice.deliver

Take the note to the traveller

## ev.moscovia.birchletter.result.read

You read each line and let the boy correct his own spelling. He takes the clearer copy to the traveller himself. Darya thanks you; the family will remember your patience.

## ev.moscovia.birchletter.result.deliver

You place the note in the traveller’s packet and return to tell the boy it is safely tucked away. Darya pays you 0.4 silver for the errand. He keeps the other copy and reads it once more to her.
