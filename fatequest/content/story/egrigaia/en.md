## city.egrigaia.name

Egrigaia

## ev.egrigaia.entry.body

Starting again from Erguiul you ride eastward for eight days, and then come to a
province called EGRIGAIA, containing numerous cities and villages, and belonging to
Tangut. The capital city is called CALACHAN. The people are chiefly Idolaters, but there
are fine churches belonging to the Nestorian Christians. They are all subjects of the
Great Kaan. They make in this city great quantities of camlets of camel’s wool, the
finest in the world; and some of the camlets that they make are white, for they have
white camels, and these are the best of all. Merchants purchase these stuffs here, and
carry them over the world for sale.

## ev.egrigaia.site_gap.title

The Smoke Hole of a Felt Tent

## ev.egrigaia.site_gap.body

A host shows you the adjustable opening at the roof of his felt tent. In a light wind he narrows it; in a strong one he opens it to keep smoke from blowing back inside. He lifts the door flap slightly: cool air enters low and smoke leaves high. He points to patches sewn around the opening, which keep out wind and snow.

## ev.ev_egrigaia_site_gap.choice_1_result

“Take this road south to the Tenduc camp,” he says. “You can recognise the path the herds take. At a fork, ask the herders instead of chasing stray wagon ruts.”

## ev.ev_egrigaia_site_gap.choice_2_result

You hold the roof cord while he lets it out a little at a time. Too wide, and the warmth escapes; too narrow, and the smoke stings your eyes. Watch the fire and the wind together.
