## city.egrigaia.name

Egrigaia

## ev.egrigaia.entry.body

In Egrigaia, a weaver named Saran is hanging a length of pale camel-wool cloth while her mother checks it for dust. Her younger brother has carried it across the yard, brushing past a charcoal basket. He insists he did not touch it. His mother points to a grey mark at the edge and asks him to fetch clean water. Saran says she can wash that corner without wetting the whole bolt.

You ask who will take the cloth when it dries. Saran says a trader from a nearby route has promised to collect it, and her mother will decide whether the marked edge meets his order. The brother returns with water and asks whether the trader will be angry. ‘He will ask what happened,’ their mother says. ‘We can show him the clean edge when it is ready.’

The trader arrives early. Saran asks him to wait in the shade while she finishes. She can tell you which households weave the white wool, and her brother knows a place to rest if you stay a day.

## ev.egrigaia.site_gap.title

The Felt Tent’s Smoke Opening

## ev.egrigaia.site_gap.body

At a pasture tent, keeper Orin is loosening the roof cord so smoke from the cooking fire can leave. His wife says the opening is still too narrow. He adjusts it, then the wind shifts and smoke comes back toward her pots. Their daughter lifts the door flap a little; clean air moves in below and the smoke rises. Orin thanks her for seeing what he missed.

You ask whether the patched felt around the opening will hold. Orin’s wife points to new stitching she made after the last snowfall. Their daughter says one seam still needs another stitch, and brings the needle. The family has a guest expected for supper, so the pots must be back over the fire soon. Orin offers to hold the felt while his wife works.

A herder stopping for water knows the road toward Tenduc and can name the camps. Orin leaves the cord loose enough for you to try adjusting it while his daughter watches the smoke.

## ev.ev_egrigaia_site_gap.choice_1_result

The herder names the camps toward Tenduc and points out the valley the animals use. Orin’s daughter helps you mark the road before returning to the cooking fire.

## ev.egrigaia.site_gap.choice.interact

Help Orin adjust the smoke opening

## ev.ev_egrigaia_site_gap.choice_2_result

You hold the cord while Orin’s wife finishes the patch. His daughter checks the smoke from inside, and the family can put the cooking pots back. You remember how they set the opening to the wind.

## ev.egrigaia.whiteedge.title

A Border for Grandmother

## ev.egrigaia.whiteedge.body

At a smaller weaving tent, two sisters are deciding which border to put on a white camel-wool shawl for their grandmother. The younger one wants a bright red thread; the older says Grandmother asked for a narrow blue line. ‘She may still like red,’ the younger says. Their father, who twisted the warp, asks whether either of them remembers what Grandmother wears when she visits. Both answer at once: the old blue scarf.

You ask when they will give her the shawl. The family is meeting her at a nearby camp tomorrow. The younger sister has already woven a little red tassel and does not want to waste it. Her older sister suggests tying it to the wrapping instead. Their father asks them to hold the shawl in daylight before deciding. They find that the white wool makes both colours vivid.

A neighbour with a cart can carry the finished shawl to Grandmother’s camp today and offers 0.8 silver to anyone who helps with the delivery. The sisters can also spend the following day showing you how the border is woven. The younger one lays her red tassel beside the blue thread and smiles.

## ev.egrigaia.whiteedge.choice.deliver

Help deliver the finished shawl today

## ev.egrigaia.whiteedge.result.deliver

You ride with the neighbour and deliver the shawl. Grandmother spots the red tassel on the wrapping before she sees the blue border and knows both sisters worked on it. The neighbour pays you 0.8 silver as promised.

## ev.egrigaia.whiteedge.choice.weave

Stay another day and learn the border weave

## ev.egrigaia.whiteedge.result.weave

You stay the next day and watch the sisters finish the narrow blue line. The younger one ties her red tassel to the parcel. Their father thanks you for taking an interest in the family’s work.
