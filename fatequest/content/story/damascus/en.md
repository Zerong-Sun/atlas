## city.damascus.name

Damascus

## ev.damascus.entry.body

As you enter Damascus, Nura is wrapping a narrow-necked bottle in soft cloth. Her nephew stands beside her stall, holding half of a broken stopper and stamping his foot.

“What will we do with the bottle for Mother?” he asks. “She leaves with the caravan tomorrow.”

Nura dries the neck and finds a new stopper. “Hold it for me. Your mother wants a little of home to take to Tripoli. Once I change the stopper, she can carry it.”

Her nephew says his mother lived in Mizza as a child. Every summer she ran to the rosewater workshops to smell the petals. Nura laughs. “She used to put her nose against the door. The maker called her back so she wouldn't burn herself.”

Nura presses in the new stopper and turns the bottle upside down. The cloth stays dry. She sees you beside the stall and asks whether you would like to rest. Her nephew has already picked up the basket to take the bottle to his mother.

## ev.damascus.entry.title

Damascus

## ev.ev_damascus_entry.choice_1_result

You ask Nura the way out of the city. She points to the gate beyond the market and tells her nephew to take the bottle first. “Walk, please. You've got glass in your hands.” You pass through with the other travellers and mark Damascus on your map.

## ev.ev_damascus_entry.choice_2_result

You help Nura wrap the bottle again, then accompany her nephew to his home. His mother opens the door in her cooking apron, smells the rosewater, and laughs that Nura has been sparing with it once more.

You stay in Damascus another day. That evening his mother tells you where the caravan for Tripoli meets and draws the road it takes out of town. You add it to your map.

## ev.gap.damascus.scene.title

The Third Sound at the Coppersmith's

## ev.gap.damascus.scene.body

Amin hangs a water jug on the rack and taps it twice. The first note rings clearly. The second is as dull as a knock on wood. His daughter puts down her polishing cloth. “The base again, Father?”

Amin nods. The jug is for Aziza, who sells water across the street. She has used her old one for more than ten years, and its spout leaks onto her sleeve whenever she serves a customer. His daughter holds the old jug beside the new one. “I'll narrow this part a little more. She likes to hold the jug in one hand and take payment with the other.”

Amin hands her the new jug to narrow the spout and chooses a handle from the rack. Someone calls out apricots for sale outside. Aziza asks across the street if the jug will be ready today. “It will!” Amin calls. “Send your daughter this afternoon. I still have to fit the handle.”

## ev.gap.damascus.scene.choice.talk

Stay to hear the repaired jug ring

## ev.gap.damascus.scene.choice.route

Ask Amin which caravan goes to Acre

## ev.gap.damascus.scene.result.talk

His daughter finishes the spout. Amin smooths the base, fits the handle, and taps the jug again. He listens, smiles, and hands it to you. “That's the sound. Give it a gentle tap.”

It rings clearly. Aziza's daughter arrives to collect it, and Amin pours a cup of water so she can see how the new spout works. Every drop lands in the cup. She carries the jug back across the street, and you mark the shop on your map.

## ev.gap.damascus.scene.result.route

Amin sets down his hammer and points to a covered cart outside. “They take copper goods to Acre. The driver is Hassan. Once I've fitted the handle, I'll come over with you and ask when he leaves.”

His daughter finishes the spout. Amin smooths the base, fits the handle, and pours half a cup of water to test it. Then he takes you to Hassan. Hassan is feeding the mules. He still has several crates to collect for his next journey and tells you to find him beside the shop when they are ready. You mark Acre and its road on your map. Amin carries the new jug over to Aziza.

## ev.damascus.dyework.title

A Piece of Blue Cloth

## ev.damascus.dyework.body

Two lengths of blue cloth hang at the dyer's door. His apprentice, Layla, begins to roll one up, but a woman stops her. “Wait. My mother stitched a little red thread into the edge.”

Layla searches and cannot find it. Rain sent both customers' cloth indoors last night. In the morning the marks were hidden under the new blue dye. The woman, Samiya, brought a length her mother had kept. She plans to make a new coat for her younger brother when he returns from his travels. As a boy, he wore a robe their mother had sewn, and Samiya wants to keep that little stitch in his new coat.

The master comes over from the dye vat and feels the corner. “The thread is still there. When the cloth dries a little, we'll all look.” Samiya puts her sewing box on a stool and helps Layla work along the hem. Layla looks up at you. “You have good eyes. Will you check the other side? I'd hate to send her home empty handed.”

## ev.damascus.dyework.choice.help

Help Layla look for the red thread

## ev.damascus.dyework.choice.route

Ask how Samiya's brother travels back from Tyre

## ev.damascus.dyework.result.help

You spread the hem beside the window. In the light you see a red thread tucked into a small repair. Samiya lifts the loose end with her nail and recognizes her mother's stitch.

Layla brings her some water and asks her to return once the cloth is fully dry. The master thanks you for helping. On her way out, Samiya laughs that her brother will probably complain that the sleeves are too long. You leave knowing a few more people in Damascus.

## ev.damascus.dyework.result.route

As she searches the cloth, Samiya tells you her brother travels with a caravan from Tyre. She knows the stretch along the coast and the turn inland toward Damascus. She even describes the green-painted cart he rides beside.

Layla finds the red thread and calls her over. Samiya holds the corner up to the light. “When he comes home, I'll make him try the coat on first.” You thank her and mark Tyre and the road on your map.
