## city.ninive.name

Ninive

## ev.ninive.entry.title

Across from Nineveh

## ev.ninive.entry.body

Mosul’s houses stand beside the Tigris, with the old mounds of Nineveh across the water. At the ferry, a father tries to persuade his daughter to let go of a sack of grain long enough to get into the boat.

‘It’s for Aunt Laila,’ she tells you. ‘Last month she sent us a bowl and told us to fill it when the harvest came.’ Her father takes one handle and lets her keep the other. Around them, travellers are finding space for their bundles and deciding which bank they need.

The ferryman calls for people bound across to wait until the animals are aboard. Mariam asks her father whether Aunt Laila will see them from the bank. ‘She always comes early when she knows we are bringing grain,’ he says. You ask if there is room for one more traveller. He glances at the boatman, who holds up two fingers for the places left. Mariam tries to shift the sack without dropping it. Her father tells her to set it on the dry plank first and keep her hand on the tied end. She does, then checks twice that the knot is secure.

## ev.ninive.entry.choice.pass

Continue toward the ferry

## ev.ninive.entry.choice.rest

Spend a day resting by the river

## ev.ev_ninive_entry.choice_1_result

The girl makes room for you on the path. You mark Mosul and Nineveh on your map before joining the other travellers.

## ev.ev_ninive_entry.choice_2_result

You rest a full day in the shade above the landing. By morning you feel stronger. The father shows you where to mark the town and its opposite bank on your map.

## ev.gap.ninive.scene.title

A Bowl for Laila

## ev.gap.ninive.scene.body

The grain sack has a hole near the seam. Laila’s niece, Mariam, is trying to cover it with her hand while her father finds a needle. Across the river, an older woman waves from beside the landing.

‘That’s her,’ Mariam says. ‘If she comes over to help, she’ll tell me the bowl was big enough without this whole sack.’ Her father stitches the seam while you steady the load. He knows the roads south; Mariam wants him to finish before the next boat leaves.

You ask whether the grain is for Laila alone. Mariam says her aunt usually saves a portion for the neighbour who helps her carry water. Her father says the bag is heavy because Mariam wanted to include enough for that household too. Across the water, Laila lifts the empty bowl for them to see. Mariam calls that they are coming; the boatman tells her to save her breath and keep hold of the sack. She grins at him, then asks her father to make the last stitch a tight one. He tests it with a short pull before he stands.

## ev.gap.ninive.scene.choice.talk

Hold the sack while he sews

## ev.gap.ninive.scene.choice.route

Ask him about the road to Baghdad

## ev.gap.ninive.scene.result.talk

The last stitch holds. Mariam carries the sack to the boat with her father and waves to her aunt. On the opposite bank, Laila is already holding out the bowl. You mark the ferry and Mosul on your map.

## ev.gap.ninive.scene.result.route

He points south along the river and names the stopping places he uses on the way to Baghdad. You mark the road and the city. Mariam calls him back: the seam is done and the boat is leaving.

## ev.ninive.ferrybowl.title

The Bowl Comes Back

## ev.ninive.ferrybowl.body

When the ferry returns, Mariam is carrying Laila’s bowl. It is full of roasted grain. ‘She said I brought enough for her neighbours too,’ Mariam says. ‘Then she filled this so we’d have something for the way home.’

Her father offers to show you the quieter path toward Tabriz after he puts the boat in order. Mariam is happy to share the roasted grain now if you would rather sit with her on the bank.

You ask whether Laila kept the bowl full while they visited. Mariam says her aunt emptied it into smaller cups for the neighbours, then refilled it from the pan before they left. ‘She never lets a dish go home empty,’ her father says. Mariam asks him if he told Laila about the torn sack. He nods; Laila sent back a stronger needle and told him to keep it with the ferry gear. The boatman calls for the next crossing, and her father rises to help move a bundle. Mariam rests the warm bowl on her knees and waits until he has the boat settled.

## ev.ninive.ferrybowl.choice.path

Walk with her father to the Tabriz road

## ev.ninive.ferrybowl.choice.eat

Sit with Mariam and share the grain

## ev.ninive.ferrybowl.result.path

You walk to the turning with her father. He names the next places to stop and waits until you have marked the route toward Tabriz and the city beyond it on your map. Then he turns back toward the boat.

## ev.ninive.ferrybowl.result.eat

Mariam pours grain into your palm and tells you how Laila roasts it in a shallow pan. You eat slowly while the ferry crosses again. The rest leaves you with more strength for the road.
