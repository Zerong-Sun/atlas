## div.jiaobei.result.00

Great fortune: the cups grant leave to ask the road of the next town before you choose.

## div.jiaobei.result.01

The cups grant leave to hire a guide for the mountain pass.

## div.jiaobei.result.02

The cups grant leave to wait one tide before boarding.

## div.jiaobei.result.03

The cups grant leave to buy grain here; the next market will be dearer.

## div.jiaobei.result.04

The cups grant leave to speak first at the gate, and plainly.

## div.jiaobei.result.05

The cups grant leave to take the inland road; the shore road is watched.

## div.jiaobei.result.06

The cups grant leave to join the caravan that leaves at dawn.

## div.jiaobei.result.07

The cups grant leave to lodge within walls tonight.

## div.jiaobei.result.08

The cups grant leave to change silver before the frontier.

## div.jiaobei.result.09

The cups grant leave to ask three prices before you buy.

## div.jiaobei.result.10

Great ill omen: the cups refuse the crossing today; seek another ford tomorrow.

## div.jiaobei.result.11

The cups refuse night travel; wait for the second watch of morning.

## div.jiaobei.result.12

The cups refuse a bargain struck in haste at this stall.

## div.jiaobei.result.13

The cups refuse the short desert cut; keep to the wells.

## div.jiaobei.result.14

The cups refuse to open a sealed letter on the road.

## div.jiaobei.result.15

The cups refuse sailing under this wind; another day.

## div.jiaobei.result.16

The cups refuse hiring the loudest guide in the square.

## div.jiaobei.result.17

The cups refuse camping without a wall in sight.

## div.jiaobei.result.18

The cups refuse paying the first toll named to you.

## div.jiaobei.result.19

The cups refuse parting from the company you already have.

## div.jiaobei.result.20

The cups laugh: ask again after you have counted your water-skins.

## div.jiaobei.result.21

The cups laugh: ask again when the dust settles and you can see the posts.

## div.jiaobei.result.22

The cups laugh: ask again once you have spoken to a local scribe.

## div.jiaobei.result.23

The cups laugh: ask again after noon prayer when the bazaar thins.

## div.jiaobei.result.24

The cups laugh: ask again with a clearer question of one road only.

## div.jiaobei.result.25

The cups laugh: ask again after you sleep one night in this city.

## div.jiaobei.result.26

The cups laugh: ask again when your companion is sober.

## div.jiaobei.result.27

The cups laugh: ask again beside the temple threshold, not in the street.

## div.jiaobei.result.28

The cups laugh: ask again after you have named the city you mean.

## div.jiaobei.result.29

The cups laugh: ask again; this cast has nothing yet to say.

## div.jiaobei.name

Jiaobei
