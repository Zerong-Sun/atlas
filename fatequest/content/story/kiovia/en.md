## ev.kiovia.entry.body

Kiev — the mother of Russian cities, now a tributary of the Golden Horde. Its golden
domes still shine above the Dnieper, though the Mongol sack of a generation ago is still
visible in the ruined quarters. The Tatar baskak collects the taxes; the river trade has
slowed, but furs, wax, and honey still find their way south to the Black Sea ports.
