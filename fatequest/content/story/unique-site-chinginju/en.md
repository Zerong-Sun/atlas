## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.title

The Morning Market Meal

## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.body

A market cook has prepared a pot of rice for people arriving with the early carts. Her nephew is counting bowls, but he has included the empty ones still waiting to be washed. ‘We will run out before the last cart,’ he says. She makes him turn each bowl upright as he counts again. A hunter delivering provisions stops to ask whether he should leave his dried food with the cook or sell it to travellers directly. She says the travellers may need something that lasts beyond today’s meal. You ask a newly arrived family what they have eaten on the road. The mother says they still have dried food but would welcome hot rice now. The nephew sets out the first clean bowls and asks the family to sit. The hunter opens a wrapped packet to show how it is kept dry. The cook needs help serving the later arrivals through the day; the hunter is ready to sell you a well-packed road ration if you plan to leave.

## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.choice_a

Spend a day serving new arrivals

## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.choice_a_result

You stay another day, bringing clean bowls to travellers as the carts arrive. The cook’s nephew learns to count only the bowls ready to use. Families at the market thank you by name, and the cook lets you sit with the last arrivals after the rush.

## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.choice_b

Buy a packed road ration

## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.choice_b_result

You pay the hunter for a portion of food he has packed for travel. He shows you how to keep its wrapper dry and offers you a taste before tying it shut. You eat enough to regain strength and carry the rest for the road.
