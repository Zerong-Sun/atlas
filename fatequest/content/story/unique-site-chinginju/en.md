## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.title

The Market at First Light

## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.body

At Chinginju, carts bring game and silk to a crowded market. A stall keeper asks for help sharing food with newly arrived travellers. A hunter offers a prepared meal for the road.

## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.choice_a

Help share food with arrivals

## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.choice_a_result

You spend a day carrying food to the new arrivals. People in the market know your face, and you learn to meet strangers with care.

## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.choice_b

Buy a prepared road meal

## ev.ev_unique_chinginju.choice_b_result

You pay 240 silver. The hunter packs a hearty meal and shows how to keep it dry, giving you strength for the next stage.
