## city.caracoron.name

Caracoron

## ev.caracoron.entry.body

Caracoron is a city of some three miles in compass. [It is surrounded by a strong
earthen rampart, for stone is scarce there. And beside it there is a great citadel
wherein is a fine palace in which the Governor resides.] ’Tis the first city that the
Tartars possessed after they issued from their own country. And now I will tell you all
about how they first acquired dominion and spread over the world. Originally the Tartars
dwelt in the north on the borders of CHORCHA.

## ev.caracoron.site_gap.title

Horsehair Rope Outside the Camp

## ev.caracoron.site_gap.body

Outside a tent, a groom twists horsehair into a thin cord and draws it taut around a wooden peg. It can stitch a saddlebag or tie down tent cloth, he says. Horsehair tightens when wet, so leave some slack in a knot. A young horse noses at your sleeve. The groom washes grease from the hair before twisting it, so the strands grip rather than slip.

## ev.caracoron.site_gap.choice.route

Ask about the road to Camul

## ev.ev_caracoron_site_gap.choice_1_result

The groom points south across the grassland. “This road leads to Camul, but it is a long way. Go with a pack train that knows where to find water and pasture.”

## ev.caracoron.site_gap.choice.interact

Try twisting a length of rope

## ev.ev_caracoron_site_gap.choice_2_result

He gives you a few strands. “Twist them one way, then lay the two strands together in the opposite direction. That keeps the cord from unravelling. A little water on your palms helps.”
