## city.dufar.name

Dufar

## ev.dufar.entry.body

Dufar is a great and noble and fine city, and lies 500 miles to the north-west of Esher.
The people are Saracens, and have a Count for their chief, who is subject to the Soldan
of Aden; for this city still belongs to the Province of Aden. It stands upon the sea and
has a very good haven, so that there is a great traffic of shipping between this and
India; and the merchants take hence great numbers of Arab horses to that market, making
great profits thereby. This city has under it many other towns and villages. Much white
incense is produced here, and I will tell you how it grows.

## ev.dufar.a.title

Dufar: The Notched Incense Trees

## ev.dufar.a.body

In the great and noble city on the sea with a very good haven, the incense men of Dufar work the trees that grow like small firs, notching them with a knife in several places so that the white incense runs out of the wounds. A master of the notching says the city belongs to the province of Aden and its count is subject to the Soldan, and that great numbers of Arab horses are taken hence to the Indian market — but the incense is the city's own voice, and it asks you whether you will listen.

## ev.dufar.a.choice.1

Buy white incense at the notcher's price

## ev.dufar.a.choice.2

Ask the notcher the sea road toward Esher

## ev.dufar.a.choice.3

Watch the notching a day and learn the trees' season

## ev.ev_dufar_a_followup.title

Dufar: The Notcher's Second Word on the Wound

## ev.ev_dufar_a_followup.body

The notcher takes you among the trees and shows you the old scars. He says the tree gives its incense only through the wound, and that the wound must be made in the right season or the tree gives nothing — the notching is a covenant, not a violence. He says the city's trade in horses and ships is the same: profit comes where the measure is kept. He asks whether you will take the incense and keep the measure.

## ev.ev_dufar_a_followup.choice_1

Buy myrrh at the notcher's second price

## ev.ev_dufar_a_followup.choice_1_result

He sells you myrrh at a fair price. The resin is sealed and ready for the sea road.

## ev.ev_dufar_a_followup.choice_2

## ev.ev_dufar_a_followup.choice_2_result
