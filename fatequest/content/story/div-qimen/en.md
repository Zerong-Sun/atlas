## div.qimen.result.00

The Door of Life opens at the first watch: depart before the rooster crows.

## div.qimen.result.01

The Door of Rest holds the second day: linger and mend your gear.

## div.qimen.result.02

The Heavenly Stem reaches the gate: a senior will speak; heed the words.

## div.qimen.result.03

A hidden gate in the northwest: a shortcut exists, but only at dawn.

## div.qimen.result.04

The Earth Door is shut: the road is watched; take the market road instead.

## div.qimen.result.05

The Door of Sickness shows in the chart: keep the invalid's goods off your cart.

## div.qimen.result.06

A white bird crosses the gate: a message arrives by courier this week.

## div.qimen.result.07

The Five Ghosts drift toward the road: travel with bells to keep them away.

## div.qimen.result.08

The Gate of Fortune opens at the third watch: buy your goods before the noon crowd.

## div.qimen.result.09

A flying star rests on the crossroads: ask twice before you choose the fork.

## div.qimen.result.10

The Door of Death lies on the south road: do not go that way for seven days.

## div.qimen.result.11

A mystic gate appears at the hour of the horse: what is lost will be found.

## div.qimen.result.12

The Heavenly Secret is visible: keep your route quiet until you pass the custom post.

## div.qimen.result.13

A red cloud covers the gate: a storm delays you; shelter in the caravanserai.

## div.qimen.result.14

The Gate of Long Life opens at the fifth watch: a trusted companion brings good news.

## div.qimen.result.15

The Nine Palaces turn: your plan is sound but the timing is off — wait a day.

## div.qimen.result.16

A locked gate in the chart: a document is needed; ask for it at the citadel.

## div.qimen.result.17

The stars form a bridge: a safe crossing lies upstream; seek it.

## div.qimen.result.18

A gate ajar to the east: a trading partner waits; go and settle the price.

## div.qimen.result.19

The Heavenly Sword points home: return by the way you came; it is safest.

## div.qimen.result.20

A low gate at noon: do not negotiate while the sun is high.

## div.qimen.result.21

The Door of Industry opens: a craft will earn your passage at the next town.

## div.qimen.result.22

A wandering star enters the gate: news of a lost friend reaches you here.

## div.qimen.result.23

The Gate of Slaughter is quiet: what you feared is past; go in peace.

## div.qimen.result.24

A threefold gate: three ways are open; the middle holds your fortune.

## div.qimen.result.25

The Earth moves under the gate: a delay on the road, but no harm.

## div.qimen.result.26

A gate lit by fire: a quarrel over coin; keep your ledger ready.

## div.qimen.result.27

The Door of Justice opens at dusk: a dispute over goods will end in your favor.

## div.qimen.result.28

A banner before the gate: join the caravan bound for the eastern city.

## div.qimen.result.29

The gate stands open at midnight: a secret errand may be done unnoticed.
