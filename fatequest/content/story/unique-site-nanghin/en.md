## ev.ev_unique_nanghin.title

The Silk Duty Table

## ev.ev_unique_nanghin.body

In Nanghin, silk and gold cloth pass a table where duty is collected. A clerk will teach you to reckon the rolls if you stay. Or you may pay for a sealed passage note.

## ev.ev_unique_nanghin.choice_a

Study the duty ledger for a day

## ev.ev_unique_nanghin.choice_a_result

You spend a day matching each roll to its mark. You earn no wage, but learn how duties change a merchant’s margin.

## ev.ev_unique_nanghin.choice_b

Pay for the sealed passage note

## ev.ev_unique_nanghin.choice_b_result

You pay 230 silver. The clerk seals your note, and merchants at the gate treat you as a known traveller.
