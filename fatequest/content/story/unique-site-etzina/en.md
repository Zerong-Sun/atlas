## ev.ev_unique_etzina.title

The Camel Yard

## ev.ev_unique_etzina.body

Outside Etzina, camels drink before the road reaches the sandy desert. A herder asks you to mend the straps on his waterskins. A driver offers to show the next known stopping place before he leaves.

## ev.ev_unique_etzina.choice_a

Mend the waterskins and fill your own

## ev.ev_unique_etzina.choice_a_result

You spend a day checking seams and filling skins. You recover strength for the desert and learn to prepare the journey.

## ev.ev_unique_etzina.choice_b

Pay the driver to mark the next stop

## ev.ev_unique_etzina.choice_b_result

You pay 160 silver. The driver marks the known road toward Egrigaia and tells you where to keep water close.
