## ev.ev_unique_etzina.title

The Camel’s Loose Waterskin Strap

## ev.ev_unique_etzina.body

Outside Etzina, a camel has finished drinking and is tugging at the strap that holds two waterskins. The strap has worn thin where it rubs against the pack frame. Its keeper asks her brother to bring a softer length from the store tent. He returns with rope that is sound but too short. ‘We can join it,’ he says. She makes him measure the distance first, including the turn around the frame. You hold one waterskin so it does not swing while they fit the new piece. A caravan driver waiting beside them says he will leave for Egrigaia once every animal has water. He can show you the first resting place for a small guiding fee. The keeper would rather have someone help her spend tomorrow checking the remaining straps and filling the skins; she promises a meal and a place to rest. Her brother draws the knot tight and asks whether the skin hangs clear of the camel’s side.

## ev.ev_unique_etzina.choice_a

Stay a day to check the animals and water

## ev.ev_unique_etzina.choice_a_result

You spend another day helping the keeper test straps and fill the skins. She serves you a meal under the store tent and lets you rest before the next journey. Her brother has learned to allow for the full turn around the frame, and you leave with stronger legs and a better sense of desert preparations.

## ev.ev_unique_etzina.choice_b

Pay the driver for the Egrigaia road

## ev.ev_unique_etzina.choice_b_result

You pay the driver for his guidance. He marks the road toward Egrigaia and the first safe place to stop, then walks you past the outer pens so you can see where the track begins. The keeper calls after him to check his own waterskins.
