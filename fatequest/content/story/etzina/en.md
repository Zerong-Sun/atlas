## city.etzina.name

Etzina

## ev.etzina.entry.body

When you leave the city of Campichu you ride for twelve days, and then reach a city
called ETZINA, which is towards the north on the verge of the Sandy Desert; it belongs
to the Province of Tangut. The people are Idolaters, and possess plenty of camels and
cattle, and the country produces a number of good falcons, both Sakers and Lanners. The
inhabitants live by their cultivation and their cattle, for they have no trade.
