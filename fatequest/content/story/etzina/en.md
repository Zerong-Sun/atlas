## city.etzina.name

Etzina

## ev.etzina.entry.body

At Etzina’s well, a herder is asking two caravan drivers to leave room for a woman with smaller water jars. One driver has lined up all his camels by the trough. ‘They drink in order,’ he says. The herder points to the woman, who has already waited while the animals took their first round. The driver leads the nearest camel aside, and the woman fills her jars before the trough is topped up again. Her grandson wants to pour from the well too quickly and splashes one jar. ‘Leave a hand’s width at the top,’ she tells him. You help set the full jars on a flat stone. You ask where travellers find water beyond the settlement. The herder names the next stopping places he knows and asks which way you plan to go; he will not guess for a route he has not taken. The second driver says he knows the road through the market if you are leaving today. The woman offers to walk you through town tomorrow after she has delivered the jars.

## ev.etzina.entry.choice.look

Spend a day learning the wells and lanes

## ev.etzina.entry.choice.pass

Pass through the settlement today

## ev.etzina.entry.title

The Water Queue at Etzina

## ev.ev_etzina_entry.choice_1_result

The driver moves his camels clear of the well while the woman’s grandson steadies her jars. He points you through the market toward the outer track. You cross Etzina knowing where to refill if you return.

## ev.ev_etzina_entry.choice_2_result

You stay another day. The woman brings you past the wells her family uses and introduces you to the herders who know the nearby tracks. Her grandson has learned to leave space at the top of each jar, and he shows you the same mark before you leave.

## ev.ev_gap_etzina.title

The Waterskin beside the Well

## ev.ev_gap_etzina.body

Beside a well at Etzina, a traveller is hanging a washed waterskin to dry. His daughter has tied its mouth shut, thinking that will keep dust out. ‘The inside needs air first,’ he says, loosening the cord. She holds it up and finds a small damp patch near the seam. You ask whether the skin leaks. He fills it with a little clean water, waits, and shows her the drop forming underneath. The daughter wants to mend it before their companions return with the animals. A woman filling jars lends her a needle and says she can use a scrap of soft leather from her own repair bundle. The traveller asks which path leads toward Egrigaia; the woman’s brother has come from that direction and knows the turns. He will be back at the well soon. The daughter spreads the waterskin on a dry stone. ‘Hold this seam open for me,’ she says. Her father asks how far the patch should cover the old stitching. ‘Past the last damp mark,’ she answers, measuring it with her thumb before she makes the first stitch.

## ev.ev_gap_etzina.choice.route

Ask the woman’s brother about Egrigaia

## ev.ev_gap_etzina.choice.route_result

The woman’s brother returns and marks the road toward Egrigaia on your board, naming the first place his party found water. The daughter repeats the direction to her father while the patch dries. You leave with the route clearly set down.

## ev.ev_gap_etzina.choice.scene

Help mend and test the waterskin

## ev.ev_gap_etzina.choice.scene_result

You hold the seam taut while the daughter sews the soft patch. Her father adds a little water and waits; no new drop appears. The woman takes back her needle and tells the girl where she found a good piece of leather.

## ev.etzina.falcon-hood.title

A Hood for the Young Falcon

## ev.etzina.falcon-hood.body

A falconer is sewing a soft hood for a young bird. His granddaughter holds the leather, but she keeps cutting the opening wider because she fears it will press against the bird’s eyes. ‘Try it against this wooden form first,’ he says. She does, and sees the hood will slip off if she cuts more. You ask whether the falcon is ready to wear it. ‘For a short moment, after it has eaten,’ the grandfather says. The girl brings a small dish of meat and waits while the bird settles on its perch. A neighbour arrives to borrow a needle. The girl has one to spare but wants to finish the seam before lending it. Her grandfather tells her to thread both needles now. ‘Will this one do for your neighbour?’ he asks. ‘It is the stronger needle,’ she says, handing it over. The neighbour stays by the door to see how she finishes her own seam. She offers you the hood to hold as she makes the last stitches. She also asks you to come back tomorrow: she wants someone else to see whether the bird stays calm during its first short fitting.

## ev.etzina.falcon-hood.choice.stitch

Help finish the falcon’s hood

## ev.etzina.falcon-hood.result.stitch

You hold the leather against the wooden form while the girl finishes the seam. Her grandfather checks that the opening will sit clear of the bird’s eyes. The neighbour takes the spare needle, and the falconer thanks you for helping his granddaughter finish before dusk.

## ev.etzina.falcon-hood.choice.watch

Return a day later for the first fitting

## ev.etzina.falcon-hood.result.watch

You return the next day after the bird has eaten. The girl fits the hood for a brief moment, watches its breathing, and removes it when her grandfather asks. He lets her name what went well before offering a correction. You leave with a new respect for the care involved in keeping the bird calm.
