## arch.polo.name

A Venetian Merchant

## arch.polo.description

The merchants of Tabriz are packing their wares for the road east. You feel for the letter of introduction inside your coat and listen for news of the Khan's court. Venice lies far behind you. Before setting out, you need a place in a caravan—and someone whose directions you can trust.

## arch.battuta.description

The sea wind catches your robe outside Alexandria. You carry a letter of introduction and a little money; Mecca is still a long way ahead. With no familiar face beside you, you listen for the call to prayer and ask where a stranger might find a meal, a bed, and company for the road.
