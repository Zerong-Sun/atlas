## arch.polo.name

A Venetian Merchant

## arch.polo.description

In a caravan yard at Tabriz, merchants are tying their goods to the mules. You take out your letter of introduction to find a driver willing to take you east. The wind lifts a corner of the letter towards a nearby brazier.

An older man gathering rope holds it down for you. "Looking for the eastbound carts? Let me tie this crate and I'll take you to ask." He tightens the last knot and asks you to push the crate. It stays firm. He gives the rope end to his apprentice and leads you through the yard.

You read the name on your letter to him. He knows the person and says a cart leaves today. Someone at the gate calls for the travellers to be counted. The man tells you to put the letter away and keep close.

## arch.battuta.description

Outside Alexandria, the sea wind presses your letter of introduction against your hand. You fold it at the gate. People ahead of you are taking turns telling the keeper where they have come from.

A traveller behind you hears you ask the person ahead about a caravan for Mecca and asks whether you too are going there. You say yes. He raises an empty waterskin. "I'll look for water when I get inside. It's a long road ahead, and I want to fill this first." When it is his turn, he gives the keeper his name, then turns to tell you to follow the street with awnings after the gate.

You put the letter back in your bag and move with the queue. The smell of bread from inside the city has reached the gate. The traveller you met is waiting by a water stall. He waves and shows you his filled waterskin.
