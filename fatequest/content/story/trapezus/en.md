## city.trapezus.name

Trebizond

## ev.trapezus.entry.choice.pass

Thank Tamar and continue through the town

## ev.trapezus.entry.choice.look

Spend a day with Tamar above the harbour

## ev.trapezus.entry.body

Trebizond stands where the mountains meet the Black Sea. Tamar has spread two wool cloaks over a low wall above the water. Her daughter Ana takes one down because the wind keeps lifting its corner. The other belongs to her younger brother Davit, who has left it outside since morning.

“He says it needs more air,” Ana tells you. Tamar says he forgot it when he went to watch boats. She has been repairing both cloaks before her children travel inland, and she wants Davit back to try his on before she finishes the hem.

Tamar asks where you are bound and points out the lane down to the boats. Ana can show you the places where travellers wait for a crossing. If you have a day, Tamar will walk with you through the streets and ask the sailors which boats have gone toward Caffa.

You ask Anna whether Davit knows which cloak is his. ‘He knows,’ she says, ‘but he will claim mine is warmer.’ Tamar calls toward the ships, and he appears carrying a small coil of rope for his pack. He tries on the cloak she kept for him; one sleeve sits too high. His mother asks him to stand still while she loosens the seam. Davit says he can wait, then points to a boatman below who has promised news of the Caffa passage. Anna reminds him to ask about the departure day as well as the fare.

## ev.trapezus.entry.title

Above the Water at Trebizond

## ev.ev_trapezus_entry.choice_1_result

You thank Tamar and move on. Davit runs up from the boats just in time to catch the cloak Ana tosses him. You mark Trebizond on your map before taking the next street.

## ev.ev_trapezus_entry.choice_2_result

You spend the day with Tamar and Ana, walking from the houses down to the water. A sailor who has crossed toward Caffa names the places where he has stopped, and Ana asks him to repeat the unfamiliar ones. You mark Trebizond, Caffa, and the sea route between them on your map. At dusk Davit returns to try his cloak; Tamar adjusts the hem while you finish your notes. You leave the next morning.

## ev.gap.trapezus.scene.title

A Patch for the Boat Sail

## ev.gap.trapezus.scene.body

At the waterfront, sailor Mikhail and his sister Iona are spreading a torn sail across clean stones. She has brought a square of cloth from home. He says the patch needs to reach farther beyond the tear, and she goes back to the bundle for a larger piece.

Their father taught both of them to sew before either was allowed near his boat. Iona says she was the better student. Mikhail says their father trusted her with the needle because she did not leave it in the sail. She finds a bigger patch and asks you to hold the corner while they sew.

They have both travelled by water toward Caffa. Iona keeps the list of stopping places because Mikhail remembers boats by the people he met rather than by where they docked.

You ask Iona whether their father ever checked the sails after teaching them to sew. ‘Every time,’ she says. ‘He pulled each patch until we thought he would rip it off himself.’ Mikhail tries the new cloth against the tear and admits the larger piece fits better. He asks his sister to hold the needle while he shifts the sail away from a puddle. She tells him to put it in the little wooden case first. The boatman waiting nearby calls that the wind may rise by evening, so a sound patch matters more than finishing at once.

## ev.gap.trapezus.scene.choice.talk

Hold the sail while Iona sews

## ev.gap.trapezus.scene.choice.route

Ask Iona to show you her Caffa stops

## ev.gap.trapezus.scene.result.talk

You keep the sail flat while Iona sews around the larger patch. Mikhail folds the finished edge and carries it back to the boat. Iona points out the waterfront sheds and the lane to the houses as you walk. You add this part of Trebizond to your map.

## ev.gap.trapezus.scene.result.route

Iona reads the stops on the way to Caffa from a folded sheet. Mikhail adds the names of people he met at two of them; she confirms the places and laughs when he swaps their order. You mark Trebizond, Caffa, and the sea route between them on your map. Then you help fold the repaired sail.

## ev.trapezus.tworoads.title

Two Roads from Tamar’s Door

## ev.trapezus.tworoads.body

Tamar has laid two travel bags by her door. Ana is going south toward Aleppo, where an aunt who taught her to weave has asked her to visit. Davit is taking their father's old spindle southeast toward Edessa; a friend there promised to repair its bent shaft.

Ana thinks Davit's bag is too heavy. He says she packed all the food into his, and empties it onto the floor to prove his point. Tamar divides the bread between them. Ana slips an extra piece back into his bag when he is not looking.

They leave by different inland roads in the morning. Tamar can walk with one child as far as the first rest place; she asks whether you will accompany the other. Both know the stopping places from family journeys, so you can choose which road to learn as you go.

You ask why Davit carries the old spindle all that way. ‘Father used it before I was born,’ he says. ‘My friend knows a turner who may straighten it.’ Anna asks whether he has written the friend’s address where he can find it. Davit pats one pocket, then another, and finally takes a folded note from the bread cloth. Tamar tells him to put it in his belt before the food stains the ink. Anna laughs, but checks her own letter to the aunt too. Their mother gives each of them a small packet of salt for the road and asks them to meet the family again when they can.

## ev.trapezus.tworoads.choice.ana

Walk one day with Ana toward Aleppo

## ev.trapezus.tworoads.choice.davit

Walk one day with Davit toward Edessa

## ev.trapezus.tworoads.result.ana

You spend the day on the road with Ana. She points out the turns her aunt taught her and names the places where the family has stayed on earlier journeys. At the first rest place she opens her bag and finds a small packet of dried fruit that Tamar slipped in beside the bread. You mark Trebizond, Aleppo, and the route between them on your map. Ana thanks you and sets off again in the morning.

## ev.trapezus.tworoads.result.davit

You spend the day with Davit on the eastern road. He shows you the places where his father once stopped with the spindle in his pack. At the first rest place he finds the extra bread Ana gave him and smiles; he saves it for breakfast. You mark Trebizond, Edessa, and the route between them on your map. Davit takes the spindle out once more to check that its shaft has survived the day.
