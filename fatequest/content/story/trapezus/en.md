## city.trapezus.name

Trapezus

## ev.trapezus.entry.body

Trebizond — the last outpost of the Byzantine Empire on the Black Sea. The Comnenus
emperors hold court in a citadel above the harbor, their purple banners flying from the
towers. Genoese and Venetian merchants compete for alum from the nearby mines, silk from
the Caucasus, and furs from the northern forests. The mountains behind the city are
impassable in winter; the sea is the only road.

## ev.trapezus.a.title

Trapezus: The Silk Port of the Black Sea

## ev.trapezus.a.body

At the port where the mountain road from the east meets the Black Sea, the merchants of Trapezus load the silk that comes down from the highlands onto the ships of the northern sea. A port master who has tallied the silk for years says the city is the sea's last stair — the road from the east ends here, and the sea road begins, and a traveller who carries silk down the mountain may board at this harbour with his cargo whole. He asks what your road brings to the water.

## ev.trapezus.a.choice.1

Buy silk at the port master's price

## ev.trapezus.a.choice.2

Ask the port master the sea road toward Tana

## ev.trapezus.a.choice.3

Watch the harbour a day and learn the silk's tally

## ev.ev_trapezus_a_followup.title

Trapezus: The Port Master's Second Word on the Stair

## ev.ev_trapezus_a_followup.body

The port master takes you to the quay's end and speaks of the mountain road behind the city. He says the silk comes down the passes as the snows allow, and the ships sail as the weather allows, and the harbour is where the two calendars meet — a traveller who reads both may load and go in the same week. He asks what your cargo is worth in days.

## ev.ev_trapezus_a_followup.choice_1

## ev.ev_trapezus_a_followup.choice_1_result

He sells you silk at a returning customer's price. The bolt is sealed for the mountain road.

## ev.ev_trapezus_a_followup.choice_2

## ev.ev_trapezus_a_followup.choice_2_result

## ev.ev_trapezus_a_followup.choice_3

## ev.ev_trapezus_a_followup.choice_3_result

You wait a day at the quay. The two calendars are learned, and in Trapezus your name now carries a little more weight.
