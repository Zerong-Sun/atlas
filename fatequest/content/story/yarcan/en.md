## city.yarcan.name

Yarcan

## ev.yarcan.entry.body

Yarcan is a province five days’ journey in extent. The people follow the Law of
Mahommet, but there are also Nestorian and Jacobite Christians. They are subject to the
same Prince that I mentioned, the Great Kaan’s nephew. They have plenty of everything,
[particularly of cotton. The inhabitants are also great craftsmen, but a large
proportion of them have swoln legs, and great crops at the throat, which arises from
some quality in their drinking-water.] As there is nothing else worth telling we may
pass on.

## ev.yarcan.site_gap.title

A Grafting Knife at the Orchard

## ev.yarcan.site_gap.body

An orchard keeper cuts a branch to a smooth sloping face. To graft it, he says, align the bark layers while the tree is growing and bind them with cloth. A dry cut will not take. Unripe fruit hangs above an irrigation channel. He keeps the cuttings in damp cloth until the afternoon cools so they do not dry too quickly.

## ev.yarcan.site_gap.choice.route

Ask about the road to Cascar

## ev.ev_yarcan_site_gap.choice_1_result

“Take this road northwest to Cascar. Stop at the next oasis; do not pass a water source just to save time.”

## ev.yarcan.site_gap.choice.interact

Watch him graft a branch

## ev.ev_yarcan_site_gap.choice_2_result

He puts two cut faces together to show where their bark meets. Bind that ring, but not so tightly that new buds are crushed. In a few days, check the bud before removing the cloth.
