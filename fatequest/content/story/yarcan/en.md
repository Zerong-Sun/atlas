## city.yarcan.name

Yarcan

## ev.yarcan.entry.body

Yarcan is a province five days’ journey in extent. The people follow the Law of
Mahommet, but there are also Nestorian and Jacobite Christians. They are subject to the
same Prince that I mentioned, the Great Kaan’s nephew. They have plenty of everything,
[particularly of cotton. The inhabitants are also great craftsmen, but a large
proportion of them have swoln legs, and great crops at the throat, which arises from
some quality in their drinking-water.] As there is nothing else worth telling we may
pass on.

## ev.yarcan.a.title

Yarcan: The Craftsmen of the Five-Day Province

## ev.yarcan.a.body

In the province five days' journey in extent, the craftsmen of Yarcan are great workers in cotton and leather, and the people follow the Law of Mahommet with Nestorians and Jacobites among them. A cotton master who keeps the craft book says the water of the country leaves its mark on many of the folk — great crops at the throat and swoln legs — and that the craftsmen carry on their work undeterred, as the water is not to be changed. He asks your business.

## ev.yarcan.a.choice.1

Buy cotton cloth at the craft-book price

## ev.yarcan.a.choice.2

Ask the cotton master the road toward Cotan

## ev.yarcan.a.choice.3

Walk the workshops a day and note the craft

## ev.ev_yarcan_a_followup.title

Yarcan: The Cotton Master's Second Page

## ev.ev_yarcan_a_followup.body

The cotton master opens his craft book at the page of the weavers' guilds. He says the city's faiths keep separate books but the same looms — Mahommet's men and the Christians work the same cotton, and the water that marks their bodies marks no difference between them. He asks whether a traveller of your book will trade with both.

## ev.ev_yarcan_a_followup.choice_1

Buy leather at the guild's joint price

## ev.ev_yarcan_a_followup.choice_1_result

He sells you leather at a fair price. The hide is well dressed and will serve on the mountain road.

## ev.ev_yarcan_a_followup.choice_2

Ask him to mark the road toward Keshimur

## ev.ev_yarcan_a_followup.choice_2_result

Keshimur is marked on your map with the passes between. The mountain road is open.

## ev.ev_yarcan_a_followup.choice_3

Sit a while in the weavers' hall and hear both books

## ev.ev_yarcan_a_followup.choice_3_result

You sit a while in the hall. Both books are heard, and in Yarcan your name now carries a little more weight.
