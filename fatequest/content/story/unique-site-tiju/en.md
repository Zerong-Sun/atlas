## ev.ev_unique_tiju.title

Salt from the Coast

## ev.ev_unique_tiju.body

At Tiju, sacks of salt arrive from the country between the city and the sea. A salt measurer needs help with a long day of lifting and checking weights. A coastal boatman sells his sailing notes.

## ev.ev_unique_tiju.choice_a

Take the heavy salt-weighing shift

## ev.ev_unique_tiju.choice_a_result

You spend a tiring day at the scales and earn 190 silver. Your strength falls, but the measurers come to know you.

## ev.ev_unique_tiju.choice_b

Buy the boatman’s sailing notes

## ev.ev_unique_tiju.choice_b_result

You pay 180 silver. His notes mark the way toward Sinju and sharpen your judgement of a salt cargo.
