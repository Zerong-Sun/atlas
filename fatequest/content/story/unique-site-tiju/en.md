## ev.ev_unique_tiju.title

The Salt Weighing Table

## ev.ev_unique_tiju.body

At Tiju’s salt weighing table, a worker named Rui has found that two sacks carry the same painted mark. Her younger brother says they were filled together, so it should not matter. Rui sets them on the scale one at a time: one weighs less because its seam leaked on the cart. ‘It matters to the person who buys it,’ she says. The brother finds spilled salt in the cart bed and asks whether it can be saved. Rui gives him a clean tray and tells him to keep the gritty pieces separate.

You ask how long a proper recount takes. Rui says a day of lifting and checking, and the yard pays 1.9 silver for someone who helps. The work is tiring. A salt hauler waiting nearby has a written land route toward Sinju and charges 1.8 silver to go over it with a traveller. Rui asks him to wait until the sacks are marked correctly, since his cart will carry one of them.

Her brother wipes the scale pan and asks you which sack should be checked next.

## ev.ev_unique_tiju.choice_a

Spend a day weighing salt with Rui’s family

## ev.ev_unique_tiju.choice_a_result

You spend a tiring day lifting and checking sacks. Rui pays you 1.9 silver for the work, and her brother can now tell the two marked bags apart. The weighing crew remembers how carefully you handled the leaking one.

## ev.ev_unique_tiju.choice_b

Pay 1.8 silver for the salt hauler’s land-road notes

## ev.ev_unique_tiju.choice_b_result

You pay 1.8 silver. The hauler marks the road toward Sinju and tells you where he checks the load after leaving Tiju. Rui’s brother copies the road turn while waiting for his sack to be loaded.
