## codex.cx-nanghin.name

Nanghin (Anqing)

## codex.cx-nanghin.body

The traveller describes a wealthy province west of Yangiu, rich in silk and woven cloth. Merchants pay duties on the goods they buy and sell.

## ev.nanghin.entry.body

Nanghin is a very noble Province towards the west. The people are Idolaters (and so
forth) and live by trade and manufactures. They have silk in great abundance, and they
weave many fine tissues of silk and gold. They have all sorts of corn and victuals very
cheap, for the province is a most productive one. Game also is abundant, and lions too
are found there. The merchants are great and opulent, and the Emperor draws a large
revenue from them, in the shape of duties on the goods which they buy and sell.

## ev.ev_gap_nanghin.title

A Bamboo Whistle at the Gate

## ev.ev_gap_nanghin.body

At Nanghin’s gate, a guard blows a short whistle and a distant cart stops. One call asks people to slow; two ask for passage through the crowded entrance. He lets you try, but warns you not to blow near a horse’s face: a startled horse can kick. Watch its ears first.
