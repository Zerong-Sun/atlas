## city.nanghin.name

Nanghin (Anqing)

## codex.cx-nanghin.name

Nanghin (Anqing)

## codex.cx-nanghin.body

The traveller describes a wealthy province west of Yangiu, rich in silk and woven cloth. Merchants pay duties on the goods they buy and sell.

## ev.nanghin.entry.body

At a silk shop in Nanghin, weaver Fang is teaching his niece to enter a sale in three columns: the thread, the finished cloth, and the charge paid when it changes hands. She has written the charge twice. ‘I thought it belonged to both buyers,’ she says. Fang asks her who actually paid it. She looks at the receipt, rubs out one line, and asks why the cloth travels farther than the thread. He says the next trader will decide that.

You ask where a traveller can leave the road and enter town. Fang’s neighbour points to the gate street, where carts are waiting their turn. He invites you to watch the workshop for a day; the niece wants another pair of eyes on her ledger. A woman from the dye yard arrives with a length of blue silk and asks whether its price includes the cost of dye. Fang lets his niece answer. She checks the first column before speaking.

The neighbour returns to his cart, calling to a child to keep the cloth well above the wheel.

## ev.nanghin.entry.title

The Weaver’s Three Columns

## ev.ev_nanghin_entry.choice_1_result

You take the gate street into Nanghin. Fang’s niece calls the dye worker back to check one more number, and the neighbour’s cart clears the doorway.

## ev.ev_nanghin_entry.choice_2_result

You spend a day comparing Fang’s receipts with his niece’s columns. By evening she can explain why the dye has its own cost and why the charge appears once. Fang lets her close the book herself.

## ev.ev_gap_nanghin.title

The Gate Keeper’s Short Whistle

## ev.ev_gap_nanghin.body

At the gate street, keeper Wu is directing carts through a narrow turn. His daughter has taken the whistle while he checks a loose wheel on a neighbour’s cart. She blows once, and the driver slows. Another cart appears behind him; she starts to blow twice, then stops because a horse has lifted its ears. Wu looks up and signals with his hand instead.

You ask why the two carts cannot pass together. The paving narrows beside an old wall, he says. His daughter knows the road from Nanghin toward Caiju because her aunt carries cloth that way. She asks whether you want the turns drawn on paper. Wu says she should finish this cart first, then show you where the road leaves town.

The neighbour’s wheel is secure, but the driver worries the load may still lean. Wu asks his daughter to walk beside the cart for a few steps. She hands you the whistle to hold while she goes, and the horse settles as the cart turns.

## ev.ev_gap_nanghin.choice.route

Ask the daughter about the land road toward Caiju

## ev.ev_gap_nanghin.choice.route_result

After the cart passes, Wu’s daughter draws the road toward Caiju, including the narrow turn outside town where her aunt stops. You mark the route on your map while Wu returns the whistle to her.

## ev.ev_gap_nanghin.choice.scene

Help the family guide the loaded cart

## ev.ev_gap_nanghin.choice.scene_result

You stand where the second driver can see you and signal him to wait. Wu’s daughter walks the first cart through; her father checks the wheel once more. The neighbour thanks you for making space.

## ev.nanghin.bluehem.title

The Blue Silk Hem

## ev.nanghin.bluehem.body

At the dye yard, the woman who visited Fang’s shop has laid out a length of blue silk for a neighbour’s wedding garment. Her younger sister says the hem should be darker; the bride asked for the colour of the river at dusk. The woman reminds her that they have only one small bowl of dye left. ‘Then we should test a thread first,’ the sister says. She dips a loose end, holds it to the light, and both women agree it is close.

You ask when the garment must be ready. The bride’s mother is coming tomorrow to collect the cloth, and the sisters still need to dry it without creasing. A child from Fang’s shop arrives with the corrected receipt; the dye charge is now written clearly. The elder sister thanks him and asks him not to step on the wet edge. He freezes, one foot in the air. The younger sister laughs and guides him to the dry path. They need someone to hold the cloth while they adjust the drying line.

‘I can hold that end,’ the child says. The younger sister checks his hands first and gives him the dry corner.

## ev.nanghin.bluehem.choice.hold

Hold the silk while the sisters move the drying line

## ev.nanghin.bluehem.choice.day

Spend a day helping finish the garment hem

## ev.nanghin.bluehem.result.hold

You hold the silk clear of the ground while the sisters shift the line. The child carries the corrected receipt to the bride’s mother. When the cloth is safely hung, the elder sister thanks you by name.

## ev.nanghin.bluehem.result.day

You stay a day and help dry, cut and fold the finished hem. The younger sister shows the bride’s mother the test thread before the cloth is taken away. She likes the dusk-blue colour and thanks the whole yard.
