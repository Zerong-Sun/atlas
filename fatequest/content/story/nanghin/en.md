## city.nanghin.name

Nanghin

## ev.nanghin.entry.body

Nanghin is a very noble Province towards the west. The people are Idolaters (and so
forth) and live by trade and manufactures. They have silk in great abundance, and they
weave many fine tissues of silk and gold. They have all sorts of corn and victuals very
cheap, for the province is a most productive one. Game also is abundant, and lions too
are found there. The merchants are great and opulent, and the Emperor draws a large
revenue from them, in the shape of duties on the goods which they buy and sell.

## ev.nanghin.a.title

Nanghin: The Opulent Merchants of the Noble Province

## ev.nanghin.a.body

In the very noble province towards the west, the merchants of Nanghin are great and opulent, and the Emperor draws a large revenue from the duties on their goods. A silk merchant who keeps three warehouses says the province has silk in great abundance, and weaves many fine tissues of silk and gold; corn and victuals are cheap, game abundant, and lions are found there — and a traveller who comes to this city with empty hands leaves with a full book.

## ev.nanghin.a.choice.1

Buy silk-and-gold at the merchant's price

## ev.nanghin.a.choice.2

Ask the merchant the road toward Caiju

## ev.nanghin.a.choice.3

Walk the warehouses a day and note the trade

## ev.ev_nanghin_a_followup.title

Nanghin: The Merchant's Second Warehouse

## ev.ev_nanghin_a_followup.body

The merchant opens the door of his second warehouse, where the duties of the province are written on a board. He says the Emperor's revenue from this city alone would buy a kingdom, and that the silk and the gold thread pass through here to every road of Manzi. He asks whether you would carry the cloth, or the knowledge of where it goes.

## ev.ev_nanghin_a_followup.choice_1

Buy lacquerware at the warehouse price

## ev.ev_nanghin_a_followup.choice_1_result

He sells you lacquerware at a fair price. The piece is sealed for the canal road.

## ev.ev_nanghin_a_followup.choice_2

Ask him to mark the road toward Chinghianfu

## ev.ev_nanghin_a_followup.choice_2_result

Chinghianfu is marked on your map with the days between. The province is knitting together.

## ev.ev_nanghin_a_followup.choice_3

Sit a while among the bales and hear the trade's tides

## ev.ev_nanghin_a_followup.choice_3_result

You sit a while among the bales. The trade's tides are learned, and in Nanghin your name now carries a little more weight.
