## city.tana-azov.name

Tana (Azov)

## ev.tana-azov.entry.body

Tana — the Venetian trading post at the mouth of the Don, where the river empties into
the Sea of Azov. From here the overland route to Sarai and the Volga begins; the journey
takes twelve days by camel through the Cuman steppe. Slaves, wax, dried fish, and
Russian furs are the principal cargoes. The Genoese at Caffa watch the same trade with
envy; the two colonies compete for every bale. In winter the sea freezes for three
months, but the land routes remain open.
