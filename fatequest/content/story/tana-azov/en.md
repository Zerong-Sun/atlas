## city.tana-azov.name

Tana (Azov)

## ev.tana-azov.entry.choice.pass

Thank Zoya and continue by the river

## ev.tana-azov.entry.choice.rest

Spend a day at Zoya's shelter

## ev.tana-azov.entry.body

The Don spreads out before it meets the Sea of Azov. Near a landing place, Zoya has set a pot over a small fire. Her grandson Murad is trying to keep a bundle of reeds from rolling into the water. Each time he leans over to catch one, another slides free.

“Sit on the bundle,” Zoya tells him. He does, and the reeds stay put. She says his father had the same trouble when he first brought a load downriver. Murad asks if his father really lost an entire bundle. Zoya says only half, and he had to explain it to the family that ordered it.

Zoya welcomes travellers when they stop beside the river. She points out where the bank is firm enough to approach the water. Murad wants to know the name of the place you came from; he is collecting names his father might recognize when he returns.

Murad asks whether his father will bring more reeds when he returns. Zoya lifts the lid of the little pot and says the order is already filled; today he is carrying the finished bundles to a family farther upriver. "Will they send him back with anything?" the boy asks. "Probably news, and perhaps the basket I lent them." She asks him to stir the pot while she shows you the firm part of the bank. Murad agrees only after she promises he can ask where you came from when she gets back.

## ev.tana-azov.entry.title

The River Landing at Tana

## ev.ev_tana_azov_entry.choice_1_result

You thank Zoya and continue beside the river. Murad calls out the name of the place you came from once more, to make sure he remembers it. You mark Tana and the river mouth on your map.

## ev.ev_tana_azov_entry.choice_2_result

You stay at Zoya's shelter for the day. Murad shows you the safe path down to the water and asks you to repeat all the town names you know. At supper his father returns with an empty cart, and Murad gets three names right before his father guesses where you are from. Zoya gives you a place to sleep. You leave the next morning stronger and mark Tana on your map.

## ev.gap.tana-azov.scene.title

Salt Fish at the Landing

## ev.gap.tana-azov.scene.body

At the landing, Murad's father Karim is packing salted fish into a barrel. Murad has written marks on the lid for each layer. He has added one mark too many and is waiting for his father to notice.

Karim sees it, counts the fish, and asks where the missing layer went. Murad says he marked a place for tomorrow's catch. “Then we shall need another barrel,” Karim replies. Zoya brings a dry cloth and helps them cover the fish while they decide how much room to leave.

Karim has carried barrels by water toward Caffa and can tell you how boats get out of the river. You can also stay and see how Murad finishes the barrel he started counting.

You ask Murad how he will tell tomorrow’s fish from today’s if both go into the same barrel. He says he will put the next catch on top. Karim shows him that the lid barely closes already, so a second barrel will keep the salt from spilling. Zoya asks whether the new barrel has been washed. Murad starts toward the shed to check. His father calls him back to finish the mark for the fish they have now; tomorrow’s count can wait. Murad scratches the correct number of lines and asks Zoya to read them back.

## ev.gap.tana-azov.scene.choice.talk

Stay until Murad finishes the barrel

Hear Karim describe the water route to Caffa

## ev.gap.tana-azov.scene.result.talk

Murad counts each layer again, erases the extra mark, and helps Karim fit the lid. Zoya points out the dry ground where they store the barrels and the track from there to the landing. You add this part of Tana to your map. Murad says the next barrel will have its own marks, even if tomorrow's catch is small.

## ev.gap.tana-azov.scene.result.route

Karim draws the Don, the Sea of Azov, and the passage out toward the Black Sea with a wet finger on a board. He names the places where his boat stopped on the way to Caffa. Murad adds a mark for each one. You record Tana, Caffa, and the route between them on your map.

## ev.tana.porridge.title

Zoya's Road Names

## ev.tana.porridge.body

Zoya has set out bowls of barley porridge for passing travellers. Murad sits beside her with a cord full of knots. His father tied one knot for every place on a journey toward Kiovia; Murad is trying to put the names back in the right order.

“He remembers the road better than his own birthday,” Zoya says. Karim corrects her from the fire: he remembers both, and it was Zoya who once forgot to send him a birthday letter. She says the letter was written, just left beneath the pot. Murad laughs and points to the last knot, which his father says marks the place where the family received it.

Zoya sells a warm bowl to travellers. If you want to hear the road story, Karim will untie the knots with Murad while you take notes. He has travelled the route himself and can name the stops toward Kiovia.

You ask whether the birthday letter ever reached Karim. He says it did, because his mother tucked it into the food bundle after she found it. Zoya asks Murad to show you the knot that marks the place where he read it. He feels along the cord, stops, then chooses the next knot instead. ‘That one,’ Karim says, ‘where we waited for the rain to ease.’ Zoya ladles another bowl for a boatman and tells Murad to wash his hands before touching the bread. He holds up both palms, dusty from the rope, and goes to the water jar.

## ev.tana.porridge.choice.meal

Buy a warm bowl of barley porridge for 0.8 silver

## ev.tana.porridge.choice.road

Follow the knots and copy the Kiovia route

## ev.tana.porridge.result.meal

You pay Zoya for a bowl. Murad brings you a spoon, then asks if you can remember the name of the first place on his father's route. You eat while he tries the knots again. The porridge warms you and gives you strength for the road. Zoya finds the old birthday letter and shows Murad where she signed it.

## ev.tana.porridge.result.road

Karim unties the cord one knot at a time. Murad says each name before his father does; twice he gets it wrong and starts again from the river mouth. You copy the sequence onto your map, marking Tana, Kiovia, and the road between them. At the last knot Zoya admits that she did send the letter in the end. Karim says he still has it.
