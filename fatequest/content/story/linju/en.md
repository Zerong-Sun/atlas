## city.linju.name

Linju

## ev.linju.entry.body

On leaving the city of Sinju-matu you travel for eight days towards the south, always
coming to great and rich towns and villages flourishing with trade and manufactures. The
people are all subjects of the Great Kaan, use paper-money, and burn their dead. At the
end of those eight days you come to the city of LINJU, in the province of the same name
of which it is the capital. It is a rich and noble city, and the men are good soldiers,
natheless they carry on great trade and manufactures. There is great abundance of game
in both beasts and birds, and all the necessaries of life are in profusion.

## ev.linju.a.title

Linju: The Soldier Merchants of the River City

## ev.linju.a.body

In the rich and noble city that stands upon the river, the men of Linju are said to be good soldiers yet carry on great trade and manufactures. A merchant who has served in the garrison and now prices silk on the wharf says the city has great numbers of vessels and all the necessaries of life in profusion, and game in both beasts and birds — and that a traveller who can hold a sword is welcomed in the market as one who can hold a price.

## ev.linju.a.choice.1

Buy silk at the wharf price

## ev.linju.a.choice.2

Ask the merchant the road toward Sinjumatu

## ev.linju.a.choice.3

## ev.ev_linju_a_followup.title

Linju: The Wharf Merchant's Second Page

## ev.ev_linju_a_followup.body

The merchant lays his ledger on a bale and shows you the river's account. He says the vessels carry the produce of the province north and south, and that game and silk are the two riches that never fail here — the one for the table, the other for the loom. He asks what a traveller who can hold a sword means to hold next.

## ev.ev_linju_a_followup.choice_1

Buy rhubarb at the merchant's road price

## ev.ev_linju_a_followup.choice_1_result

He sells you rhubarb at a fair price. The root is packed and will keep on the northern road.

## ev.ev_linju_a_followup.choice_2

## ev.ev_linju_a_followup.choice_2_result

Cacanfu is marked on your map with the days between. The river road north is complete.
