## city.linju.name

Linju

## ev.linju.entry.body

On leaving the city of Sinju-matu you travel for eight days towards the south, always
coming to great and rich towns and villages flourishing with trade and manufactures. The
people are all subjects of the Great Kaan, use paper-money, and burn their dead. At the
end of those eight days you come to the city of LINJU, in the province of the same name
of which it is the capital. It is a rich and noble city, and the men are good soldiers,
natheless they carry on great trade and manufactures. There is great abundance of game
in both beasts and birds, and all the necessaries of life are in profusion.
