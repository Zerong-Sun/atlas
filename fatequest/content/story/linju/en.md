## city.linju.name

Linju

## ev.linju.entry.body

At a riverside storehouse in Linju, a boatman and his sister are trying to match a stack of baskets to a written list. She has counted fourteen; he insists there were fifteen when they left the landing. Their mother comes out with a wet cloth and says one basket is cooling in the shade because its lid cracked. The brother stops looking under the boat. ‘You could have told me,’ he says. ‘You could have asked before shouting at the water,’ she replies.

You ask where travellers enter the town. The sister points to the street beside the storehouse. Her mother invites you to look around when the baskets are settled; the people along the river sell supplies for carts as well as boats. A porter arrives with a new lid and asks which basket needs it. Mother and daughter both point to the shade.

The boatman checks the list again, now counting the shaded basket. He has room at his table if you want to spend a day learning how the landing sorts its goods.

## ev.linju.entry.choice.look

Spend a day at the landing with the family

## ev.linju.entry.choice.pass

Follow the street past the storehouse

## ev.linju.entry.title

At the Riverside Storehouse

## ev.ev_linju_entry.choice_1_result

You follow the street into Linju. Behind you, the boatman adds the shaded basket to the list and his sister carries the new lid over to try it. Their mother waves before returning to the storehouse.

## ev.ev_linju_entry.choice_2_result

You stay a day and learn how the family checks baskets arriving by water and leaving by cart. The brother writes a separate mark for damaged lids, and his sister decides where to store them. You remember the landing through their work.

## ev.ev_gap_linju.title

The Umbrella Maker’s Counter

## ev.ev_gap_linju.body

Umbrella maker Shen has set a half-finished oiled-paper umbrella on his counter. His daughter opens it once, then stops when one rib catches the edge. ‘That is the rib I told you about,’ she says. Shen asks her to show him exactly where it snags. She traces the paper seam, and he realizes he sanded the wrong rib. You ask whether the umbrella can still be used. He says it can after they trim and oil the seam again.

A customer is waiting to take the umbrella along the land road toward Cacanfu. He has a wrapped parcel and worries about the coming rain. Shen tells him to keep the parcel under the counter while they work. His daughter asks the customer which turns he will take once he leaves town. He draws the first junction in a patch of flour dust on the board.

Shen hands you a clean cloth to hold below the seam. His daughter is ready to test the umbrella once more, and the customer waits beside his dry parcel.

## ev.ev_gap_linju.choice.route

Ask the customer about the land road to Cacanfu

## ev.ev_gap_linju.choice.route_result

The customer draws the turns from Linju toward Cacanfu on your map. Shen’s daughter checks the first junction against the mark in the flour dust. You note the road while they finish the umbrella.

## ev.ev_gap_linju.choice.scene

Help Shen and his daughter test the umbrella

## ev.ev_gap_linju.choice.scene_result

You hold the cloth while Shen trims the snagging rib. His daughter opens the umbrella twice without catching the paper; the customer thanks all three of you and finally takes his parcel from under the counter.

## ev.linju.basketlid.title

The Lid in the Shade

## ev.linju.basketlid.body

Behind the riverside storehouse, the boatman’s sister is trying a new lid on the damaged basket. Their mother says it sits too tightly; a basket of fresh vegetables needs air. The sister loosens the binding, but then the lid shifts when the porter lifts it. ‘Now the road will shake it off,’ he says. The boatman comes out with a short strip of reed and offers to weave a flexible loop.

You ask where the vegetables are going. The mother says they are for an elderly neighbour who can no longer come to the landing herself. The porter knows her doorway but has another load to carry before dusk. The sister wants to send the basket soon, while the mother wants the lid right first. The boatman weaves one loop and asks the porter to try lifting the basket again. It holds, though the reed end still needs trimming. The neighbour’s granddaughter arrives, surprised to find everyone working on her grandmother’s supper.

The granddaughter puts down her own small basket and asks if she can carry the lighter one.

## ev.linju.basketlid.choice.deliver

Carry the basket with the granddaughter

## ev.linju.basketlid.choice.mend

Spend a day learning to weave the flexible lid

## ev.linju.basketlid.result.deliver

You trim the loose reed end and carry the basket with the granddaughter. Her grandmother opens it at the doorway, pleased that the vegetables are still fresh. The family at the landing hears the news when you return.

## ev.linju.basketlid.result.mend

You stay the day and make another flexible lid with the brother and sister. The porter tests it on a different basket before leaving. Their mother sends the vegetables with the granddaughter and saves your work for the next delivery.
