## city.mien.name

Mien

## ev.mien.entry.body

And when you have travelled those 15 days through such a difficult country as I have
described, in which travellers have to carry provisions for the road because there are
no inhabitants, then you arrive at the capital city of this Province of Mien, and it
also is called AMIEN, and is a very great and noble city. The people are Idolaters and
have a peculiar language, and are subject to the Great Kaan. And in this city there is a
thing so rich and rare that I must tell you about it.

Arriving at MIEN

## ev.ev_mien_entry.choice_1_result

## ev.ev_mien_entry.choice_2_result

You rest and ask what men say of this place. The road to Mien opens on your map. The account is written down before the hour turns. It costs you 1 day on the road.

## ev.ev_mien_a.choice_2_result

## ev.ev_mien_a.choice_3_result

You spend a day about the towers. Their measure is learned, and a small turn of fortune favours you.

## ev.mien.a.title

Mien: The Two Towers by the King's Tomb

## ev.mien.a.body

In the great and noble city of Mien, the people speak a peculiar language and keep the tomb of a rich and puissant king, beside which stand two towers — one of gold and one of silver — raised by his command as he lay dying. An old keeper who guards the tomb says the towers were to shine for the dead king's honour, and that the city is subject to the Great Kaan. He asks whether you have come to see the wonder or to trade.

## ev.mien.a.choice.1

Buy silk at the tomb-market price

## ev.mien.a.choice.2

Ask the keeper the road toward Sindafu

## ev.mien.a.choice.3

Circle the two towers a day and note their measure

## ev.ev_mien_a_followup.title

Mien: The Keeper's Tale of the Dying King

## ev.ev_mien_a_followup.body

The keeper sits you down in the tomb's shade and tells the tale as it is told in the city. He says the king, when he was about to die, commanded the two towers so that his name might shine after him — one tower of gold, one of silver, each as tall as a tall man's lance. He says the towers have stood through the conquest, and that a traveller who remembers the king's wish is remembered kindly in Mien.

## ev.ev_mien_a_followup.choice_1

Offer a small gift at the tomb for the king's honour

## ev.ev_mien_a_followup.choice_1_result

Your gift is accepted without ceremony. The keeper speaks your name to the tomb, and a little favour goes with you on the road.

## ev.ev_mien_a_followup.choice_2

Ask the keeper the road toward the sea

## ev.ev_mien_a_followup.choice_2_result

The road toward the coast is marked on your map. The way out of the valley opens further.

## ev.ev_mien_a_followup.choice_3

Wait a day and hear the city's other wonders

## ev.ev_mien_a_followup.choice_3_result

You wait a day and hear the wonders. The city's tales are learned, and in Mien your name now carries a little more weight.
