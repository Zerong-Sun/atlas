## ev.edessa.entry.body

Edessa — an ancient Christian city on the edge of the Turcoman steppe, once home to the
fabled Mandylion, the cloth that bore Christ's face. Now a frontier fortress, its walls
scarred by siege and counter-siege. The caravans from Mosul water their camels here
before crossing into Anatolia; the trade in horses, carpets, and cotton keeps the bazaar
alive.
