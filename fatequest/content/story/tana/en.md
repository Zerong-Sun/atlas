## city.tana.name

Tana

## ev.tana.entry.body

Tana is a great kingdom lying towards the west, a kingdom great both in size and worth.
The people are Idolaters, with a language of their own, and a king of their own, and
tributary to nobody. No pepper grows there, nor other spices, but plenty of incense; not
the white kind however, but brown. There is much traffic here, and many ships and
merchants frequent the place; for there is a great export of leather of various
excellent kinds, and also of good buckram and cotton. The merchants in their ships also
import various articles, such as gold, silver, copper, and other things in demand.

## ev.tana.site_gap.title

A Pot of Coconut Sugar by the Shore

## ev.tana.site_gap.body

A sugar maker watches coconut sap bubble in a wide pot. He skims it with a wooden spoon. Too fierce a fire drives off the water quickly and makes the sugar bitter. When the syrup coats the back of the spoon, he pours it into moulds to cool. He scrapes a thin crust from the rim for a child to taste, sweet with a hint of char.

## ev.tana.site_gap.choice.route

## ev.ev_tana_site_gap.choice_1_result

“Follow this coastal road south to Melibar. If you want to sail, ask a captain on shore; ships leave when the wind suits.”

## ev.tana.site_gap.choice.interact

Try judging when the syrup is ready

## ev.ev_tana_site_gap.choice_2_result

He shows you the line it leaves on the spoon. If it runs off at once, it is still thin. A coating means it is time to take it from the fire; longer boiling darkens the colour and deepens the toasted taste.
