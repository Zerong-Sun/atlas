## city.chinghianfu.name

Chinghianfu

## ev.chinghianfu.entry.body

A reader from a small Christian congregation has come to the bookbinder with a loose page. It slipped from a volume used at their gathering, and the children have handled it so often that the corner is soft. The binder’s daughter holds it beside the book. ‘This page belongs after the story about the traveller,’ she says. The reader checks the first words of the next leaf and agrees. ‘Can it be stitched in before the meeting?’ you ask. ‘It has to dry flat first,’ the binder says. The reader looks at the damp corner. ‘Then we will read another passage today,’ he replies. Someone had tried to paste it with too much glue. The reader offers to leave it overnight and asks his daughter to tell the other children they will use a different passage today. At the doorway, a boatman wants directions to the landing toward Chinginju. The binder points him down the street and asks his daughter to guide him past the crowded market. She invites you to follow now, or return tomorrow when the repaired book is ready and she can show you more of the town.

## ev.chinghianfu.entry.choice.look

Stay a day with the reader and binder

## ev.chinghianfu.entry.choice.pass

Take the street through town

## ev.chinghianfu.entry.title

The Reader’s Missing Page

## ev.ev_chinghianfu_entry.choice_1_result

The binder’s daughter points out the street to the landing before taking the boatman through the market. The reader carries the rest of his books back to the meeting house. You follow the same turn across town and know where to find the waterside path.

## ev.ev_chinghianfu_entry.choice_2_result

You stay another day. The page dries flat, and the binder shows his daughter how to stitch it in without pulling the edge. The reader returns, checks that the children can turn it safely, and introduces you to the families who use the book.

## ev.ev_gap_chinghianfu.title

The Water Carrier’s Steps

## ev.ev_gap_chinghianfu.body

A water carrier has set two buckets halfway up the stone steps from the landing. The lowest steps are slick with green growth, and his niece has brought sand to scatter there. ‘If I cover the whole stair, people will carry it into the well,’ she says. He asks her to use only enough for a firm foothold. You offer to take one bucket while he tests the bottom step. A woman waiting above says she knows a cleaner well near the western gate, but its keeper asks that visitors rinse their buckets first. ‘Where do we rinse the buckets?’ the carrier asks. ‘At the jar outside the wall,’ she says. His niece repeats the directions while tying the sand bag shut. You ask about the road toward Nanghin; the woman has travelled it and offers to draw the first turn. The carrier wants to finish bringing the water up before anyone rushes down the slick steps.

## ev.ev_gap_chinghianfu.choice.route

Ask about the road toward Nanghin

## ev.ev_gap_chinghianfu.choice.route_result

The woman draws the road toward Nanghin on your board, beginning at the western gate. The niece repeats the turn while the water carrier lifts the last bucket. You leave with the route as they open the steps to the next person.

## ev.ev_gap_chinghianfu.choice.scene

Help carry water up the steps

## ev.ev_gap_chinghianfu.choice.scene_result

You take one bucket while the carrier tests each step. His niece uses a little sand where his foot slips and keeps the rest in her bag. At the top he thanks you and points out the jar where visitors rinse their buckets before using the well.

## ev.chinghianfu.binder.title

A Book for the Neighbour’s Child

## ev.chinghianfu.binder.body

A bookbinder has promised to mend a small picture book for a neighbour’s child. The child has arrived early and is counting the loose leaves on the table. ‘There were eight pictures,’ she says. The binder finds seven. His husband remembers seeing a leaf beneath the drying rack, where it was placed after a drink spilled. You retrieve it, but the ink has run near the corner. The child can still recognize the goat in the picture. ‘That is the one my grandfather likes,’ she says. The binder asks whether she wants him to cut off the stained edge. She shakes her head; the goat would lose its ear. His husband suggests sewing the page in as it is and putting a plain sheet behind it so the stain will not touch the next one. The child helps count the order again and asks you to hold the leaves while they stitch, or return tomorrow to read the finished book with her grandfather.

## ev.chinghianfu.binder.choice.sort

Hold the leaves while they stitch

## ev.chinghianfu.binder.result.sort

You hold the leaves in order as the binder threads them together. The child counts all eight pictures and shows the goat to her grandfather when he comes to collect her. The family thanks you, and the binder remembers your help when you pass his shop again.

## ev.chinghianfu.binder.choice.listen

Return a day later for the finished book

## ev.chinghianfu.binder.result.listen

You return the next day. The repaired book is dry, and the child reads the goat’s page aloud while her grandfather fills in a word she misses. The binder’s husband serves tea. You stay long enough to hear why that worn page matters to the family.
