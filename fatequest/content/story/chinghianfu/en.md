## city.chinghianfu.name

Chinghianfu

## ev.chinghianfu.entry.body

Chinghianfu is a city of Manzi. The people are Idolaters and subject to the Great Kaan,
and have paper-money, and live by handicrafts and trade. They have plenty of silk, from
which they make sundry kinds of stuffs of silk and gold. There are great and wealthy
merchants in the place; plenty of game is to be had, and of all kinds of victual. There
are in this city two churches of Nestorian Christians which were established in the year
of our Lord 1278; and I will tell you how that happened.

## ev.ev_gap_chinghianfu.title

Water Buckets on the Stone Steps

## ev.ev_gap_chinghianfu.body

Stone steps lead down to the water in Chinghianfu. A water carrier sets down his buckets to catch his breath. The lowest steps grow slick with moss. He asks whether you can help lift the buckets or would rather know which street has a clean well; he has no wish to fall again.
