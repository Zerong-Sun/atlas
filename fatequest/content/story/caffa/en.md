## city.caffa.name

Caffa

## ev.caffa.entry.choice.pass

Thank Arslan and continue through Caffa

## ev.caffa.entry.choice.rest

Spend a day at Arslan's shelter

## ev.caffa.entry.body

At Caffa, the road reaches the Black Sea. Arslan has stopped his cart near the shore to let his horses drink. His granddaughter Sada is brushing sand from a leather bag. She says it was clean when they left home; Arslan says she has opened it six times since then to count the dried apples inside.

“There are still eight,” she tells him. He asks how many there were in the morning. Sada looks at you before answering: ten. Arslan breaks one apple in half and gives her a piece, saying she can count that as one or two, whichever makes her happier.

They have travelled from the open country beyond the shore. Arslan knows where the carts gather before setting out again and shows you a path between the houses and the water. He offers you a place beneath his shelter if you need to recover from the road.

## ev.caffa.entry.title

The Road Reaches Caffa

## ev.ev_caffa_entry.choice_1_result

You thank Arslan and move on. Sada calls after you to say there are seven and a half apples now, because she has eaten her half. You mark Caffa and the shore road on your map.

## ev.ev_caffa_entry.choice_2_result

You stay at Arslan's shelter for the day. He shows you where the carts gather, while Sada makes you count the apples twice to prove she has not hidden any. They share supper and give you a place to sleep. You leave the next morning stronger and mark Caffa on your map.

## ev.caffa.site_gap.title

Fur Bundles by the Shore

## ev.caffa.site_gap.body

Beside the water, Rena and her brother Matvei are moving bundles of furs onto wooden slats. Matvei sets a bundle on the bare ground; Rena lifts it again. The bottom of its wrapping is already damp.

Their mother packed the furs before they left the steppe, sewing a different coloured thread into each bundle so the family could tell which belonged to whom. Matvei knows the colours but keeps forgetting to lift the bundles clear of the ground. Rena says their mother would notice that too.

A boat going along the coast toward Trebizond may carry some of the bundles. Rena can tell you where to ask about the crossing, or would welcome help putting the last pieces safely above the wet ground.

‘Mother stitched the red thread into mine,’ Matvei says, turning a pack to show you. Rena checks the seam and replies, ‘Yes, and she told you twice to keep that side dry.’ You ask whether the ship to Trebizond has space for all their cargo. Rena says they will learn when the captain weighs the load; she has not promised the family more room than she has. Matvei lifts the wet pack onto the slats and asks if it can still travel. His sister feels the lining, finds the fur dry inside, and tells him to leave it open to the air until the boatman calls. She hands you the next bundle by its dry handle.

## ev.caffa.site_gap.choice.route

Ask Rena about the Trebizond crossing

## ev.caffa.site_gap.choice.cargo

Help Rena lift the last fur bundles

## ev.caffa.site_gap.result.route

Rena points along the Black Sea coast toward Trebizond and tells you where the boatmen gather. Matvei reads the coloured stitches aloud as he counts the bundles for the crossing. You mark Caffa, Trebizond, and the route over the water on your map, then leave them to finish the loading.

## ev.caffa.site_gap.result.cargo

You and Matvei lift the last bundles onto dry slats. Rena checks the coloured stitches and finds every package in its place. When their mother returns, Matvei shows her which ones he stacked himself. She thanks you, and the family tells neighbouring carriers who helped protect the furs.

## ev.caffa.leathercut.title

The Leather for Sada’s Boots

## ev.caffa.leathercut.body

Arslan has spread pieces of leather beside his cart. He is cutting a pair of boots for Sada, who claims her feet grew during the journey. She puts both feet on the leather and asks for extra room at the toes.

“You said that before we left,” Arslan says. Sada says the road has been long. He measures again anyway. A larger bundle of finished leather, brought from inland, is tied beside the wheel for sale. Arslan also needs someone to sort and wrap the smaller pieces before the next cart goes out.

Arslan sets the full leather lot on a dry board. Sada watches him cut the last boot piece, then points to a small offcut he promised she could keep. He asks her to wait until the buyer has checked the whole pack.

You ask why she wants the offcuts. ‘I am making a cover for my little sister’s knife,’ Sada says. Arslan reminds her that the blade must be wrapped separately until she learns to stitch the sheath. She asks whether you can show her how to make the first hole without tearing the leather. Her grandfather gives her an awl and points to a scrap that is safe to practise on. The cart driver calls from the road, asking when the remaining small pieces will be ready to load. Arslan says after the boot work is settled, and asks Sada to keep the awl away from the finished leather.

## ev.caffa.leathercut.choice.buy

Buy Arslan’s bundle of leather for 120 silver

## ev.caffa.leathercut.choice.work

Stay a day and sort the leather for a wage

## ev.caffa.leathercut.result.buy

You pay Arslan and take the bundle of leather. He checks the ties before letting you lift it. Sada watches him cut the boots; after he has left extra room for her toes, she gathers the offcuts to make a small pouch. You carry the purchased leather onward.

## ev.caffa.leathercut.result.work

You spend the day sorting and wrapping the smaller pieces. Arslan cuts Sada's new boots between bundles. By evening she can walk in them without her toes pressing the front, and she shows you a pouch made from the scraps. Arslan pays the agreed wage. You leave the next morning, after Sada has run around the cart twice to test her new boots.
