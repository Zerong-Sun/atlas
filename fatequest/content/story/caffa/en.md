## ev.caffa.entry.body

Caffa, the Genoese colony on the Crimean shore, ringed by walls that the Tartars have
never breached. From here, Tatar slaves, Russian furs, and Black Sea grain flow west to
Constantinople and beyond. The harbor freezes in winter, but the trade never stops —
sledges cross the ice to Tana when the ships cannot sail. The Genoese consul rules the
city; his palace commands the harbor, and his galleys patrol the straits.
