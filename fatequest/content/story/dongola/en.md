## ev.dongola.entry.body

Dongola — a Nubian kingdom on the upper Nile, Christian since the sixth century. The
cathedral of painted saints overlooks the river; the king still mints coins with the
cross. Caravans bring gold and ivory from the interior and slaves from the pagan south.
The desert on either side is waterless for ten days' journey.
