## city.dongola.name

Dongola

## ev.dongola.entry.body

At Dongola, a caravan has halted near the river while a woman repairs a strap on a water carrier's shoulder. Her son points to the church beyond the houses. “Grandfather said there are pictures older than his father inside.” The woman smiles. “There are. Go and ask the reader which ones he can show you.” A church reader comes out with a bucket and asks the boy to carry it. You ask where the road turns toward the next station. He points past the ferry, then asks whether you have enough water for the dry stretch. The woman feels the weight of your skin and says it should be filled before departure. Inside the church, painted figures remain from earlier generations, but the reader is busy patching a small leak in the roof today. The boy returns with his bucket, proud that he has been trusted with it, and asks whether you will stay to hear the reader's account after the work is done.

## ev.dongola.entry.choice.rest

## ev.dongola.entry.title

A Water Carrier at Dongola

## ev.ev_dongola_entry.choice_1_result

The reader points out the road beyond the ferry and tells you where the next water stop lies. The boy follows you a few steps before his mother calls him back to the strap. You fill your skin, fix the turn in mind and continue.

## ev.ev_dongola_entry.choice_2_result

You stay for a day. The reader shows the boy the painted wall after the roof has been mended, and the water carrier's family shares supper with you. A full night's rest restores your strength; when you leave, you know the station and its ferry road well.

## ev.dongola.site_gap.title

The Ferry Rope

## ev.dongola.site_gap.body

At the river ferry, a boatman is replacing the worn end of a reed rope. His daughter holds the coil while he tests each strand. ‘The old part caught under the hull yesterday,’ she says. He asks her to show you the frayed place, then explains why the rope must give a little as the water rises. A traveller arrives with a crate and tries to set it by the rail. The boatman asks him to move it toward the middle of the boat. ‘When everyone stands on one side to see the far bank, I still have to keep her level.’ You ask how to reach the Arabian towns from here. His daughter says the road first follows the Nile north toward Cairo; from there travellers cross Sinai before continuing into Arabia. ‘My aunt went that way,’ she says. ‘She sent word from Cairo before she crossed the desert.’ The boatman points to the marks on his pole, which he uses to read the water. His daughter asks whether today's level changes the loading order. He checks it before answering, then lets her place the traveller's crate at the centre of the boat.

## ev.dongola.site_gap.choice.route

Ask about the Nile road toward Cairo

## ev.ev_dongola_site_gap.choice_1_result

From the far bank, the daughter points out the northbound Nile road. She makes you repeat the turn where the ferry path ends and names the next place to find water. ‘When you reach Cairo, ask again before crossing Sinai,’ she says. Her father checks the river mark once more before returning to the boat.

## ev.dongola.site_gap.choice.interact

Help inspect the ferry rope

## ev.ev_dongola_site_gap.choice_2_result

You hold the new coil while the boatman draws each length through his hands. At the frayed part he shows you how the fibres have rubbed against the hull. His daughter reties the sound end under his eye, and he lets her test the finished knot.

## ev.dongola.garden.title

The Garden beside the Landing

## ev.dongola.garden.body

A woman tends a small garden above the river landing. Her nephew has brought seeds from a relative upriver but cannot remember which packet holds the beans and which holds the gourds. “Your aunt drew them on the paper,” she says. He opens the bundle; rain has blurred the drawings. She puts a seed from each packet in his palm and asks him to look at their shapes. He recognizes the beans, then admits he has already sown some in the wrong bed. You ask whether they must dig them out. “We can wait until they come up,” she says. “For today, help me keep the fresh water in the bed we know.” The little channel to it has filled with sand. The nephew fetches a wooden scoop, and a ferry passenger offers to carry the remaining seeds into the shade. The woman asks you to stay long enough to see which sprouts appear.

## ev.dongola.garden.choice.channel

Clear the water channel with them

## ev.dongola.garden.result.channel

You and the nephew lift sand from the channel while his aunt steadies the little gate. Water reaches the new bed before the heat grows strong. A ferry passenger sees the three of you working and brings another scoop; the garden's neighbours thank you for helping keep their shared water flowing.

## ev.dongola.garden.choice.wait

Stay a day and help sort the seed beds

## ev.dongola.garden.result.wait

You stay for a day, marking the two beds and helping the nephew sort the remaining seeds. At supper his aunt brings beans from the last harvest and tells him that the uncertain bed can wait until it sprouts. You leave rested after sharing their meal.
