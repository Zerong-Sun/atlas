## div.astrodice.result.00

Great fortune: the dice name a morning departure before the heat rises.

## div.astrodice.result.01

The dice name a three-day wait: the next caravan is better company.

## div.astrodice.result.02

The dice name the north gate; the south road is crowded with tax clerks.

## div.astrodice.result.03

The dice name a short leg only; do not press beyond the next well.

## div.astrodice.result.04

The dice name bargaining at dusk, when the stalls lower their first prices.

## div.astrodice.result.05

The dice name a translator before any contract is sealed.

## div.astrodice.result.06

The dice name inland forage; the coast wind turns against shipping.

## div.astrodice.result.07

The dice name lodging near the mosque courtyard, not the riverbank.

## div.astrodice.result.08

The dice name paying the toll in full and asking no favours.

## div.astrodice.result.09

The dice name sending a scout ahead to the next caravanserai.

## div.astrodice.result.10

The dice name postponing the mountain pass until after the new moon.

## div.astrodice.result.11

The dice name buying water-skins here; they will be dearer ahead.

## div.astrodice.result.12

The dice name travelling with the spice merchants, not alone.

## div.astrodice.result.13

The dice name avoiding night watches on the open steppe.

## div.astrodice.result.14

The dice name changing horses at the next post, not this one.

## div.astrodice.result.15

Ill omen: the dice name postponing the mountain pass until after the new moon.

## div.astrodice.result.16

The dice name resting one day for a lame camel rather than forcing the march.

## div.astrodice.result.17

The dice name asking the astrologer of this court which month the road opens.

## div.astrodice.result.18

The dice name keeping silver divided among three purses.

## div.astrodice.result.19

The dice name taking the longer river road; the short cut floods.

## div.astrodice.result.20

The dice name waiting for the Friday market before you sell.

## div.astrodice.result.21

The dice name speaking little of your destination in this inn.

## div.astrodice.result.22

The dice name hiring a local watch for the warehouse night.

## div.astrodice.result.23

The dice name not crossing the ferry until the boatmen sober after noon.

## div.astrodice.result.24

The dice name reading the wind for two dawns before you sail.

## div.astrodice.result.25

The dice name seeking the Armenian quarter for news of the inland road.

## div.astrodice.result.26

The dice name not trusting the first guide who greets you at the gate.

## div.astrodice.result.27

The dice name marking the wells on your tablet before you leave.

## div.astrodice.result.28

The dice name offering a small gift at the shrine before you bargain.

## div.astrodice.result.29

The dice name choosing the road that still has shade at noon.
