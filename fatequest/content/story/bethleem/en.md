## city.bethleem.name

Bethlehem

## ev.bethleem.entry.body

Beside a hill road in Bethlehem, Yona sweeps ashes from his oven into a clay bowl. Pilgrims passing through sometimes sit by his hearth. His daughter Lina has just taken out a tray of small loaves. The edge of one is black.

“I said only yesterday that I'd watch the fire this time.” Lina turns the loaf over, trying to hide the burnt edge.

Yona picks up the wooden peel. “The back of the oven is hotter. See which loaves the travellers take first? Always the ones near the front. We'll eat this one ourselves and bake another tray for them this afternoon.”

Lina asks whether he burned loaves as a child. Yona says that the first time he watched an oven alone, he spoiled a whole basket. His mother showed him how to turn the tray halfway round. The next day he had bread he could sell. Lina looks into the oven and draws a circle in the dust with the peel, a reminder to turn the tray later.

Yona sees you resting nearby and asks whether you will continue along the road or wait for the next baking.

## ev.bethleem.entry.choice.pass

Ask Yona about the hill road and continue today

## ev.bethleem.entry.choice.rest

Rest by the oven for a day and try Lina's fresh bread

## ev.bethleem.entry.title

Bethlehem

## ev.ev_bethleem_entry.choice_1_result

You ask Yona to show you the road. He sets down the peel and walks you to the edge of the path so you can see the next fork. Lina calls him back to taste a loaf, and he answers her. You continue on your way and mark Bethlehem on your map.

## ev.ev_bethleem_entry.choice_2_result

You spend the day by the oven. That afternoon Lina remembers to turn the tray. Every loaf comes out evenly coloured. She offers one to her father. Yona breaks it open and tastes it. “Done right through. These are ready for the travellers.”

At dusk they invite you and a few passersby to share warm bread, olives, and soup. Lina breaks her burnt loaf from the morning and finishes it with the soup. You sleep on one of the pallets they lend to travellers and leave the next morning with your strength restored. You mark Bethlehem on your map.

## ev.gap.bethleem.scene.title

A Small Pair of Scissors by the Oil Lamp

## ev.gap.bethleem.scene.body

Salma, who sells lamp oil, is filling a clay lamp. Her grandson Gabriel sits beside her with a small pair of scissors, studying the wick.

“Where do I cut?” he asks.

“Leave a little above the oil,” Salma says, holding the lamp steady. “Too much and the rim will blacken. Too little and the flame will sink.”

Gabriel sets down the scissors. He was looking after the lamp in their yard last night. The wind blew it out, and the people returning home had to find the door latch in the dark. Salma strokes his hair. “Move it inside the wall and try again. I'll be beside you tonight.”

A traveller from the coast stops to buy oil and joins them to look at the lamp. Salma asks whether you will help Gabriel test it. The traveller says he knows the long road to Alexandria.

## ev.gap.bethleem.scene.choice.talk

Trim the wick and test the lamp with Gabriel

## ev.gap.bethleem.scene.choice.route

Listen to the traveller describe the road to Alexandria

## ev.gap.bethleem.scene.result.talk

You hold the lamp while Gabriel trims the wick. Salma lights it and asks him to move it inside the wall. Wind comes through the yard entrance. The flame shifts a little and stays lit. Gabriel picks up the lamp and says he will place it there himself tonight. Salma shows you where to find her oil stall, and you mark this resting place in Bethlehem on your map.

## ev.gap.bethleem.scene.result.route

The traveller tells you the route to Alexandria: first reach the coast, then continue towards Egypt. On his last journey he met a man repairing a cart wheel along the way. The delay cost him half a day, and by the time the wheel was fixed he had companions for the next stretch. You mark Alexandria and the route on your map.

As you talk, Gabriel finishes trimming the wick. Salma lights the lamp and asks him to test it inside the wall. When he comes back, he smiles. “It stayed lit.”

## ev.bethleem.stool.title

A Small Stool on the Hillside

## ev.bethleem.stool.body

Outside a small house on the slope, Hanna sets a low stool on level ground and tries to rock it. It stands firm. Her son Ishaq crouches beside her and measures two of its legs with his fingers. “Will it stand as well at Grandmother's?”

“On this level ground,” Hanna says. “Your grandmother's doorway has old stone slabs. We'll take it there and try it.”

Ishaq says his grandmother's knees trouble her. She stands to knead dough and has to stop several times before a meal is ready. Hanna has been making the stool for days, and he kept asking her to hurry. Now that it is nearly finished, he worries it will rock on the stones at her door.

Hanna puts a small wooden shim in her pocket. “I'll adjust it when we get there. You can walk up the slope with us, or stay here and help me smooth the rough edge first.”

## ev.bethleem.stool.choice.sand

Stay at the house and help Hanna smooth the stool's edge

## ev.bethleem.stool.choice.deliver

Carry the stool up the slope with Hanna and Ishaq

## ev.bethleem.stool.result.sand

You and Ishaq sand the edge until it no longer catches on a hand. Hanna feels along the grain and asks her son to sit on the stool. He says it is steady, then jumps up. “Grandmother gets the first proper turn.”

Hanna carries it away and promises to tell you whether it needs a shim on her mother's stone floor. The neighbours hear that you helped them finish it and greet you when you pass.

## ev.bethleem.stool.result.deliver

You and the family walk up the slope to the grandmother's place. On the old stone slabs, one leg does lift slightly. Hanna takes the shim from her pocket and settles the stool. Ishaq holds his grandmother's hand as she sits. She runs a hand over the seat and says she can now take her time kneading dough.

On your way back, Ishaq shows you where the hill road joins the way to Jerusalem. Next time you visit, he says, he will bring bread his grandmother made herself. You add Jerusalem and that road to your map.
