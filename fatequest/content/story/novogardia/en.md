## ev.novogardia.entry.body

Novgorod — the great trading republic of the north, where the Hansa merchants buy
Russian wax, honey, and fur from the boyars. The frozen lake serves as a winter highway;
sledges carry goods when the ships are locked in ice. The cathedral of St. Sophia holds
the city's treasury in its crypt. No Tartar tax-collector has ever entered the walls.
