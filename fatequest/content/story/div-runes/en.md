## div.runes.result.00

Great fortune: the rune names company — join the next wagon before the steppe widens.

## div.runes.result.01

The rune names a river crossing at dawn, when the ice is still firm.

## div.runes.result.02

The rune names speaking the northern tongue at the next post.

## div.runes.result.03

The rune names a gift of salt to the first elder who greets you.

## div.runes.result.04

The rune names marking the stars for three nights before you turn.

## div.runes.result.05

The rune names keeping your sword visible but sheathed in this town.

## div.runes.result.06

The rune names buying felt and not silk on this market day.

## div.runes.result.07

The rune names a detour to the wooden church for news of the road.

## div.runes.result.08

The rune names waiting out the blizzard in the stockaded yard.

## div.runes.result.09

The rune names hiring a local boy who knows the wolf tracks.

## div.runes.result.10

The rune names delay: one day for the ferry ropes to be mended.

## div.runes.result.11

The rune names delay until the tribute train has passed.

## div.runes.result.12

The rune names delay: dry your boots before the next marsh.

## div.runes.result.13

Ill omen: the rune names danger on the reed bank; keep to the raised track.

## div.runes.result.14

The rune names danger in the abandoned tower; do not shelter there.

## div.runes.result.15

The rune names danger if you travel without a bell on the lead horse.

## div.runes.result.16

The rune names an ally among the fur traders of this quay.

## div.runes.result.17

The rune names an ally if you share bread at the gate-fire.

## div.runes.result.18

The rune names travel light across the frozen lake; leave the cart.

## div.runes.result.19

The rune names travel with the amber merchants bound west.

## div.runes.result.20

The rune names travel only while the sun clears the pine line.

## div.runes.result.21

The rune names holding your question until you reach the next idol-post.

## div.runes.result.22

The rune names holding silver back from the first inn's reckoning.

## div.runes.result.23

The rune names holding the southern fork; the northern is flooded.

## div.runes.result.24

The rune names asking the Varangian guard which bridge still stands.

## div.runes.result.25

The rune names writing the tribal passwords you are given tonight.

## div.runes.result.26

The rune names not racing the horse that limps into the yard.

## div.runes.result.27

The rune names counting your arrows before you leave the stockade.

## div.runes.result.28

The rune names paying the ferryman what he asks; argue later ashore.

## div.runes.result.29

The rune names turning back one station if the sky turns iron-grey.
