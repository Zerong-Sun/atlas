## ev.ev_unique_paukin.title

The Cart on the Raised Road

## ev.ev_unique_paukin.body

On the raised road outside Paukin, a cart wheel has settled between two paving stones. Driver Lan asks her son to bring the spare plank, but he has tied it under the sacks. ‘I thought it would stay dry there,’ he says. She tells him a plank is most useful where hands can reach it. He unties it, checks that the grain sacks are secure, and asks where to set it.

You ask how long the cart has been stuck. Lan says since morning; she still hopes to reach the market before dark. A local guide stands nearby with a road sheet showing the way from Paukin toward Tiju. He charges 1.5 silver to check the difficult stretches with you. Lan asks him whether he can help push first. He agrees, provided someone steadies the sacks while he moves the plank.

Water lies on both sides of the high road. Lan’s son tests the board against the stone edge, and his mother tells him to wait until everyone has a place to stand.

## ev.ev_unique_paukin.choice_a

Spend a day helping Lan free the loaded cart

## ev.ev_unique_paukin.choice_a_result

You spend a strenuous day helping lift and move the loaded cart. Your arms ache afterward, but Lan reaches the market and tells the gate workers who helped her. Her son stores the plank where it can be reached next time.

## ev.ev_unique_paukin.choice_b

Pay the guide 1.5 silver to mark the road toward Tiju

## ev.ev_unique_paukin.choice_b_result

You pay 1.5 silver. After the cart is free, the guide marks the raised road and its next turn toward Tiju. He shows you which stones grow slick after rain; Lan’s son listens because he will drive that way again.
