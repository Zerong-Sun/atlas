## ev.ev_unique_paukin.title

The Stone Causeway

## ev.ev_unique_paukin.body

Water spreads on both sides of the stone causeway into Paukin. A cart has stopped where a wheel slips against the stones. Its driver asks for help with the heavy lift; a guide offers to walk the raised road with you.

## ev.ev_unique_paukin.choice_a

Help lift the cart from the stones

## ev.ev_unique_paukin.choice_a_result

You spend a tiring day freeing the wheel. Your strength falls, but the driver tells the gate workers who helped him.

## ev.ev_unique_paukin.choice_b

Pay the guide to walk the causeway

## ev.ev_unique_paukin.choice_b_result

You pay 150 silver. The guide shows where the stones hold firm and marks the onward road toward Tiju.
