## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.title

Dry Silk at the Wharf

## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.body

At Cacanfu’s canal quay, a broker finds damp on a bale of gauze. He needs help separating it from dry rolls. Beside him, a merchant offers a sound bolt of silk at a fixed price.

## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.choice_a

Help keep the gauze dry

## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.choice_a_result

You spend a day moving the gauze above the dry rolls. The broker praises your care, and you learn how merchants judge a shipment.

## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.choice_b

Buy the sound silk bolt

## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.choice_b_result

You pay 9,000 silver and take one bolt of silk. It cannot be sold back at this same quay; its worth lies farther along the road.
