## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.title

The Damp Silk Roll

## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.body

A broker at the canal landing has found that rain reached one roll of fine silk. His apprentice wants to put it back with the dry ones before the owner notices. ‘The damp will spread if we pack them together,’ the broker says. He asks her to undo the wrapper without pulling the threads. You hold the dry end while she lifts the wet edge onto a clean board. A merchant waiting to buy silk sees the work and asks whether any of the other rolls are sound. The apprentice brings a sealed one from the shelf and shows him the intact wrapper. He says he will buy it once he has checked its length. The broker gives his apprentice a second board and asks her to leave space between the rolls. She wants to know whether the damp one can be sold later; he says they must let it dry and inspect the colour first. You can spend a day helping with that work, or buy the verified dry roll from the merchant’s stock now.

## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.choice_a

Spend a day drying and checking the silk

## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.choice_a_result

You stay another day, lifting each section of damp silk onto clean boards while the apprentice checks the colour. The broker shows both of you how to spot water marks before a roll is packed. He thanks you in front of the buyers, and his apprentice lets you inspect the final list.

## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.choice_b

Buy a verified dry roll of silk

## ev.ev_unique_cacanfu.choice_b_result

The merchant measures a dry roll with you, then ties its wrapper shut. You pay for the full piece and take it with you. The broker keeps the damp silk separate until it can be checked again.
