## ev.maldive.mat.title

A Mat for the Returning Family

## ev.maldive.mat.body

In a courtyard near the island landing, two sisters are weaving a new sleeping mat. Their cousin is due to visit with his wife and child, and the older mat is too short for the three of them. The younger sister wants to finish before the boat arrives. She pulls a coconut leaf through the weave too hard and tears it. ‘We have another,’ the older one says, handing her a fresh strip. You ask whether the child is old enough to sleep on the mat. ‘Only when someone can convince him to leave his own grandmother’s arms,’ she replies. The sisters’ mother comes out carrying a folded cloth and reminds them the guesthouse has a dry room ready for their relatives. The younger sister asks whether you can hold the mat steady while she weaves the last edge. Her older sister says someone will also have to carry it to the guesthouse before their cousin sees it half finished. The boat’s sail is already visible beyond the palms. Their mother gives the cloth to her daughter and goes to the shore to greet the visiting family.

## ev.maldive.mat.choice.weave

Help finish the mat’s last edge

## ev.maldive.mat.result.weave

You hold the mat steady while the younger sister works coconut leaves through the final edge. Their mother tests the firm border and pays you for the help. When their cousin arrives with his family, he spreads it in the guesthouse and laughs: his son has already fallen asleep in the child’s grandmother’s arms.

## ev.maldive.mat.choice.carry

Carry the mat to the guesthouse

## ev.maldive.mat.result.carry

You and the older sister take the finished mat into the dry guest room. Their cousin arrives with his wife and child and recognizes the sisters’ familiar pattern. He thanks you in front of his aunt, who makes room for the child’s things beside the mat.
