## city.coigangiu.name

Coigangiu

## ev.coigangiu.entry.body

Huaiyin — a boat-town on the Grand Canal. Every family keeps a sampan; the children
learn to row before they learn to walk. The grain fleet passes through here in autumn on
its way to Cambaluc; the inns are full of boatmen, and the smell of river-fish frying
drifts across the water from dawn till midnight.

## ev.coigangiu.a.title

Coigangiu: The Boatwrights of the Canal Town

## ev.coigangiu.a.body

In the boat-town on the Grand Canal, the boatwrights of Coigangiu work the sampans under the willows, and every family keeps a boat as other families keep a door. A boatwright who has caulked hulls since boyhood says the grain fleet passes in autumn on its way to Cambaluc, and that a traveller who knows the canal can hire a sampan for the reach as another hires a horse for the road. He asks if you mean to buy timber's worth or water's worth.

## ev.coigangiu.a.choice.1

Buy a small sampan's worth of river salt for the road

## ev.coigangiu.a.choice.2

Ask the boatwright the reaches toward Paukin

## ev.coigangiu.a.choice.3

Help caulk a hull a day and learn the river

## ev.ev_coigangiu_a_followup.title

Coigangiu: The Boatwright's River Book

## ev.ev_coigangiu_a_followup.body

The boatwright sets down his adze and speaks of the river as of a person. He says the canal has moods — autumn fleets, spring shallows, the reach above Siju where the towpath fails — and that the boatmen keep a book of them passed from father to son. He offers you a page of it, if you will carry nothing away but the knowing.

## ev.ev_coigangiu_a_followup.choice_1

## ev.ev_coigangiu_a_followup.choice_1_result

## ev.ev_coigangiu_a_followup.choice_2

## ev.ev_coigangiu_a_followup.choice_2_result

## ev.ev_coigangiu_a_followup.choice_3_result
