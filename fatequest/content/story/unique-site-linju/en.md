## ev.ev_unique_linju.title

The Watcher’s Table

## ev.ev_unique_linju.body

At a table near Linju’s market gate, an older watchman named Peng is teaching his granddaughter to notice which carts have already passed. She writes each one down, then asks why a basket seller has appeared twice. Peng looks carefully and sees that the man came back to collect a forgotten rope. ‘So your list is right,’ he says. She wants him to admit that he doubted her. He does, and she smiles before adding the rope to the note.

You ask what the watchman does when the road is busy. Peng says he helps drivers make room and listens when someone is late. A hunter at the next table has brought cooked food from home to sell to travellers for 1.9 silver a portion. His younger brother packed it, but forgot spoons. Peng’s granddaughter offers two clean ones from her grandmother’s stall. The hunter promises to bring them back washed.

Peng sets a clean page beside your hand. The next cart comes into view, and his granddaughter asks whether you saw where it turned.

## ev.ev_unique_linju.choice_a

Spend a day learning to watch the market road

## ev.ev_unique_linju.choice_a_result

You spend a day helping Peng and his granddaughter keep the road clear. She catches two carts that have been counted twice and shows you the difference in their wheel marks. The gate people thank you, and your eye for the road grows sharper.

## ev.ev_unique_linju.choice_b

Buy the hunter’s hot meal for 1.9 silver

## ev.ev_unique_linju.choice_b_result

You pay 1.9 silver and eat the hot meal before moving on. The hunter returns the washed spoons to Peng’s granddaughter, who checks that her grandmother’s mark is still on both. You feel ready for the road.
