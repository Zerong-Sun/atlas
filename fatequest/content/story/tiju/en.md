## codex.cx-tiju.name

Tiju (Taizhou)

## codex.cx-tiju.body

The account places Tiju a day's ride southeast of Cayu (Gaoyou). Its vessels carry much trade; salt is made in the country toward the sea.

## ev.tiju.entry.body

At a salt yard outside Tiju, worker Min is measuring the drying beds while her brother brings lunch in a basket. He has put the rice bowl on top of the salt samples. ‘Now the lid tastes like the sea,’ he says after trying it. Min tells him to move the food to a clean board, then asks whether he brought enough for their aunt as well. He has. The aunt arrives carrying a sieve and makes the boy show her where he placed her bowl.

You ask which road reaches the town. Min points toward the houses and says carts carry salt inward while smaller traders take tools and food back out. Her aunt says the best way to learn the place is to watch the yard and the market for a day. The brother asks if that means you will stay for lunch. Min says she should first count the beds before anyone opens the basket.

A neighbour calls that one drying pan is ready to scrape. Min asks her brother to set aside the food and bring the clean sieve.

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.title

The Knife Grinder’s Stone

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.body

A knife grinder beside the salt yard is sharpening Min’s scraping blade. His daughter has poured too much water onto the stone and asks whether the edge is spoiled. He tells her to wipe the blade and look closely. The edge is still sound; the floor is simply wet. She fetches a rag and asks if the stone needs less water next time. ‘A little at a time,’ he says.

You ask whether he knows the land road to Sinju. The grinder’s sister carried blades to its market last month and is expected back by the afternoon. He can point out the first turn, but his daughter says the sister knows the road beyond it better. Min comes to collect her blade and asks whether it is ready for the next salt pan. The grinder tests it on a scrap of reed, then gives it to her handle first.

His daughter offers you the clean side of the rag to dry your hands. She has saved a small practice blade and wants to try one more careful pass across the stone.

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.choice.route

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.choice.route_result

The grinder’s sister returns and shows you the road toward Sinju, including the turn beyond the first market. His daughter follows each bend on your map while you mark it.

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.choice.scene_result

You hold the practice blade while the daughter adds water a little at a time. Her father checks the edge and nods. Min takes her own scraper back to the salt yard, thanking you for staying.

## ev.tiju.saltbasket.title

The Salt Sample Basket

## ev.tiju.saltbasket.body

Back at the salt yard, Min’s aunt is separating three small salt samples for buyers who will come after lunch. Min’s brother has written the same mark on two baskets. He says the salt looks identical. His aunt asks him to touch each sample: one is coarse, another is fine, and the third still has moisture in it. He rubs his fingers together and admits he can tell the difference now.

You ask why they keep the damp salt apart. Min says it must stay in the drying bed longer; a buyer taking it today would have to do that work later. Her aunt asks the boy to write the right marks and then tell the buyers honestly what is ready. A neighbour arrives carrying a board for the baskets, but it is too narrow for all three. The boy suggests setting only the dry two on it. Min nods, then asks whether he remembers to wash before lunch. He looks at his salt-covered hands and heads for the water jar.

The aunt asks who will carry the ready baskets inside. The boy volunteers after washing, and Min gives him a clean cloth for the handles.

## ev.tiju.saltbasket.choice.labels

Help the brother present the two dry samples

## ev.tiju.saltbasket.choice.day

Stay a day and check the damp sample as it dries

## ev.tiju.saltbasket.result.labels

You help the boy read the two corrected labels to the buyers. Min’s aunt confirms the coarse and fine samples, and the damp one stays in the yard. The buyers thank the family for telling them plainly.

## ev.tiju.saltbasket.result.day

You stay a day and check the third sample with Min as the sun moves. By evening its grains no longer cling to your fingers. Her brother writes a fresh label and proudly sets the basket on the board.
