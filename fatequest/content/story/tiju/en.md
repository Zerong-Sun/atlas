## codex.cx-tiju.name

Tiju (Taizhou)

## codex.cx-tiju.body

The account places Tiju a day's ride southeast of Cayu (Gaoyou). Its vessels carry much trade; salt is made in the country toward the sea.

## ev.tiju.entry.body

When you leave Cayu, you ride another day to the south-east through a constant
succession of villages and fields and fine farms until you come to TIJU, which is a city
of no great size but abounding in everything. The people are Idolaters (and so forth).
There is a great amount of trade, and they have many vessels. And you must know that on
your left hand, that is towards the east, and three days’ journey distant, is the Ocean
Sea. At every place between the sea and the city salt is made in great quantities.

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.title

A Whetstone at the Crossroads

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.body

Outside Tiju, a knife grinder pushes a blade along a stone, lifting its tip slightly so the edge does not curl. He asks whether you want to try or need the way to Sinju (Zhenzhou), where many travellers take the river route. Once the blade is sharp, he wipes away stone dust that could cause rust.

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.choice.route

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.choice.route_result

“Go southwest through the canal country toward Sinju (Zhenzhou). The road takes more than a day.”

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.choice.scene

Ask how to sharpen a knife

## ev.ev_gap_tiju.choice.scene_result

He gives you an old blade to practise with. Push lightly and listen: a harsh sound means too much pressure. Stop if the edge heats, or you may soften the steel.
