## ev.aden.entry.body

You must know that in the province of ADEN there is a Prince who is called the Soldan.
The people are all Saracens and adorers of Mahommet, and have a great hatred of
Christians. There are many towns and villages in the country. This Aden is the port to
which many of the ships of India come with their cargoes; and from this haven the
merchants carry the goods a distance of seven days further in small vessels. At the end
of those seven days they land the goods and load them on camels, and so carry them a
land journey of 30 days. This brings them to the river of ALEXANDRIA, and by it they
descend to the latter city. It is by this way through Aden that the Saracens of
Alexandria receive all their stores of pepper and other spicery; and there is no other
route equally good and convenient by which these goods could reach that place.
