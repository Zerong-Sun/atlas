## city.cobinan.name

Cobinan

## ev.cobinan.entry.title

At the Mirror Workshop

## ev.cobinan.entry.body

At a Cobinan metalworker’s shop, an apprentice holds a polished steel mirror so her father can inspect the surface. His reflection wavers where she has not finished working, and he sends her back to the bench with a smile.

Across the lane, another workshop tends a furnace for mineral work. The workers keep the pale deposit caught above the furnace separate from the residue below; traders call the first tutiya and the second spodium. The apprentice points you toward a place to rest and asks whether you have seen a polished steel mirror before.

The apprentice asks her father whether she should start again on the uneven patch. ‘Just that patch,’ he says. ‘Turn it toward the doorway and look at the line of light.’ She does, and the uneven patch is easier to see in the shifting light. You ask what the workers across the lane sell from their furnace. The father names the two materials they keep apart and adds that traders come looking for them as well as for mirrors. His daughter asks you to come back when she has finished polishing; she wants another pair of eyes on her work.

## ev.gap.cobinan.scene.title

The Dyer’s Blue Cloth

## ev.gap.cobinan.scene.body

A dyer named Mina lifts a length of blue cloth from her vat. Her sister ordered it for a new room and wants the colour even across the whole wall. Mina hangs the cloth where it will dry, then asks you to look at it from the far end of the lane.

‘From here every patch looks good,’ she says. ‘Tell me what you see from there.’ Her sister will arrive soon. Mina knows a caravan that goes toward Camadi and can tell you where it leaves if you ask.

You take a few steps back and ask where her sister plans to hang it. ‘Across the room from a window,’ Mina says. ‘She wants colour there even when the sun moves.’ An apprentice offers to turn the cloth for you. Mina tells him to leave it still so you can judge the same side she saw. He asks whether one pale strip matters. ‘She will notice it on the first morning,’ Mina answers. ‘She has been waiting for this cloth since the room was finished.’

## ev.gap.cobinan.scene.result.talk

## ev.gap.cobinan.scene.result.route

Mina tells you which yard the caravan uses and where the road turns toward Camadi. You mark the route and town on your map while the cloth dries.

## ev.cobinan.mirrorcase.title

The Mirror for His Sister

## ev.cobinan.mirrorcase.body

At the metalworker’s shop, the apprentice’s father has finished a small mirror for his sister. She is opening a stall and wants it to hang beside the door. He has made a wooden case to protect it on the journey across town, but he cannot leave the forge before dusk.

He offers to pay you for taking it over and helping her hang it, a job that will take the day. If you are short of time, he can instead show you how the mirror’s face was finished while the case is still open.

His apprentice asks whether the case needs more padding. ‘Enough to keep the mirror from knocking against the lid,’ he says, pressing the cloth down around it. You ask why his sister wants a mirror beside a market door. ‘She sells ribbons,’ he replies. ‘People like to see how they look before buying.’ The apprentice suggests carrying it herself, but her father reminds her of the unfinished work at the bench. She asks you to tell her aunt who polished the mirror if you take it over.
