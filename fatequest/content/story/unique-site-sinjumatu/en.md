## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.title

The Two Loading Crews

## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.body

At Sinjumatu’s river landing, two boat crews are waiting for the same stretch of quay. One has sacks of grain already on a cart; the other has pottery that must not be left in the rain. Their foreman, Old Song, asks each crew when its boat can actually come alongside. The grain crew points to a sail in the distance. The pottery crew’s vessel is still tied behind another boat.

You ask why they cannot simply take turns. Song says they can, but they need to settle the order before the sky darkens. A young worker from the pottery crew says his aunt made the bowls and will blame him for a chipped rim. The grain carter says his family wants the sacks under cover tonight. Song asks both to count how long loading will take. When they give honest estimates, the argument becomes smaller.

A cart driver from the road toward Coigangiu has a written land-route sheet for sale at 2 silver. He waits while Song asks you whether you can help the crews agree on a loading order.

## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.choice_a

Spend a day helping the crews agree on loading order

## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.choice_a_result

You spend a day hearing both crews and matching their estimates to the arriving boats. The pottery goes under cover first, then the grain cart backs in. Song thanks you before both crews, and you leave knowing them better.

## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.choice_b

Buy the driver’s land-route sheet for 2 silver

## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.choice_b_result

You pay 2 silver for the sheet. The driver points out the road toward Coigangiu and the cart stop where he checked it last. Song adds one note about when the river landing is busiest, and you record the route.
