## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.title

Where the River Divides

## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.body

At Sinjumatu a southern river is divided for the boats that carry trade onward. A foreman asks you to spend a day settling an argument between crews over the loading order. A pilot sells his channel marks.

## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.choice_a

Settle the loading order with both crews

## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.choice_a_result

You spend a day hearing both crews and agree on turns at the quay. The dock workers trust you, and you grow better at dealing with strangers.

## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.choice_b

Pay for the pilot’s channel marks

## ev.ev_unique_sinjumatu.choice_b_result

You pay 200 silver. The pilot shows the branch toward Coigangiu and how the current changes at the split.
