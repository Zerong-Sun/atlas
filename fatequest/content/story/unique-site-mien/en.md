## ev.ev_unique_mien.title

The Two Royal Towers

## ev.ev_unique_mien.body

After the long road into Mien, you find the towers raised for former kings. A keeper will recount how they came to be built. A porter offers to lead you back through the difficult approaches for a fee.

## ev.ev_unique_mien.choice_a

Stay and hear the keeper’s account

## ev.ev_unique_mien.choice_a_result

You spend a day hearing the story and copying what you can verify. Your care with other people’s accounts grows.

## ev.ev_unique_mien.choice_b

Hire the porter for the return approach

## ev.ev_unique_mien.choice_b_result

You pay 200 silver. The porter points out the safer way toward Sindafu and saves you from guessing at the mountain forks.
