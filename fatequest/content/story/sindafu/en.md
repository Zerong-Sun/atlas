## city.sindafu.name

Sindafu

## ev.sindafu.entry.body

At a tea shop in Sindafu, a porter is trying to fit a new table through the door. Shopkeeper Yu says he ordered it for two people; the carpenter made it wide enough for six. ‘You said the whole family would come,’ the carpenter replies. Yu says he meant the family would take turns watching the shop. The porter sets the table down and asks them to decide before he lifts it again.

You ask whether travellers can enter by this street. Yu points toward the market and offers you tea. His mother arrives from the back room, looks at the table, and says it can go under the awning for guests waiting out rain. The carpenter measures the space and finds it will fit if one stool moves. Yu asks his mother whether she has a stool to spare. ‘The one nobody sits on because it rocks,’ she says.

The porter waits while the family tests the new arrangement. Yu invites you to spend a day learning the neighbourhood from the people who pass his shop.

Spend a day at Yu’s tea shop

## ev.sindafu.entry.title

The Tea Seller’s Spare Table

## ev.ev_sindafu_entry.choice_1_result

You pass the tea shop and enter Sindafu. Behind you, Yu’s mother moves the rocking stool and the porter lifts the wide table under the awning.

## ev.ev_sindafu_entry.choice_2_result

You stay a day at the shop. Yu’s mother tells you who uses the street in the morning and evening; the carpenter notices the rocking stool and asks to take it apart later. You record what you learned of the neighbourhood.

## ev.ev_gap_sindafu.title

The Tea Shop’s Bamboo Tallies

## ev.ev_gap_sindafu.body

Yu’s helper has put coloured bamboo tallies beside the new table. One colour means tea, another means hot water, but a child has mixed the piles while playing. ‘I can put them back,’ the child says. Yu asks which cup was refilled twice. The child cannot remember, so the helper reads back what she saw and lets him sort the tallies from that.

You ask why they use the little sticks. Yu says the tea shop grows crowded when carts stop on the way to Cachanfu, and the tallies help him remember who has already paid. A driver at the wide table hears you mention the road. He knows the long land route toward Cachanfu and offers to mark the first junction after he finishes his tea.

The child finds one blue tally under the table. Yu’s helper asks him to check whether it belongs to the driver or the woman by the door. He brings it to her instead of guessing, and she thanks him.

‘Which table was mine?’ the driver asks. The child points to the repaired stool, then checks the tallies before he answers. Yu gives him a nod.

## ev.ev_gap_sindafu.choice.route_result

The driver marks the first junction and the later water stops toward Cachanfu. You record the route, and Yu’s helper hands back the tallies that belong to his table.

## ev.ev_gap_sindafu.choice.scene

## ev.ev_gap_sindafu.choice.scene_result

You ask each guest which cups were refilled and sort the tallies with the child. Yu closes the account without guessing, and the helper thanks you for giving the boy time to learn.

## ev.sindafu.stoolleg.title

The Rocking Stool

## ev.sindafu.stoolleg.body

Beside Yu’s tea shop, the carpenter has taken apart the stool that used to rock. Its shortest leg was once repaired with a scrap of wood, but the scrap has worn down. Yu’s mother says she kept the stool because her husband made it when their son first opened the shop. The carpenter asks whether she wants the old scrap saved. ‘If it can still show him how he worked,’ she says. Yu tells her he remembers his father measuring that leg twice and laughing at himself for cutting it short anyway.

You ask if a new piece will change the stool’s height. The carpenter sets four thin strips on the ground and tests each one. Yu’s helper brings a cup of tea, but the carpenter asks her to keep it away from the dry glue. The child who mixed the tallies offers to hold the strips in order. He asks Yu’s mother which was her husband’s work. She picks up the worn scrap and tells him what happened on the day the shop opened.

## ev.sindafu.stoolleg.choice.test

Help test the repaired stool with Yu’s family

## ev.sindafu.stoolleg.choice.day

Stay a day learning the carpenter’s repair

## ev.sindafu.stoolleg.result.test

You help the carpenter fit the best strip and test the stool on the uneven floor. Yu’s mother sits down; it no longer rocks. She puts the worn old scrap in a small box, and Yu brings tea for everyone.

## ev.sindafu.stoolleg.result.day

You stay a day cutting and testing wooden strips with the carpenter. The child keeps them in order, and Yu’s mother tells you more about the first day of the shop. By evening the stool is steady enough for guests under the awning.
