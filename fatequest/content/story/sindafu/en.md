## city.sindafu.name

Sindafu

## ev.sindafu.entry.body

When you have travelled those 20 days westward through the mountains, as I have told
you, then you arrive at a plain belonging to a province called Sindafu, which still is
on the confines of Manzi, and the capital city of which is (also) called SINDAFU. This
city was in former days a rich and noble one, and the Kings who reigned there were very
great and wealthy. It is a good twenty miles in compass, but it is divided in the way
that I shall tell you.

## ev.sindafu.a.title

Sindafu: The Three Parts of the Old King's City

## ev.sindafu.a.body

In the rich and noble city that is a good twenty miles in compass, the people still speak of the old king who, drawing near to death and leaving three sons, commanded that the city be divided into three parts, one for each son. A bridge-keeper who tends the crossings between the parts says each quarter keeps its own trade and its own pride, and that a traveller who honours all three is welcome in all three.

## ev.sindafu.a.choice.1

Buy silk at the bridge-market price

## ev.sindafu.a.choice.2

Ask the bridge-keeper the road toward Kenjanfu

## ev.sindafu.a.choice.3

Walk the three parts a day and note their trades

## ev.ev_sindafu_a_followup.title

Sindafu: The Bridge-Keeper's Tale of the Division

## ev.ev_sindafu_a_followup.body

The bridge-keeper tells the division as the city tells it: the old king, seeing death near, called his three sons and gave each a third of the city, that none might say he loved one above another. He says the parts have traded and quarrelled since, and that the bridges are where the three learn to agree — and that a traveller who knows the tale can cross any bridge in Sindafu without a toll of talk.

## ev.ev_sindafu_a_followup.choice_1

## ev.ev_sindafu_a_followup.choice_1_result

## ev.ev_sindafu_a_followup.choice_2

## ev.ev_sindafu_a_followup.choice_2_result
