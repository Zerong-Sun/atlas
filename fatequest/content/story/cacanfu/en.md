## city.cacanfu.name

Cacanfu

## ev.cacanfu.entry.body

Cacanfu is a noble city. The people are Idolaters and burn their dead; they have
paper-money, and live by trade and handicrafts. For they have plenty of silk from which
they weave stuffs of silk and gold, and sendals in large quantities. [There are also
certain Christians at this place, who have a church.] And the city is at the head of an
important territory containing numerous towns and villages.

## ev.cacanfu.entry.title

Concerning the Cities of Cacanfu and of Changlu

## ev.ev_cacanfu_entry.choice_1_result

You pass through without stopping. You set the way to Cacanfu on your map.

## ev.ev_cacanfu_entry.choice_2_result

You rest and ask what men say of this place. The road to Cacanfu opens on your map. The account is written down before the hour turns. It costs you 1 day on the road.

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a.choice_1_result

He weighs the bolt for you at the loom's fixed price. The stuff will hold its worth in Cambaluc, and the account of Cacanfu goes with it.

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a.choice_2_result

He reads the ferries and the tolls from his ledger: Chinangli and Linju below, Cambaluc above. The water road north is on your map.

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a.choice_3_result

You spend a day among the looms and the ferries. The river's measure is noted, and a small turn of fortune favours you.

## ev.cacanfu.a.title

Cacanfu: The River Loom and the Ferry Ledger

## ev.cacanfu.a.body

At the head of a great river that carries merchandise to Cambaluc, the weavers of Cacanfu work silk and gold into stuffs on the quay-side looms. A ferry clerk who has counted every boat for twenty years sits over his ledger and says the city's wealth is in the thread and the water both — buy from the loom without hearing the river, and you will pay the price of a man who knows neither.

## ev.cacanfu.a.choice.1

Buy a bolt of silk-and-gold at the loom price

## ev.cacanfu.a.choice.2

Ask the ferry clerk the water road toward Cambaluc

## ev.cacanfu.a.choice.3

Watch the looms a day and note the river's measure

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a_followup.title

Cacanfu: The Clerk's Second Page

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a_followup.body

The ferry clerk turns back a page of his ledger and lays it before you. He says the river remembers every toll between this city and Cambaluc, and that the stuffs woven here are carried down to Chinangli and Linju, and up to the great city where the Kaan's court pays in paper. He asks whether you mean to trade in thread or in water.

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a_followup.choice_1

Buy salt packed for the canal road

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a_followup.choice_1_result

He sells you salt at a price the canal boats accept. The salt of Changlu is packed and ready for the water.

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a_followup.choice_2

Ask him to name the days to Cambaluc and Chandu

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a_followup.choice_2_result

Cambaluc and Chandu are named with the days between. The two great cities are no longer only names.

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a_followup.choice_3

Sit a while over the ledger and hear the river's tolls

## ev.ev_cacanfu_a_followup.choice_3_result

You sit a while over the ledger. The tolls and the river's moods are noted, and in Cacanfu your name now carries a little more weight.
