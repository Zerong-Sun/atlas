## city.cacanfu.name

Cacanfu

## ev.cacanfu.entry.body

Boats laden with silk crowd the wharf at Cacanfu. A broker spreads a waterway map on his counter and points to the side channels. “These waterways join the route to Cambaluc. Send the silk north by boat; near the capital, other merchants can take it onward without loading it all into carts.” A weaver nearby works gold thread into silk and laughs: “Light gauze hates damp. Keep it above the dry silk rolls when you load the boat.”

## ev.ev_gap_cacanfu.title

The Scale Beside the Canal

## ev.ev_gap_cacanfu.body

At the river wharf of Cacanfu, a merchant disputes the weight of a sack of beans. The weighman asks whether to weigh the sack with the beans or subtract the weight of the empty bag. Each batch of sacks differs, he says; weighing one first will settle the argument before the boat leaves.

## ev.ev_gap_cacanfu.choice.route

Ask the way to Linju

## ev.ev_gap_cacanfu.choice.route_result

“Go southeast from here and follow the river to Linju.”

## ev.ev_gap_cacanfu.choice.scene

## ev.ev_gap_cacanfu.choice.scene_result
