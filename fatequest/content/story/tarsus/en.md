## ev.tarsus.entry.body

Tarsus — the birthplace of the Apostle Paul, now a town of the Armenian kingdom. The
Cydnus River still flows through the city, cool from the Taurus snows even in summer.
Cotton grows in the plain, and the caravans to the Cilician Gates stock up here before
the mountain crossing. The road east climbs three thousand feet in a single day.
