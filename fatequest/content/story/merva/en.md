## city.merva.name

Merva

## ev.merva.entry.body

A shallow pool lies where one of Merv’s old canals has filled with silt. Ducks walk over the low bank. At the roadside lodging, Hamid is replacing the thatch above a room his sister uses for travellers. She calls up to him that the soup is getting cold. ‘The roof can have its turn after lunch,’ she says. He lowers the bundle of reeds but stays on the ladder to show you where last night’s rain came through.

You ask whether the canal still serves anyone. Hamid’s sister points to a narrower channel behind the lodging. ‘We use that one. This old bed holds water only after a hard rain.’ Their neighbour arrives with a shovel and says his mulberry trees have had too little water this week. Hamid promises to help clear the small channel once the roof is safe. The neighbour looks at the soup and asks whether there is room for him at the table.

Hamid’s sister brings out another bowl. She has a mat ready if you stay the day; the neighbour is leaving soon and can show you the road past the working wells.

## ev.merva.entry.choice.pass

Go on after asking about the working wells

## ev.merva.entry.choice.rest

Rest a day and share their meal

## ev.merva.entry.title

The Roof Beside the Canal

## ev.ev_merva_entry.choice_1_result

The neighbour shows you which wells are still kept clear. You thank Hamid’s sister for the directions and mark Merv on your map before taking the road.

## ev.ev_merva_entry.choice_2_result

You stay a day, eat with the household and sleep beneath the newly patched roof. Hamid and the neighbour clear the narrow channel in the morning. By the time you leave, you feel rested and know which wells to follow.

## ev.merva.site_gap.title

The Copyist’s Drying Pages

## ev.merva.site_gap.body

Nuri, a copyist, is drying fresh sheets beneath a mulberry tree. Her young cousin holds the corners with thin strips of wood. He lifts one strip too soon and a page curls toward the dirt. ‘That is the letter for my father,’ he says, trying to catch it. Nuri catches the clean edge and lays it flat again.

You ask why the letter needs such care. Nuri says her uncle works on the road toward Sapurgan and reads every line his son sends, even the accounts of their donkey. The boy has written that the animal stole half a melon. ‘It only took the rind,’ he protests when Nuri reads it back. She smiles and tells him to correct the page once it is dry.

Nuri tests the corner with the back of her hand. She has spare paper for you to try a line, and the boy knows which stopping places his father uses when he travels east. He watches the page and waits for the wind to settle.

## ev.merva.site_gap.choice.route

Ask the boy about his father’s road to Sapurgan

## ev.ev_merva_site_gap.choice_1_result

The boy names the wells and stopping places his father uses toward Sapurgan. Nuri checks two names with him, then lets you copy them. You mark the route while the letter finishes drying.

## ev.merva.site_gap.choice.interact

Try writing on Nuri’s spare sheet

## ev.ev_merva_site_gap.choice_2_result

Nuri grinds ink for you. You let the line dry before touching the page; her cousin copies your careful hand on the back of his draft. You learn how their paper is prepared, and he finally corrects the story of the melon rind.

## ev.merva.waterturn.title

The Water Turn

## ev.merva.waterturn.body

At a small working canal, Zahra has placed two pebbles beside the sluice to remember whose turn comes next. Her brother insists the pebble nearest the water is his. She asks him to read the names she scratched on the board above it. He leans close, then laughs: he had been looking at the board upside down.

Their neighbour brings a cracked wooden shutter to replace the one in the channel. You ask why Zahra is waiting. ‘If we put it in before the afternoon turn, his vegetables lose their water,’ she says, nodding toward her brother. He says he can help fit the board after his field has its share. Zahra promises to bring supper if they finish before dark.

The new shutter is ready, but its lower edge needs to be held steady while the brother fastens it. Their mother, coming from the fields, asks whether everyone has eaten. Zahra lifts the empty water jug so she can see the answer for herself.

## ev.merva.waterturn.choice.board

Pay 1 silver for the replacement board

## ev.merva.waterturn.result.board

You put 1 silver toward the board. Zahra writes down the payment before her brother fixes the shutter, then brings supper as promised. The neighbours thank you when the water reaches both fields in turn.

## ev.merva.waterturn.choice.wait

Stay until tomorrow’s water turn and learn the schedule

## ev.merva.waterturn.result.wait

You stay until the next day’s water turn and help Zahra read the worn names on the board. Her brother shows you how far the water runs before he closes the gate. By evening you understand the timing well enough to plan another canal crossing.
