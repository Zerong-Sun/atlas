## city.mecha.name

Mecha

## ev.mecha.entry.body

Near Mecha, a family at a roadside shelter is sorting water skins for people arriving from different directions. A woman writes each owner's name on a wooden tag. Her brother points to two skins with the same mark and asks which belongs to an older traveller waiting in the shade. She asks him to feel the seams. “He patched his own with red thread yesterday,” she says. The brother finds the thread and carries the skin over. You ask which way travellers go if they continue north toward Medina. The woman says her cousin made that journey recently and will return to the shelter by evening. A group of pilgrims pauses nearby to adjust their clothing before entering the city; the woman gives them space and lets them take water in turn. Her brother brings you a cup and asks whether you are leaving today. If you can stay, he says, his cousin will show you the road on a board after supper.

## ev.mecha.entry.title

Names on the Water Skins

## ev.ev_mecha_entry.choice_1_result

The woman points out the road through the town and tells you which shelter to ask at farther on. Her brother returns the patched skin to its owner before you leave. You continue with the turns through Mecha clear in mind.

## ev.ev_mecha_entry.choice_2_result

You spend a day at the shelter. In the evening the cousin arrives, names Medina and traces the northward route on a board, including the first place to replenish water. The older traveller thanks the family for finding his skin. You leave knowing both the town and the way toward Medina.

## ev.mecha.site_gap.title

The Potter’s Water Jars

## ev.mecha.site_gap.body

A potter beside the water shelter has set two clay jars in the shade. One is thick and heavy, the other light enough for his daughter to lift alone. She wants to send the lighter one with a traveller going north. “He has a cart,” she says. Her father taps the rim and asks her how she will keep it from knocking against his other goods. She fetches a rolled cloth. You ask whether the thick jar would be safer. “Perhaps,” he says, “but he'll have to lift it at every water stop.” The daughter fits the cloth around the lighter jar and shows you that its mouth is wide enough to clean. A traveller arrives to collect it, asking which road to take toward Medina. She points to the same northern route used by people at the shelter and asks him to bring back word of how the jar travels. He promises to do so if it reaches the next stop intact. The potter reminds his daughter to keep the spare cloth for the return journey.

## ev.mecha.site_gap.choice.route

Ask about the road to Medina

## ev.ev_mecha_site_gap.choice_1_result

The daughter points out the northern road toward Medina and names the first water stop on it. The traveller repeats her directions before loading the jar. You follow the turn on the ground, then thank her for showing you.

## ev.mecha.site_gap.choice.interact

Help pack the lighter jar

## ev.ev_mecha_site_gap.choice_2_result

You hold the cart's side rail while she cushions the light jar with cloth. The potter shows you the difference between its thinner wall and the heavy jar left in shade. The traveller gives the packed jar a gentle push; it stays in place, and the daughter smiles.

## ev.mecha.cloak.title

A Coat with Two Names

## ev.mecha.cloak.body

At the roadside shelter, a seamstress has an old coat spread across her knees. She promised to mend it for a traveller who left before dawn, but two different names have been stitched inside the collar. Her son guesses the coat was passed from one brother to another. “It was,” she says, “but I need to know which one is coming back for it.” A woman filling a water skin recognizes the older brother's neat letters and says the younger one now wears the coat. The seamstress asks her son to add a fresh patch beneath the collar, leaving the old names in place. “He will want to remember who carried it first.” The boy asks you to hold the cloth while he makes the first stitch. He wants to catch the traveller before the northbound group leaves; his mother says they can wait at the shelter's gate and ask each person by name. The boy asks whether the younger brother will know his new stitches; his mother says the uneven first one will give him away.

## ev.mecha.cloak.choice.find

Help find the coat’s current owner

## ev.mecha.cloak.result.find

You wait with the boy at the gate and ask the northbound travellers by name. The younger brother recognizes the coat and shows you an old patch on its sleeve. He thanks you for bringing it back, and the people at the shelter remember your help.

## ev.mecha.cloak.choice.stitch

Stay a day and help mend the collar

## ev.mecha.cloak.result.stitch

You stay for a day while the boy practises his stitches and his mother repairs the collar. The younger brother comes back for the coat before the group leaves. He reads both names and tells the boy who wore it first; you have time to talk over a shared meal before moving on.
