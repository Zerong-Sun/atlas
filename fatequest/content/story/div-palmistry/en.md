## div.palmistry.result.00

The life line runs long: many leagues await; do not hurry them.

## div.palmistry.result.01

The life line breaks: a rest is needed; stop at the next river.

## div.palmistry.result.02

The life line curves wide: a wide journey; far cities call.

## div.palmistry.result.03

The life line runs close: a cautious road; keep to the beaten track.

## div.palmistry.result.04

The head line is straight: a direct mind; the plan is clear.

## div.palmistry.result.05

The head line slopes: a dreaming mind; guard the purse.

## div.palmistry.result.06

The head line is deep: a firm decision; the road bends to it.

## div.palmistry.result.07

The head line is faint: an uncertain mind; ask twice.

## div.palmistry.result.08

The heart line rises: an open heart; friendship comes easily.

## div.palmistry.result.09

The heart line descends: a guarded heart; keep counsel.

## div.palmistry.result.10

The heart line is long: loyalty; the companion is true.

## div.palmistry.result.11

The heart line is short: a quick affection; a quick parting.

## div.palmistry.result.12

The fate line is strong: a destined road; follow it without doubt.

## div.palmistry.result.13

The fate line is broken: a change of course; the new way is better.

## div.palmistry.result.14

The fate line begins at the wrist: a road from humble start to high end.

## div.palmistry.result.15

The fate line begins at the palm: a road chosen freely; take it.

## div.palmistry.result.16

The sun line shines: fame at the next city; accept the honor.

## div.palmistry.result.17

The sun line is absent: a quiet journey; wealth unspoken.

## div.palmistry.result.18

The mount of the thumb is full: strength; the heavy load is yours to carry.

## div.palmistry.result.19

The mount of the forefinger is full: ambition; aim at the chief's seat.

## div.palmistry.result.20

The mount of the middle finger is full: caution; count the coin twice.

## div.palmistry.result.21

The mount of the ring finger is full: art; a craft will pay the way.

## div.palmistry.result.22

The mount of the little finger is full: speech; a word well placed opens gates.

## div.palmistry.result.23

The bracelets are deep: long years and a long road.

## div.palmistry.result.24

A star on the mount of the moon: a sea voyage; a crossing is blessed.

## div.palmistry.result.25

A cross on the mount of Jupiter: a marriage or a meeting of import.

## div.palmistry.result.26

A triangle on the head line: a clever bargain; sign it.

## div.palmistry.result.27

A square on the life line: protection; a shield follows you.

## div.palmistry.result.28

Many fine lines: a busy road; many small dealings.

## div.palmistry.result.29

The heart and head lines meet: a single will; your choice is the right one.
