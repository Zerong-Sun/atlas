## city.medina.name

Medina

## ev.medina.entry.body

Medina — City of the Prophet — lies three days from al-Safrā’ upon the
pilgrim road. Mount Uhud stands three miles north; upon its slopes is the mosque
of Hamzah and the tombs of the martyrs. Eastward, by Bāb al-Baqī‘, lies the
cemetery of al-Gharqad — tombs of Safiyyah, of Mālik ibn Anas under a modest
cupola, of Ibrāhīm son of the Prophet under a white one. Men come to acquire
blessings at these graves. You must know that after Mecca this is the second
city of the Faith, and that no pilgrim from Irāk or Syria passes without visiting
the sacred tomb.

## ev.medina.a.title

Medina: The Palm Gardens and the Watering of the Hosts

## ev.medina.a.body

At the venerated city where the pilgrims come to renew their provision of water, the garden masters of Medina tend the palm gardens and the wells that serve the hosts. A master who keeps the water accounts says the road from the coast runs three days to this place, and that the city measures its water as carefully as its dates — a traveller who draws without leave is a burden, one who asks is a guest. He asks which you mean to be.

## ev.medina.a.choice.1

## ev.medina.a.choice.2

Ask the garden master the roads toward Petra

## ev.medina.a.choice.3

Walk the gardens a day and learn the water's keeping

## ev.ev_medina_a_followup.title

Medina: The Garden Master's Word on the Wells

## ev.ev_medina_a_followup.body

The garden master takes you to the well that serves the pilgrim road and speaks of water as the city's true coin. He says the gardens and the wells were the reason the road was made, and that the city renews the provision of every host that passes — the water is drawn, the dates are counted, the blessing is spoken. He asks what you will draw from this city's measure.

## ev.ev_medina_a_followup.choice_1

Buy dates at the pilgrim measure, sealed for the road

## ev.ev_medina_a_followup.choice_1_result

He sells you dates at the gardener's own measure, sealed in leaf. The fruit will keep to the next oasis.

## ev.ev_medina_a_followup.choice_2

## ev.ev_medina_a_followup.choice_2_result

## ev.ev_medina_a_followup.choice_3

## ev.ev_medina_a_followup.choice_3_result
