## city.medina.name

Medina

## ev.medina.entry.body

Medina — City of the Prophet — lies three days from al-Safrā’ upon the
pilgrim road. Mount Uhud stands three miles north; upon its slopes is the mosque
of Hamzah and the tombs of the martyrs. Eastward, by Bāb al-Baqī‘, lies the
cemetery of al-Gharqad — tombs of Safiyyah, of Mālik ibn Anas under a modest
cupola, of Ibrāhīm son of the Prophet under a white one. Men come to acquire
blessings at these graves. You must know that after Mecca this is the second
city of the Faith, and that no pilgrim from Irāk or Syria passes without visiting
the sacred tomb.

## ev.medina.site_gap.title

Drinking Water at the Inn

## ev.medina.site_gap.body

Outside an inn stands a shared water jar. The keeper turns a copper ladle upside down on its rim. Rinse it before use and turn it over afterwards, he says, so dust does not settle inside. Travellers can drink here. He lifts the lid to show clean water and leaves the ladle handle pointing out for the next person.

## ev.medina.site_gap.choice.route

Ask about the road to Petra

## ev.ev_medina_site_gap.choice_1_result

“Leave through the north gate and follow this road north to Petra. There are resting places along the way; check your waterskins before evening.”

## ev.medina.site_gap.choice.interact

Bring another bucket of water

## ev.ev_medina_site_gap.choice_2_result

You carry well water to the jar. The keeper shows you how to skim floating leaves first. Keep the jar in shade and leave a small gap beneath the lid, he says, so the water does not grow stale.
