## div.geomancy.result.00

The figure opens the road if you keep to the caravan hours.

## div.geomancy.result.01

Great fortune: the figure opens a side gate; ask the porter for the merchants' entrance.

## div.geomancy.result.02

The figure opens trade in salt and hides; leave silks for another city.

## div.geomancy.result.03

The figure opens a morning audience; do not wait until the heat.

## div.geomancy.result.04

The figure opens the desert road only with two spare water-skins.

## div.geomancy.result.05

The figure opens learning the local prayer times before you bargain.

## div.geomancy.result.06

The figure opens hiring a watchman for your packs at the inn.

## div.geomancy.result.07

The figure opens a short stay; leave before the weekly tax count.

## div.geomancy.result.08

The figure opens asking the qadi's clerk which roads are under ban.

## div.geomancy.result.09

The figure opens sharing a fire with the next company, not camping alone.

## div.geomancy.result.10

The figure holds you one day: mend harness before you go.

## div.geomancy.result.11

The figure holds counsel: write the distances between wells tonight.

## div.geomancy.result.12

The figure holds: do not sell until you have heard three prices.

## div.geomancy.result.13

The figure holds: wait for the dust storm to pass the ridge.

## div.geomancy.result.14

The figure holds: keep your letters sealed until the next city.

## div.geomancy.result.15

Great ill omen: the figure stops the night march; daylight only on this band.

## div.geomancy.result.16

The figure stops a bargain with a man who will not name his city.

## div.geomancy.result.17

The figure stops crossing the river at this ford; go upstream.

## div.geomancy.result.18

The figure stops trusting maps drawn in another season.

## div.geomancy.result.19

The figure stops paying guides who demand half in advance.

## div.geomancy.result.20

The figure stops entering the warehouse quarter after dark.

## div.geomancy.result.21

The figure stops arguing tolls; pay and keep moving.

## div.geomancy.result.22

The figure stops dividing your company on this plain.

## div.geomancy.result.23

The figure stops opening wine before the next shrine.

## div.geomancy.result.24

The figure stops chasing rumour of a shorter road east.

## div.geomancy.result.25

The figure warns: the next well may be guarded; approach with gifts.

## div.geomancy.result.26

The figure warns: leave heavy cargo and send a light scout first.

## div.geomancy.result.27

The figure warns: do not sleep in the ruined caravanserai.

## div.geomancy.result.28

The figure warns: change your planned day of departure by one.

## div.geomancy.result.29

The figure warns: ask again after you have seen the sky clear.

## div.geomancy.name

Geomancy
