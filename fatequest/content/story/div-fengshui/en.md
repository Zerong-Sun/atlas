## div.fengshui.result.00

The mountain stands behind the house: the road behind is safe; return when needed.

## div.fengshui.result.01

A stream before the door: wealth flows toward you; buy at the waterside.

## div.fengshui.result.02

The dragon line runs true: a prosperous route; the land itself favors you.

## div.fengshui.result.03

A broken dragon line: a hazard ahead; mend your cart before the gorge.

## div.fengshui.result.04

A house with a straight road to its door: fortune rushes past; stand aside.

## div.fengshui.result.05

A well in the courtyard: a source of refreshment; the inn is honest.

## div.fengshui.result.06

A tall tree at the gate: shelter and shade; rest here.

## div.fengshui.result.07

A dead tree at the gate: a warning; this place holds no fortune.

## div.fengshui.result.08

The water turns at the bend: the fortune turns with it; settle there.

## div.fengshui.result.09

A road that curves like a bow: a blessed dwelling; bargain well.

## div.fengshui.result.10

A road that pierces like an arrow: ill fortune aims at the door; do not stay.

## div.fengshui.result.11

A garden in the south: warmth and growth; plant your plan here.

## div.fengshui.result.12

A wall in the north: protection from the cold; winter travel is guarded.

## div.fengshui.result.13

An open east: the morning light blesses the departure.

## div.fengshui.result.14

A cluttered west: the evening burden; travel light.

## div.fengshui.result.15

A pond that stills: patience is repaid; wait a day.

## div.fengshui.result.16

A waterfall: loud fortune; a sudden gain, soon spent.

## div.fengshui.result.17

A bridge at the gate: a crossing gift; pay the toll gladly.

## div.fengshui.result.18

A shrine at the crossroads: the gods of the road watch; offer a coin.

## div.fengshui.result.19

A bell tower to the east: the morning bell calls you to rise early.

## div.fengshui.result.20

A smithy to the south: sparks and strength; mend your iron here.

## div.fengshui.result.21

A mill by the water: the wheel turns; your fortune grinds slowly fine.

## div.fengshui.result.22

A graveyard on the hill: the ancestors watch; do no dishonor.

## div.fengshui.result.23

A market at the crossing of roads: trade flows; sell before noon.

## div.fengshui.result.24

A stable in the east: the horse is ready; a fast start.

## div.fengshui.result.25

A garden wall that crumbles: a falling fortune; repair it or leave.

## div.fengshui.result.26

A roof that leaks at the corner: a small loss; fix it cheaply now.

## div.fengshui.result.27

A door facing the sunset: the day closes early; plan by morning.

## div.fengshui.result.28

A window facing the mountain: a clear view; the path is known.

## div.fengshui.result.29

The land breathes even: balance; the journey holds neither great risk nor great gain.
