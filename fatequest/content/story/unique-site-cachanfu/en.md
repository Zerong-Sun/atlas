## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.title

Ginger on the Great River

## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.body

The Caramoran is too broad for a bridge here. Ginger sacks wait on the bank while a ferryman measures the current. He will take a paying traveller first; the merchants need another hand to load their sacks.

## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.choice_a

Load the ginger sacks before crossing

## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.choice_a_result

You spend a day helping with the ginger and earn 150 silver. The merchants remember your help.

## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.choice_b

Pay for the first ferry across

## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.choice_b_result

You pay 190 silver. The ferryman sets off before the cargo boats and points out the crossing toward Sindafu.
