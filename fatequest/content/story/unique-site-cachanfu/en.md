## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.title

Ginger Bags at the Ferry

## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.body

A ginger seller has brought his bags to the ferry, but the boat is not ready to load. His younger cousin wants to stack them directly on the damp bank. ‘Put the boards down first,’ the seller says. He has promised the buyer that the ginger will arrive dry. The cousin asks why one bag is lighter than the rest. A seam has opened and a few roots have fallen out. You help collect them while the seller finds a needle. The ferryman calls that a crossing is possible now, but only people and light parcels can go before the heavier cargo. The seller asks the cousin to decide whether to wait and mend every bag or pay to put the smaller parcel on the early ferry. The cousin says she will mend first, because the buyer will count the roots on arrival. You can spend the next day helping the family load the full shipment and earn a share of the work, or pay the ferryman for a place in the early boat and ask him to show you the route toward Sindafu.

## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.choice_a

Spend a day loading the ginger shipment

## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.choice_a_result

You stay another day and help the seller move the repaired bags onto dry boards before they go aboard. The cousin checks each seam. Once the shipment is counted, the seller pays you for the work and tells the ferryman who helped him keep his promise.

## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.choice_b

Pay for an early crossing and directions

## ev.ev_unique_cachanfu.choice_b_result

You pay the ferryman for a place on the early boat. As he takes the craft across, he points out the road toward Sindafu and names the next place to ask for water. The cousin waves from the bank, still sewing the last ginger bag.
