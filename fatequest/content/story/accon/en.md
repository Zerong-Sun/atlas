## city.accon.name

Acre

## ev.accon.entry.body

Beside the sea at Acre, Safa offers water to travellers from beneath a piece of shade cloth. She has set out two low stools. Her younger brother Yusuf comes running back with a basket. The flatbreads inside have gone cold. The breeze lifts a corner of the cloth above the old harbour steps.

“Did you give it to Uncle?” she asks.

Yusuf stops. “I took the wrong one. This is for the netmenders by the shore. Uncle's basket is still on the cart.”

Their uncle is carrying a family letter to Jerusalem. Safa put the letter under the bread she packed for him. The driver stopped beside the road a little while ago and went to collect one last sack. She looks at the sky, tells Yusuf to drink some water, and starts down the road with him.

“Have you seen a cart with yellow cloth over it?” Yusuf asks you. “There was a coil of rope tied on top.” Safa takes the cold bread from him. “I'll help you look. If Uncle has gone on, we can follow the road for a while.”

## ev.accon.entry.choice.look

Help Safa find the basket and rest by the shore overnight

## ev.accon.entry.choice.pass

Ask which road leaves Acre and continue travelling

## ev.accon.entry.title

Acre

## ev.ev_accon_entry.choice_1_result

You ask Safa where the main road begins. She sets down the water jug and points to an earthen track beside the old harbour steps. “Follow it to the fork and ask whoever is travelling there.” Yusuf has already run off with the basket. You follow her directions and mark Acre on your map.

## ev.ev_accon_entry.choice_2_result

You walk along the road with Safa and Yusuf. The driver is still loading a sack of beans. Their uncle is crouched beside him, holding a rope taut. Yusuf hands him the basket and lifts the bread. The letter is still underneath. Their uncle tucks it into his clothes and asks Yusuf to tell his grandmother that he'll have someone write back from Jerusalem.

You spend the night at a resting place by the shore. The next morning their uncle meets you beside the road and points out the direction his party will take to Jerusalem. You add the city and road to your map.

## ev.accon.netmaker.title

The Netmaker's Two Needles

## ev.accon.netmaker.body

By the old harbour, Marya spreads a wet net over the stone steps. Her son Ilya squats beside her with a bone needle. He cannot make it pass through the row of small holes. A small cloth shade stands beside them, with a basket of needles and thread underneath.

“Your grandfather used that one,” Marya says. “The point is worn blunt, so it catches in the small holes. Try my new needle.”

Ilya shakes his head. His grandfather promised that once he learned to mend a net, he could take one to the shore himself. His grandfather is resting in a shelter nearby today, but Ilya still wants to finish his first net with the old needle.

Marya does not hurry him. She sits beside him and slowly opens a knot along the edge. Ilya holds out the bone needle for you to see. Its point has gone blunt. “You've been to other places,” he says. “Have you seen anyone sharpen one of these and use it again?”

## ev.accon.netmaker.choice.help

Stay and help Ilya mend this part of the net

## ev.accon.netmaker.choice.visit

Go with Ilya to his grandfather's shelter and ask about the old needle

## ev.accon.netmaker.result.help

You hold the net flat while Ilya picks up a thread with the old needle. When the point catches, Marya hands him a small sharpening stone. He works the point for a moment and tries again. By the last hole, he has found the movement for himself.

Marya checks the net and smiles. “Grandfather will like this. Show him your work tomorrow.” Ilya puts the old needle back in its cloth bag and, for the first time, picks up the new one as well. Marya thanks you, and the other netmenders nearby remember your help.

## ev.accon.netmaker.result.visit

You walk along the shore with Ilya and find the shelter where his grandfather is resting. He is sitting in the shade repairing a basket. When he sees the old needle, he laughs. “What are you doing with that? It's more stubborn than I am.”

Ilya spreads the unfinished section of net on a board. His grandfather sharpens the point lightly against a stone and asks him to try again. Ilya gets it through the hole at last. His grandfather puts the net away and asks him to show his mother tomorrow. You help them sort the net lines beneath the shelter until dark. His grandfather shares a hot meal with you and finds you a straw mat. You leave rested the next morning.
