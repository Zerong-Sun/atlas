## div.dream.result.00

A gate left open: the road ahead stands ready; set out at first light.

## div.dream.result.01

Water rising at the threshold: a crossing is near, but keep your purse laced.

## div.dream.result.02

A flock of cranes overhead: good company will join your wagon before noon.

## div.dream.result.03

Eating bread alone: the next town offers hospitality if you ask at the well.

## div.dream.result.04

A key found in grass: something lost returns, perhaps your direction.

## div.dream.result.05

A ship with no sails: delay on this route; the word you wait for has not come.

## div.dream.result.06

Climbing a tower: knowledge will raise you — seek the scribe at the shrine.

## div.dream.result.07

A door that will not open: the way is barred until you pay the toll or earn the pass.

## div.dream.result.08

A burning cart: danger on the western road; sell the goods before you leave.

## div.dream.result.09

Teeth falling out: an elder's counsel will be hard to hear but true.

## div.dream.result.10

A dead tree blooming: a worn-out venture revives; take it up once more.

## div.dream.result.11

Crossing a river in a dream: your wish to travel is sound, but wait one market day.

## div.dream.result.12

A wedding feast: an alliance is offered; agree, but name your terms.

## div.dream.result.13

A road rising into cloud: the mountain pass is uncertain; hire a guide.

## div.dream.result.14

A child laughing: a light heart pays better than gold on the open road.

## div.dream.result.15

A purse full of sand: what seems wealth is not; count your silver twice.

## div.dream.result.16

A hare racing the wind: hurry, but not so fast you lose your cart.

## div.dream.result.17

An empty inn: rest where you find shelter, not where you planned.

## div.dream.result.18

A bell without a clapper: your question has no answer yet; ask again at the next city.

## div.dream.result.19

A lantern in rain: a small light will see you through the dark marsh.

## div.dream.result.20

A horse standing in water: this route will test you; keep the reins short.

## div.dream.result.21

A stranger handing you a coin: help comes from an unexpected quarter; take it.

## div.dream.result.22

A broken bridge: change course; the ford is three days south.

## div.dream.result.23

A field ready for harvest: your cargo will sell well at the next gate.

## div.dream.result.24

A shadow following you: trouble stalks your trail; travel with a companion.

## div.dream.result.25

A window thrown open: news arrives that changes your route — welcome it.

## div.dream.result.26

A snake in the road: do not force this argument; let the dispute settle.

## div.dream.result.27

A roof mended with gold: spend on what shelters you; it will be repaid.

## div.dream.result.28

A journey in the sky: your ambition is grand; shorten the first leg.

## div.dream.result.29

Waking from a dream of departure: the sign favors moving, yet keep your plans to yourself.
