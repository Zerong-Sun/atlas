## div.meihua.result.00

One plum blossom falls: a single loss marks the beginning; continue.

## div.meihua.result.01

Two blossoms: a pairing serves the journey; travel with another.

## div.meihua.result.02

Three blossoms: three days of rain; plan for wet roads.

## div.meihua.result.03

Four blossoms: four gates; the south gate opens for you.

## div.meihua.result.04

Five blossoms: five coins find their way into your purse.

## div.meihua.result.05

Six blossoms: six leagues a day; an easy pace.

## div.meihua.result.06

Seven blossoms: seven days of waiting at the pass.

## div.meihua.result.07

Eight blossoms: eight directions; only one is yours — the east.

## div.meihua.result.08

A blossom on the water: what you set afloat returns to you.

## div.meihua.result.09

A blossom in the dust: an idea takes root in plain soil.

## div.meihua.result.10

The number of the hour: act when the bell strikes; not before.

## div.meihua.result.11

The number of the day: the market favors the third day's trading.

## div.meihua.result.12

The number of your age: a mature counsel is best; heed the elder.

## div.meihua.result.13

The number of the road: count your baggage; one thing too many.

## div.meihua.result.14

A blossom caught in a web: a plan entangled; free it slowly.

## div.meihua.result.15

A blossom on the threshold: a guest arrives who changes your plans.

## div.meihua.result.16

A blossom turning in the wind: direction uncertain; ask twice.

## div.meihua.result.17

A blossom at the door of the shrine: a vow is heard; keep your promise.

## div.meihua.result.18

A blossom in the morning dew: an early start brings fortune.

## div.meihua.result.19

A blossom fallen at noon: a mid-course change is wise.

## div.meihua.result.20

A blossom at dusk: an evening errand should wait for daylight.

## div.meihua.result.21

A blossom among the reeds: a hidden path reveals itself.

## div.meihua.result.22

A blossom on the cart wheel: the journey turns upon a small choice.

## div.meihua.result.23

A blossom beside the fire: warmth and kinship; seek the hearth.

## div.meihua.result.24

A blossom carried by the bird: news from afar alights with you.

## div.meihua.result.25

A blossom in the rain: tears and relief; the storm passes.

## div.meihua.result.26

A blossom on the altar: a promise made in haste; bind it well.

## div.meihua.result.27

A blossom under the moon: a secret journey is guarded.

## div.meihua.result.28

A blossom on the ledger: a debt is recorded; settle it.

## div.meihua.result.29

A blossom in the closing hand: the cast closes; accept the answer.
